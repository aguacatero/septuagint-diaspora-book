## Legacy assumptions superseded by the constitution

1. **Binary “LXX vs. MT” framing** is superseded by a pluriform textual model that can classify Old Greek, other Jewish Greek forms/revisions, Hebrew textual witnesses, authorial adaptation, mixed cases, or indeterminate cases.
2. **Authorial Hebrew incapacity** is not a project premise. The project tests demonstrable compositional use of Hebrew Scripture author-by-author.
3. **“Mistranslation”** is not a default category. It must be demonstrated after textual analysis.
4. **Mythic/Hellenistic parallels** are not excluded from the book, but they are analyzed only after scriptural analysis using explicit comparative controls.
5. **Historicity** is not permanently bracketed. It is bracketed during textual/literary analysis and reopened in the final HIS audit.
6. **Repetition across dependent Gospels** is not counted automatically as independent attestation.
7. **John** is part of the primary Gospel corpus.
8. **Acts** may inform Luke–Acts authorial practice but is not automatically independent historical attestation for Luke's Jesus traditions.
9. **Allusions** are tracked separately from explicit/identifiable quotations and cannot inflate quotation statistics.
10. **Argumentative chapters are downstream of the registry.** Legacy prose must not be used to pre-classify evidence.

## Migration rule

Do not silently correct old files merely to make them look consistent with the new method. Preserve them as historical artifacts, add supersession notices where necessary, and migrate individual claims into `research/registry/` with explicit status, classification, counterevidence, and confidence.

Known legacy claims needing reanalysis are tracked in `research/registry/corrections_log.md`.
