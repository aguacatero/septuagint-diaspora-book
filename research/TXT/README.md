# TXT — Textual Tradition Module

TXT asks which textual tradition best explains a New Testament author's wording in a defined evidence unit.

## Allowed classification family

Use the ordered categories defined in `textual_classifications.md`. Classification is separate from confidence.

## Required questions

1. What is the exact NT wording in the critical edition used by the project?
2. What relevant Old Greek wording is attested?
3. What other Jewish Greek forms or revisions are relevant?
4. What Hebrew textual witnesses are relevant, including DSS where available?
5. Does the NT wording require direct Hebrew consultation, or can Greek transmission, authorial adaptation, oral/liturgical transmission, or an unknown intermediary explain it?
6. What is the strongest counterexample to the proposed classification?
7. What would falsify or materially weaken the classification?

## Negative evidence

TXT must preserve counterexamples and indeterminate cases. A Hebrew-aligned reading is not a failure of the project; it is a result that must be explained without moving the goalposts.

See `hebrew_consultation_test.md` for the direct-Hebrew threshold.
