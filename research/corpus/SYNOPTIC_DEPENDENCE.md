# Synoptic Dependence Rules

Markan priority is the project's **operational baseline**, not a premise that must be true for the book's conclusions to survive.

## Rules

1. Q is not used as evidence in the project.
2. Matthew and Luke are not counted automatically as independent witnesses where literary dependence on Mark or another shared tradition is plausible.
3. Source relationships are evaluated passage-by-passage when they affect TXT, NAR, or HIS conclusions.
4. Major conclusions should be checked for robustness under reasonable alternative relationships between Matthew and Luke.
5. John is not granted blanket independence or dependence; individual traditions are assessed claim-by-claim.

The purpose of source criticism here is evidentiary bookkeeping: one tradition copied into several books must not be transformed into several independent attestations merely by canonical repetition.
