# Primary Corpus

The primary corpus for the project is:

- Mark
- Matthew
- Luke–Acts
- John
- undisputed Pauline letters

## Use rules

- Each Gospel receives its own TXT audit rather than being treated as one homogeneous linguistic population.
- Luke–Acts may be analyzed as one authorial corpus for language, scriptural practice, and authorial habits where warranted.
- Acts is not automatically independent historical attestation for material narrated in Luke.
- The undisputed Pauline letters are analyzed before the Gospels in the book architecture because they are the earliest surviving Christian authorial evidence in the primary corpus.
- Repetition across dependent texts does not create independent attestation by itself.
- Control corpora are comparative tools and do not silently enter primary-corpus statistics.
