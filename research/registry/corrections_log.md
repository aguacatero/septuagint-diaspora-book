# Legacy Corrections / Reanalysis Log

This file records claims from legacy project materials that require reanalysis under the new protocol. Entries remain visible rather than being silently rewritten.

## LEG-001 — Pliny language overclaim

**Legacy formulation:** Pliny is used as evidence that Christians were speaking or singing in Greek.

**Status:** `UNVERIFIED_LEGACY`

**Required action:** Re-check the primary text and modern scholarship. Separate what Pliny actually reports from inferences about the language used by the Christians he describes. Do not use the passage as direct evidence of Greek liturgical language unless the primary text supports that conclusion.

## LEG-002 — John 19:37 / Zechariah 12:10

**Legacy formulation:** John 19:37 is treated as straightforward LXX-aligned evidence.

**Status:** `UNVERIFIED_LEGACY`

**Required action:** Treat this as a counterexample test in TXT. Compare John, Old Greek Zechariah, relevant Hebrew witnesses, and other Greek revisions/traditions. Determine whether the reading favors another Greek tradition, Hebrew-direct use, inherited quotation form, authorial adaptation, or remains indeterminate.

## LEG-003 — Mark 1:2–3 “single-Isaiah LXX editorial decision”

**Legacy formulation:** Mark's combined citation is described as mirroring an LXX editorial decision and used as direct proof of LXX dependence.

**Status:** `UNVERIFIED_LEGACY`

**Required action:** Re-collate the exact wording of Mark 1:2–3 against Isaiah 40:3, Malachi 3:1, Exodus-related wording where relevant, Old Greek witnesses, Hebrew witnesses, and proposed composite-citation practices. Do not describe the phenomenon as an LXX editorial decision unless the textual witnesses establish that claim.

## Rule

A correction entry is not itself a replacement conclusion. Each case must receive a stable evidence ID and pass through the appropriate module before the manuscript states a new result.
