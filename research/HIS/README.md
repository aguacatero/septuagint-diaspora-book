# HIS — Historical Residue Module

HIS reopens the historical-Jesus question only after textual, narrative, and cultural analysis.

The audit begins from **zero assumed biography**. Each proposition must independently earn evidentiary standing.

## Evaluation dimensions

Each historical proposition should be evaluated for:

- chronology;
- textual integrity;
- genuine independence;
- proximity to the claimed events;
- dependence on Christian proclamation;
- identifiable source relationship;
- explanatory alternatives;
- effects of prior TXT/NAR/CUL findings.

## Independence rule

Independence is claim-specific, not source-wide. A source may be independent for one proposition and dependent or uncertain for another.

Repeated material across literarily dependent Gospels does not automatically create multiple independent attestations.

## Permitted outcomes

The final synthesis may support:

- a recoverable historical individual with some surviving biography;
- an extremely minimal individual surrounded by extensive literary construction;
- genuinely indeterminate evidence;
- a literary/nonhistorical-origin model.

No outcome is protected in advance.
