## 1. B001-2: Romans 9:25 / Hosea 2:23 [LXX 2:25]

### S05-E001 — Gentile Calling Grounded in Prophetic Adoption
- **Passage Target:** Romans 9:25 / Hosea 2:23 [LXX 2:25]
- **Claim Type:** Exegetical / Literary
- **Printed Page:** Page 130 (also pp. 128–129)
- **Source Locator:** Teil II.2.2a, S. 130
- **NotebookLM Citation:** `wilk_1998_jesaja_paulus.pdf p. 130`
- **Author's Position:** In Romans 9:25, Paul quotes Hosea 2:25 LXX (`καλέσω τὸν οὐ λαόν μου λαόν μου καὶ τὴν οὐκ ἠγαπημένην ἠγαπημένην`), applying the prophetic promise of divine favor directly to Gentile believers. Wilk shows that Paul uses the future verb `καλέσω` to create an explicit verbal link with the preceding aorist `ἐκάλεσεν` in Rom 9:24. By promising to the Gentiles the very covenant blessings that originally belonged to Israel, the citation scripturally validates Paul's gospel of Gentile inclusion.
- **Supporting Quotation:**
  > „Indem es Heiden diejenigen 'Auszeichnungen' verheißend zuspricht, 'die Israel besaß und besitzt', erweist es die Berechtigung der paulinischen Darstellung...“ (p. 130)
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Citation of Hosea 2:25aβ.bα.1b LXX; verbal link between `καλέσω` (v. 25) and `ἐκάλεσεν` (v. 24).
- **Qualifications:** Although Hosea historically addressed the northern kingdom of Israel, Paul interprets the designation `οὐ λαός μου` as referring directly to Gentile nations.
- **Confidence:** HIGH

## 2. B001-3: Romans 9:26 / Hosea 1:10 [LXX 2:1]

### S05-E002 — Divine Sonship Granted in the Sphere of Gentile Alienation
- **Passage Target:** Romans 9:26 / Hosea 1:10 [LXX 2:1]
- **Claim Type:** Exegetical
- **Printed Page:** Page 129 (also pp. 128, 131, 185–188)
- **Source Locator:** Teil II.2.2a, S. 129
- **NotebookLM Citation:** `wilk_1998_jesaja_paulus.pdf p. 129`
- **Author's Position:** Paul combines Hosea 2:1 LXX with Hosea 2:25 LXX in Romans 9:25–26 to establish that Gentiles receive the status of sons of the living God (`υἱοὶ θεοῦ ζῶντος`) in the very geographic and spiritual realm (`ἐν τῷ τόπῳ... ἐκεῖ`) where they were formerly alienated from the covenant.
- **Supporting Quotation:**
  > „In der Tat kann der Apostel die Zitatkombination ihrem Wortlaut zufolge kaum anders denn als Rede über Heiden verstanden haben...“ (p. 129)
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Concatenation of Hosea 2:1 LXX and 2:25 LXX; catchword connection via `κληθήσονται` back to `ἐκάλεσεν` (Rom 9:24).
- **Qualifications:** Paul assumes his readers recognize the designation "not my people" as an intuitive and self-evident description of the Gentile condition prior to the gospel.
- **Confidence:** HIGH

## 3. B001-4: Romans 10:11 / Isaiah 28:16 (and Romans 9:33)

### S05-E003 — Textual Harmonization and Universalizing Addition of `πᾶς`
- **Passage Target:** Romans 10:11 / Isaiah 28:16
- **Claim Type:** Textual-Critical / Exegetical
- **Printed Page:** Page 45 (also pp. 31–34, 47, 162)
- **Source Locator:** Teil II.1.2.1.3a, S. 45; Teil II.1.1.2.2a, S. 31–34
- **NotebookLM Citation:** `wilk_1998_jesaja_paulus.pdf p. 45`
- **Author's Position:** In Romans 10:11, Paul modifies Isaiah 28:16bβ LXX: he substitutes the indicative future `οὐ καταισχυνθήσεται` for the Septuagint's subjunctive `οὐ μὴ καταισχυνθῇ` to harmonize the citation with the future-tense verbs of the immediate context (Rom 10:9, 13), and he prefixes `πᾶς` to establish the universal scope of salvation for both Jews and Greeks.
- **Supporting Quotation:**
  > „Die rein stilistische Modifikation — Paulus schreibt 'οὐ καταισχυνθήσεται' statt 'οὐ μὴ καταισχυνθῇ' — dürfte in dem Bestreben begründet sein, das Zitat in das von Futura durchzogene Umfeld einzupassen.“ (p. 45)
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Comparison of LXX Isa 28:16bβ with Rom 10:11; addition of `πᾶς`; indicative future verb form.
- **Qualifications:** The prefixing of `πᾶς` is Paul's deliberate theological addition, absent from Greek and Hebrew witnesses of Isaiah 28:16 as well as from Paul's earlier citation in Rom 9:33.
- **Confidence:** HIGH

### S05-E004 — Christological Identification of the Foundation Stone
- **Passage Target:** Romans 10:11 / Isaiah 28:16 (and Romans 9:33)
- **Claim Type:** Exegetical / Theological
- **Printed Page:** Page 164 (also pp. 162–166, 224)
- **Source Locator:** Teil II.3.1b, S. 164
- **NotebookLM Citation:** `wilk_1998_jesaja_paulus.pdf p. 164`
- **Author's Position:** Paul interprets the "stone in Zion" from Isaiah 28:16 christologically across both Romans 9:33 and 10:11. In Romans 9:33, he conflates it with Isaiah 8:14 to present a dual aspect: a stumbling stone for unbelieving Israel and vindication for believers. In Romans 10:11, he cites it independently with `πᾶς` to articulate an unmixed, positive promise of righteousness for all who place their trust in Christ.
- **Supporting Quotation:**
  > „So begreift er beide Jesajaworte als Hinweise auf Christus, der als eine, in sich identische Person nach der einen Seite Juden und Heiden Gerechtigkeit verleiht...“ (p. 164)
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Composite quotation of Isa 28:16 and 8:14 in Rom 9:33; independent citation of Isa 28:16bβ in Rom 10:11.
- **Qualifications:** Refined per self-audit: although God is the speaker in Isaiah 28:16, the stone (`λίθος`) is identified by Paul directly as Jesus Christ as proclaimed in the apostolic gospel.
- **Confidence:** HIGH

## 4. B001-5: Romans 10:13 / Joel 2:32 [LXX 3:5]

### S05-E005 — Transfer of the Divine Name to the Lord Jesus
- **Passage Target:** Romans 10:13 / Joel 2:32 [LXX 3:5]
- **Claim Type:** Exegetical / Literary
- **Printed Page:** Page 61 (also pp. 24, 47, 164–165, 224, 390)
- **Source Locator:** Teil II.2.1.1.1a, S. 61
- **NotebookLM Citation:** `wilk_1998_jesaja_paulus.pdf p. 61`
- **Author's Position:** In Romans 10:13, Paul quotes Joel 3:5a LXX verbatim (`πᾶς γὰρ ὃς ἂν ἐπικαλέσηται τὸ ὄνομα κυρίου σωθήσεται`). The citation is linked via the shared catchword `πᾶς` to Isaiah 28:16 in Rom 10:11, transferring the Old Testament formula for calling upon the name of the Lord God (YHWH) directly to the public confession of Jesus as Lord.
- **Supporting Quotation:**
  > „...demgegenüber unterstreicht die Anspielung auf Joel 35a die Verbindung von κύριος-Homologie und Rettung.“ (p. 61)
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Verbatim quotation of Joel 3:5a LXX in Rom 10:13; tripartite catchword connection via `πᾶς` linking Rom 10:11, 10:12, and 10:13.
- **Qualifications:** In the prophetic context of Joel, `κύριος` designated YHWH; in Paul's argument (Rom 10:9–13), `κύριος` refers unequivocally to the risen Christ.
- **Confidence:** HIGH

## 5. B001-6: Romans 11:34 / Isaiah 40:13

### S05-E006 — Textual Insertion of `ἤ` Matching Rom 11:33 Doxological Structure
- **Passage Target:** Romans 11:34 / Isaiah 40:13
- **Claim Type:** Textual-Critical / Literary
- **Printed Page:** Page 308 (also pp. 307–311, 358–359)
- **Source Locator:** Teil III.2.3.1b, S. 308
- **NotebookLM Citation:** `wilk_1998_jesaja_paulus.pdf p. 308`
- **Author's Position:** In Romans 11:34, Paul quotes Isaiah 40:13 LXX, inserting `ἤ` before `τίς σύμβουλος` to divide the citation into two distinct, balanced rhetorical questions matching the paired concepts of God's wisdom (`σοφία`) and knowledge (`γνῶσις`) in Romans 11:33.
- **Supporting Quotation:**
  > „Angesichts der schwachen Bezeugung von ἤ in der handschriftlichen Überlieferung von Jes 4013... dürfte das Wort auf Paulus zurückgehen...“ (p. 308)
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Textual insertion of `ἤ` in Rom 11:34 vs. `καί` in LXX Isa 40:13; correspondence to the dual exclamation in Rom 11:33.
- **Qualifications:** Because `ἤ` is almost completely unattested in the Septuagint manuscript tradition of Isaiah 40:13, it must be recognized as Paul's deliberate authorial modification.
- **Confidence:** HIGH

### S05-E007 — The Unsearchable Wisdom of Israel's Salvation
- **Passage Target:** Romans 11:34 / Isaiah 40:13
- **Claim Type:** Exegetical / Theological
- **Printed Page:** Page 394 (also pp. 307–311)
- **Source Locator:** Teil V.2.1.4, S. 394
- **NotebookLM Citation:** `wilk_1998_jesaja_paulus.pdf p. 394`
- **Author's Position:** Paul incorporates Isaiah 40:13 at the culmination of Romans 11 to celebrate that God's paradoxical and sovereign plan for Israel's salvation—involving temporary hardening alongside Gentile inclusion—completely transcends human understanding and counsel.
- **Supporting Quotation:**
  > „...bringt es den wunderbaren Charakter des — von Menschen nur demütig nach- und mitzuvollziehenden — Heilshandelns Gottes an Israel gebündelt zur Sprache.“ (p. 394)
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Integration of Isa 40:13 into the concluding hymn of Romans 11:33–36.
- **Qualifications:** The first clause of Rom 11:34 reflects upon God's merciful decree, while the second reflects upon His execution of that decree in history.
- **Confidence:** HIGH

## 6. B001-7: Romans 15:12 / Isaiah 11:10

### S05-E008 — Verbatim Old Greek Citation with Omission of Temporal Clause
- **Passage Target:** Romans 15:12 / Isaiah 11:10
- **Claim Type:** Textual-Critical / Exegetical
- **Printed Page:** Page 46 (also pp. 18–19, 238)
- **Source Locator:** Teil II.1.2.1.4a, S. 46; Teil II.1.1.1, S. 18–19
- **NotebookLM Citation:** `wilk_1998_jesaja_paulus.pdf p. 46`
- **Author's Position:** In Romans 15:12, Paul quotes Isaiah 11:10a LXX verbatim, deliberately omitting the introductory temporal phrase `ἐν τῇ ἡμέρᾳ ἐκείνῃ` because he regards the promised "Root of Jesse" as having already appeared in the historical person of Jesus Christ (Rom 15:8).
- **Supporting Quotation:**
  > „Diese Zeitangabe kann entfallen, da nach Meinung des Apostels — wie sie in Röm 158 zum Ausdruck kommt — die verheißene 'Wurzel Isais' mit Jesus Christus bereits erschienen ist.“ (p. 46)
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Omission of `ἐν τῇ ἡμέρᾳ ἐκείνῃ` in Rom 15:12 vs. LXX Isa 11:10a.
- **Qualifications:** Refined per self-audit: Wilk notes on p. 238 n. 28 that any hypothesis of a pre-Pauline Hebraizing revision in Paul's Vorlage remains under textual reservation due to the absence of direct manuscript evidence for Isaiah 11.
- **Confidence:** HIGH

### S05-E009 — Christ's Reign as the Climax of Mutual Jewish-Gentile Praise
- **Passage Target:** Romans 15:12 / Isaiah 11:10
- **Claim Type:** Exegetical / Theological
- **Printed Page:** Page 394 (also pp. 146–158)
- **Source Locator:** Teil V.2.1.4, S. 394
- **NotebookLM Citation:** `wilk_1998_jesaja_paulus.pdf p. 394`
- **Author's Position:** Paul places Isaiah 11:10 at the climax of his fourfold quotation chain in Romans 15:9–12 to establish Christ as the eschatological ruler of the Gentiles, thereby providing the theological foundation for the mutual acceptance and united worship of Jewish and Gentile believers in the church.
- **Supporting Quotation:**
  > „Sie erklärt Christus — wie ihn sein Evangelium bezeugt... zum Garanten der Gemeinschaft von Juden- und Heidenchristen...“ (p. 394)
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Progression of the fourfold scriptural chain in Rom 15:9–12 (Ps 17:50; Deut 32:43; Ps 116:1; Isa 11:10).
- **Qualifications:** Christ's universal lordship over the Gentiles is inseparably tied to His service to the circumcised (`διάκονον... περιτομῆς`) in Rom 15:8.
- **Confidence:** HIGH

## 7. B001-8: 1 Corinthians 1:19 / Isaiah 29:14

### S05-E010 — Deliberate Substitution of `ἀθετήσω` Influenced by Psalm 32:10 LXX
- **Passage Target:** 1 Corinthians 1:19 / Isaiah 29:14
- **Claim Type:** Textual-Critical / Exegetical
- **Printed Page:** Page 45 (also pp. 44, 18)
- **Source Locator:** Teil II.1.2.1.2, S. 44–45
- **NotebookLM Citation:** `wilk_1998_jesaja_paulus.pdf p. 45`
- **Author's Position:** In 1 Corinthians 1:19, Paul cites Isaiah 29:14b LXX, substituting `ἀθετήσω` ("I will set aside / nullify") for the Septuagint's `κρύψω` ("I will hide"). Wilk argues that this substitution was an intentional authorial clarification (`Verdeutlichung`), likely influenced by Psalm 32:10 LXX (`κύριος... ἀθετεῖ βουλὰς ἀρχόντων`), designed to emphasize that God actively invalidates worldly wisdom rather than merely concealing it.
- **Supporting Quotation:**
  > „Demgegenüber lässt das — vielleicht durch Ps 3210(𝔊) vermittelte — Wort ἀθετεῖν... den Sinn der jesajanischen Prophetie in ihrer paulinischen Deutung deutlicher zutage treten.“ (p. 45)
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Substitution of `ἀθετήσω` for `κρύψω` in 1 Cor 1:19 vs. LXX Isa 29:14b; lexical parallel in Ps 32:10 LXX.
- **Qualifications:** Wilk refutes the claim that `ἀθετήσω` was an accidental memory slip, proving from context that it accurately reflects Paul's theology of the Cross.
- **Confidence:** HIGH

### S05-E011 — Scriptural Warrant for the Destruction of Worldly Wisdom
- **Passage Target:** 1 Corinthians 1:19 / Isaiah 29:14
- **Claim Type:** Exegetical / Theological
- **Printed Page:** Page 393 (also pp. 101–105, 160–162)
- **Source Locator:** Teil V.2.1.3, S. 393
- **NotebookLM Citation:** `wilk_1998_jesaja_paulus.pdf p. 393`
- **Author's Position:** Paul uses Isaiah 29:14 in 1 Corinthians 1:19 as programmatic scriptural warrant (`γέγραπται γάρ`) demonstrating that God's saving act in the cross of Christ intentionally destroys human intellectual pride and self-reliance as avenues of divine knowledge.
- **Supporting Quotation:**
  > „Sie erlaubt es Paulus, das Gericht Gottes über die Weisheit jüdischer und hellenistischer Provenienz als notwendige Kehrseite des Heilsgeschehens in Christus darzustellen...“ (p. 393)
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Introductory formula `γέγραπται γάρ` in 1 Cor 1:19 grounding the discourse of 1 Cor 1:18–31.
- **Qualifications:** Human wisdom in this passage embraces both Jewish scribal/legal pride and Hellenistic philosophical speculation.
- **Confidence:** HIGH
