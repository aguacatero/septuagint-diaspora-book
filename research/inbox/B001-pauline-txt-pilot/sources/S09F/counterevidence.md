## 1. Boundary Separating Oliver's Secondary Claims from Primary Textual Data

Because Oliver (2015) conducts a **rhetorical-structural and theological analysis** of Romans 10:5–13 rather than an apparatus-level textual collation of MT vs. Old Greek/LXX variants, B001 must keep Oliver's actual claims strictly separated from project primary-text comparisons (`P01F` / `P03F`).

### A. Canonical Locus `B001-4`: Romans 10:11 / Isaiah 28:16

- **What Oliver Actually Claims:**
  - Oliver explicitly labels the citation in Rom 10:11 as `Is 28:16 (LXX)` (`Printed p. 3 [PDF p. 3]`; `Table 1, Printed p. 5 [PDF p. 5]`) and places it among Paul's prophetic citations (`Printed p. 4 [PDF p. 4]`).
  - He links `καταισχύνω` in Rom 10:11 back to `ἐπαισχύνομαι` in Rom 1:16 (`Printed p. 3 [PDF p. 3]`, citing `Jewett 2007:138` on the removal of human shame and `Matlock 2010:79, 80` on faith in Rom 1:16 and 10:9–10) and argues that vv. 11–13 use `πᾶς` (vv. 11, 13) and `κύριος πάντων` (v. 12) in a fourfold `γάρ` sequence to assert the universality of God's salvation (`Printed p. 7 [PDF p. 7]`, citing Bechtler 1994:306).
- **Direct-Locus Qualifications & Counter-Readings Inside Oliver:**
  - Oliver **does NOT** claim text-critically that Paul altered LXX Isaiah 28:16 by adding `πᾶς` or modifying the Vorlage.
  - **`S09F-E010` (`Printed p. 3 [PDF p. 3], fn. 5`):** Oliver cautions against over-reading universal faith-righteousness directly out of Isaiah 28:16 without textual discipline: referring to G. Aho (`1981:308`), he writes that stipulating from Isa 28:16 *"that ‘the way of righteousness by faith is meant for all’ ... could be a too subjective reading of the verse within this pericope."* (He also engages `Aho 1981:308` on `Printed p. 8 [PDF p. 8]` on heart-faith and mouth-confession as two sides of one coin.)
  - **Moo (`1996:657`) explicitly citing `Rm 9:30; 10:4, 11` (`Printed p. 8 [PDF p. 8]`):** Oliver acknowledges Moo's counter-position—derived directly in part from Rom 10:11 alongside 9:30 and 10:4—that Paul's rhetorical aim is not to make oral confession a second salvation requirement alongside faith, since faith in the heart remains the crucial requirement. While Oliver resists Moo's downplaying of `ὁμολογέω` inside vv. 9–10, Moo's citation of `Rm 10:11` stands as direct locus-relevant counterevidence inside Oliver's own debate.
  - **Authorial Background Assumption vs. Vorlage Evidence (`Printed p. 1 [PDF p. 1]`):** Following Lund (`1942:139`) and Fetler (`2005:231`), Oliver states as a background premise that Paul was educated in Greek and had Hebrew training. Because Oliver presents this strictly as an inherited biographical assumption (for rhetorical literacy) and performs zero Hebrew-vs-Greek textual comparison, B001 must **not** treat p. 1 as text-critical evidence that Paul consulted Hebrew MT manuscripts of Isa 28:16 or Joel 2:32.
  - **Counterevidence against over-reading `B001-4` in S09F:** Oliver offers **zero** discussion of the MT (`הַמַּאֲמִין לֹא יָחִישׁ`, "he who believes will not make haste") vs. LXX (`καὶ ὁ πιστεύων οὐ μὴ καταισχυνθῇ`) Vorlage divergence or LXX manuscript variants (`+ ἐπʼ αὐτῷ` in `א A Q`). Therefore S09F provides **Substantive** exegetical/structural evidence for `B001-4`, not primary text-critical collation.

> **Project Primary-Text Note (Non-Source-Attributed — `P01F-P004` / `P03F-P003`):**  
> Primary Greek collation in `B001` (`P01F-P004` `SBLGNT` Romans 10:11: `λέγει γὰρ ἡ γραφή· Πᾶς ὁ πιστεύων ἐπʼ αὐτῷ οὐ καταισχυνθήσεται.`; `P03F-P003` `Swete LXX` Isaiah 28:16: `...καὶ ὁ πιστεύων οὐ μὴ καταισχυνθῇ.` with apparatus `+ επ αυτω א A Q`) shows `Πᾶς` and `ἐπʼ αὐτῷ` present in Paul's form against Swete's B-text of Isa 28:16. This is a project-level primary-text observation from `P01F`/`P03F`, **not** a claim made by Oliver (2015).

### B. Canonical Locus `B001-5`: Romans 10:13 / Joel 2:32 (LXX Joel 3:5)

- **What Oliver Actually Claims (`S09F-E005`, `S09F-E006`, `S09F-E007`):**
  - Oliver classifies Rom 10:13 as a prophetic citation from Joel 2:32 (within Joel 2:28–32; `Printed p. 4 [PDF p. 4]`), noting that in **`Acts 2:17–21` the apostle Peter quotes Joel's words directly** (`Printed p. 4 [PDF p. 4], fn. 10`), while **Paul reuses these invocation words elsewhere only in 1 Corinthians 1:2** (`Printed p. 4 [PDF p. 4]`, describing those who call on the Name of Jesus).
  - He treats v. 13 as the climactic `γάρ` clause (`Printed p. 7 [PDF p. 7]`) and *"something ‘new’"* that binds oral calling (`ἐπικαλέομαι`, echoing v. 12) to `σωθήσεται` (echoing v. 9; `Printed p. 10 [PDF p. 10]`), pivoting into the apostolic missionary chain of Rom 10:14–15 (`Printed p. 5 [PDF p. 5]` and `pp. 10–11`).
- **Direct-Locus Counterevidence, Rejected Summary Readings, & Internal Tensions (`pp. 7–11`):**
  - **Rejection of calling as a summary of believing and confessing (`Printed p. 7 [PDF p. 7]`):** On p. 7, Oliver considers whether `ἐπικαλέω` in v. 13 merely summarizes `πιστεύω` and `ὁμολογέω` (vv. 9–10) and explicitly rejects it: *"There is no evidence in the context for this postulation."*
  - **Rejection of Hart (`1999`) & Hodges (`1989:198`) on v. 13 (`Printed p. 7 [PDF p. 7]`):** Oliver records Hart's and Hodges's Free Grace counter-reading that `σωτηρία` in v. 10b and `σωθήσεται` in v. 13 refer to already-justified believers calling on the Lord in public prayer (temporal/fellowship deliverance), then raises his own unresolved exegetical question: if v. 13 serves as the pericope's conclusion, why does v. 13 include `"confess"`/`calling` while omitting `"believe"` (`pp. 7, 9`)?
  - **Reversion from `ὁμολογέω` to `πιστεύσωσιν` in v. 14 (`Printed p. 10 [PDF p. 10]`):** Immediately after v. 13, Paul drops `ὁμολογέω` in v. 14 and *"reverts to what his Theology always professes: ‘Believe’"* (`ἐπικαλέσωνται -> πιστεύσωσιν -> ἀκούσωσιν -> κηρύσσοντος`), exposing the anomalous tension of vv. 9–13 within Paul's wider soteriology.
  - **Missing Text-Critical Precision:** Oliver does not discuss LXX versification (`Joel 3:5a`) versus Hebrew/English numbering (`Joel 2:32a`), nor does he examine Paul's omission of `καὶ ἔσται` or addition of postpositive `γάρ`.

> **Project Primary-Text Note (Non-Source-Attributed — `P01F-P005` / `P03F-P004`):**  
> Accepted primary comparison in `B001` between **`P01F-P005`** (`SBLGNT` Romans 10:13: `Πᾶς γὰρ ὃς ἂν ἐπικαλέσηται τὸ ὄνομα κυρίου σωθήσεται.`) and **`P03F-P004`** (`Swete LXX` Joel 2:32 [LXX 3:5]: `καὶ ἔσται πᾶς ὃς ἂν ἐπικαλέσηται τὸ ὄνομα Κυρίου σωθήσεται·`) shows Paul reproduces the relative-and-matrix clause verbatim while dropping `καὶ ἔσται` and inserting postpositive `γάρ` (separate non-Oliver MT witness in Joel 3:5a [Eng. 2:32a]: `כֹּ֧ל אֲשֶׁר־יִקְרָ֛א בְּשֵׁ֥ם יְהוָ֖ה יִמָּלֵ֑ט`). These textual collations belong to `P01F-P005` and `P03F-P004`, **not** to Oliver (2015).

### C. Contextual Pericope (`B001-CONTEXT`): Exegetical Alternatives, Structural Objections, & Self-Confessed Tensions (`pp. 7–11`)

Every alternative and counter-argument Oliver catalogs around the Rom 10:9–10 chiasm is preserved below in clean scholarly prose and labeled by scope:

1. **Consecutive Salvation Stages Alternative — *Contextual (`B001-CONTEXT`) with Future `σωθήσεται` Link to `B001-5`* (`Printed pp. 7–8 [PDF pp. 7–8]`):**
   - Oliver evaluates the view that v. 10 describes two consecutive stages: heart-belief brings present `δικαιοσύνη`, while mouth-confession is still required to obtain future `σωτηρία` (appealing to the future tense `σωθήσεται` in v. 13 and **`Rom 13:11`**: `νῦν γὰρ ἐγγύτερον ἡμῶν ἡ σωτηρία ἢ ὅτε ἐπιστεύσαμεν`).
   - Proponents cited by Oliver include **Hodge (`1947:341, 343`)**, **Sanday & Headlam (`1902:290`)**, **Barclay (`1975:139`)**, **Boice (`1993:1209`)**, and **Godet (`1956:383`)** (*"two conditions of salvation"*).
   - **Oliver's contextual rejection (`p. 8`):** *"The pericope, however, does not seem to support this possibility, as Paul is rather informing his listeners about what the consequences of their actions are."*

2. **Synonymous / Two-Aspects Alternative vs. Distinct-Actions Reading — *Contextual (`B001-CONTEXT`) with Direct `B001-4` Touchpoint via `Moo 1996:657` and `Aho 1981:308`* (`Printed p. 8 [PDF p. 8]`):**
   - Oliver examines commentators who collapse confession and belief into one reality or two inseparable aspects of one saving faith: **Hart (`1999`)**, **Ironside (`1928:131`)**, **Nygren (`1949:383`)**, **Moo (`1996:657` — explicitly invoking `Rm 9:30; 10:4, 11` for faith as the crucial requirement)**, **Aho (`1981:308`)**, **Kruse (`2012:410`)**, and—claiming chiastic parallelism between vv. 9 and 10—**Ito (`2006:250`)**, **Dunn (`1988b:609`)**, and **Johnson (`1974:151`)**.
   - **Oliver's rejection (`p. 8`):** Oliver insists the text of vv. 9–10 marks no semantic parallelism; Paul distinguishes `καρδία` (site of faith -> `δικαιοσύνη`) from `στόμα` (site of confession -> `σωτηρία`).

3. **Faith-Eventually-Produces-Confession Alternative — *Contextual (`B001-CONTEXT`)* (`Printed p. 8 [PDF p. 8]`):**
   - **Murray (`1968:55–57`)**, **Shedd (`1978:318–319`)**, and **Schreiner (`1998:609`)** argue that genuine heart-faith will eventually manifest as oral confession.
   - **Oliver's rejection (`p. 8`):** This is a dogmatic deduction from general Pauline theology rather than an induction from v. 10c, where confession is stated as the condition leading to salvation, not salvation's consequence (noting `Hodges 1989:197`).

4. **Unresolved Theological Tension & Argument-Level `Hapax Legomenon` (`Printed pp. 8–11 [PDF pp. 8–11]`):**
   - **`Hapax Legomenon` (`pp. 8–9, fn. 21`):** Oliver concedes that Paul has no true NT parallel for making `ἐὰν ὁμολογήσῃς` a condition of eternal salvation—only Jesus's own sayings in `Mt 10:32` / `Lk 12:8` (`Carson 1984:490`; `Hagner 1993:289`; `fn. 19` noting temporal-reward readings in `Moyer 1997:267`, `Dillow 1992:127, 384`, `Hodges 1985:28–33`). Hence vv. 9–10 act *"as a sort of a Hapax Legomenon"*—an argument Paul uses only once.
   - **Direct contradiction with Pauline norm (`p. 10`):** Assigning `σωτηρίαν` to `στόματι`-"something-being-said" rather than `καρδίᾳ`-faith is *"in total contrast to Paul’s whole ‘gospel’ and theology"*, underscored by v. 14 dropping `ὁμολογέω`.
   - **Tentative conclusion (`p. 11`):** Calling Rom 10:5–13 *"the odd exception"* whose words *"do not really form a clear symphony with the rest of his letter"*, Oliver explicitly frames his missionary/prophetic-witness explanation as a modest, subjective proposal (*"it looks quite safe to make the conclusion..."*; *"The answer (for me)..."*).

5. **Paul Does NOT Treat Omitted Deut 30 Clauses as Irrelevant (`S09F-E003`, `Printed p. 4 [PDF p. 4]` & `Table 2, Printed p. 5 [PDF p. 5]`):**
   - Oliver explicitly states: *"Paul does not cite these words, but implicates them in his own context"* (`S09F-E003`, `Printed p. 4 [PDF p. 4]`), citing `Derrett 1983:143` (fn. 9), `Hart 1999`, `Dunn 1988b:603`, and `Kruse 2012:408`.
   - Note also **hedged encasement language (`S09F-E008`, `p. 3`)** (*"It leaves the impression that..."*) and the **`4-3-2-1` line-wrap check (`S09F-E004`, `p. 9`)** verified via `pdftotext -layout`.
