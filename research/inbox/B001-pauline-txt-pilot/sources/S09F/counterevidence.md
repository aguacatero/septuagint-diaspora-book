## 1. Boundary Separating Oliver's Secondary Claims from Primary Textual Data

Because Oliver (2015) conducts a **rhetorical-structural and theological analysis** of Romans 10:5–13 rather than an apparatus-level textual collation of MT vs. Old Greek/LXX variants, B001 must keep Oliver's actual claims strictly separated from project primary-text comparisons (`P01F` / `P03F`).

### A. Canonical Locus `B001-4`: Romans 10:11 / Isaiah 28:16

- **What Oliver Actually Claims:**
  - Oliver explicitly labels the citation in Rom 10:11 as `Is 28:16 (LXX)` (`Printed p. 3 [PDF p. 3]`; `Table 1, Printed p. 5 [PDF p. 5]`) and places it among Paul's prophetic citations (`Printed p. 4 [PDF p. 4]`).
  - He links `καταισχύνω` in Rom 10:11 back to `ἐπαισχύνομαι` in Rom 1:16 (`Printed p. 3 [PDF p. 3]`, citing Jewett 2007:138) and argues that vv. 11–13 use `πᾶς` (vv. 11, 13) and `κύριος πάντων` (v. 12) in a fourfold `γάρ` sequence to assert the universality of God's salvation (`Printed p. 7 [PDF p. 7]`, citing Bechtler 1994:306).
- **What Oliver Explicitly Qualifies or Does NOT Claim (`S09F-E010`):**
  - Oliver **does NOT** claim text-critically that Paul altered LXX Isaiah 28:16 by adding `πᾶς` or modifying the Vorlage.
  - On `Printed p. 3 [PDF p. 3], fn. 5`, Oliver specifically cautions against reading universal faith-righteousness directly out of Isaiah 28:16 without textual discipline: referring to G. Aho (`1981:308`), he writes that stipulating from Isa 28:16 *"that ‘the way of righteousness by faith is meant for all’ ... could be a too subjective reading of the verse within this pericope."*
  - **Counterevidence against over-reading `B001-4` in S09F:** Oliver offers **zero** discussion of the MT (`הַמַּאֲמִין לֹא יָחִישׁ`, "he who believes will not make haste") vs. LXX (`καὶ ὁ πιστεύων οὐ μὴ καταισχυνθῇ`) Vorlage divergence or LXX manuscript variants (`+ ἐπʼ αὐτῷ` in `א A Q`). Therefore S09F provides **Substantive** exegetical/structural evidence for `B001-4`, not primary text-critical collation.

> **Project Primary-Text Note (Non-Source-Attributed — `P01F-P004` / `P03F-P003`):**  
> Primary Greek collation in `B001` (`P01F-P004` `SBLGNT` Romans 10:11: `λέγει γὰρ ἡ γραφή· Πᾶς ὁ πιστεύων ἐπʼ αὐτῷ οὐ καταισχυνθήσεται.`; `P03F-P003` `Swete LXX` Isaiah 28:16: `...καὶ ὁ πιστεύων οὐ μὴ καταισχυνθῇ.` with apparatus `+ επ αυτω א A Q`) shows `Πᾶς` and `ἐπʼ αὐτῷ` present in Paul's form against Swete's B-text of Isa 28:16. This is a project-level primary-text observation from `P01F`/`P03F`, **not** a claim made by Oliver (2015).

### B. Canonical Locus `B001-5`: Romans 10:13 / Joel 2:32 (LXX Joel 3:5)

- **What Oliver Actually Claims (`S09F-E005`, `S09F-E006`, `S09F-E007`):**
  - Oliver classifies Rom 10:13 as a prophetic citation from Joel 2:32 (within Joel 2:28–32; `Printed p. 4 [PDF p. 4]`), noting that Peter quotes **`Acts 2:17–21`** directly (`Printed p. 4 [PDF p. 4], fn. 10`), while Paul cites Joel 2:32 elsewhere only in 1 Cor 1:2 (`Printed p. 4 [PDF p. 4]`).
  - He treats v. 13 as the climactic `γάρ` clause (`Printed p. 7 [PDF p. 7]`) and *"something ‘new’"* that binds oral calling (`ἐπικαλέομαι`, echoing v. 12) to `σωθήσεται` (echoing v. 9; `Printed p. 10 [PDF p. 10]`), pivoting directly into the apostolic missionary chain of Rom 10:14–15 (`Printed p. 5 [PDF p. 5]` and `pp. 10–11`).
- **Counterevidence & Missing Text-Critical Precision in Oliver:**
  - Oliver does not discuss LXX numbering (`Joel 3:5a`) versus Hebrew/English numbering (`Joel 2:32a`), nor does he examine Paul's omission of `καὶ ἔσται` or substitution/addition of postpositive `γάρ`.
  - Oliver's reading of Rom 10:13–15 emphasizes heraldic proclamation and salvation as a present/eschatological reality (in polemical tension with Zane Hodges, Dillow, and Free Grace commentators on eternal vs. temporal salvation; `Printed p. 3 [PDF p. 3]`, `pp. 7–9`, `p. 8 fn. 19`).

> **Project Primary-Text Note (Non-Source-Attributed — `P01F` / `P03F`):**  
> Primary comparison shows Romans 10:13 (`πᾶς γὰρ ὃς ἂν ἐπικαλέσηται τὸ ὄνομα κυρίου σωθήσεται`) reproduces verbatim the core relative and matrix clause of Old Greek/LXX Joel 3:5a (`καὶ ἔσται πᾶς ὃς ἂν ἐπικαλέσηται τὸ ὄνομα κυρίου σωθήσεται`, matching MT Joel 2:32a `כֹּ֧ל אֲשֶׁר־יִקְרָ֛א בְּשֵׁ֥ם יְהוָ֖ה יִמָּלֵ֑ט`), while dropping `καὶ ἔσται` and inserting `γάρ`. This textual comparison is maintained from `P01F`/`P03F`, separate from Oliver's theology.

### C. Contextual Pericope (`B001-CONTEXT`): Romans 10:5–10 & Encasement Architecture

- **Paul Does NOT Treat Omitted Clauses as Irrelevant (`S09F-E003`, `Printed p. 4 [PDF p. 4]` & `Table 2, Printed p. 5 [PDF p. 5]`):**
  - Countering any oversimplification that Paul arbitrarily ignores Deut 30:12–14's covenantal obedience clauses (*"so we/you may obey it"*), Oliver explicitly states: *"Paul does not cite these words, but implicates them in his own context"* (`S09F-E003`, `Printed p. 4 [PDF p. 4]`), noting in fn. 9 (`Printed p. 4 [PDF p. 4]`, citing Derrett 1983:143) that Moses repeated the phrase three times to emphasize obedience and explaining Paul's selective citation via contemporary Jewish expositional practice (Hart 1999; Dunn 1988b; Kruse 2012).
- **Hedged Structural Language on Scriptural Encasement (`S09F-E008`, `Printed p. 3 [PDF p. 3]`):**
  - Oliver introduces his encasement thesis with explicit epistemic hedging (*"It leaves the impression that..."*), signaling a literary-rhetorical synthesis rather than a syntactic proof.
- **Line-Wrap Typography Check (`S09F-E004`, `Printed p. 9 [PDF p. 9]`):**
  - Oliver's printed text reads `1-2-3-4 and 4-3-2-1`; because of an end-of-line hyphen wrap on Printed p. 9 (`4-3-2-\n1`), plain `pdftotext` without `-layout` extracts `4-3-21`. The canonical printed wording is **`4-3-2-1`**.

## 2. Authorial Qualifications, Polemical Context, & Verified Locators

The following internal qualifications and interlocutors constrain Oliver's argument and are verified directly against the 12-page PDF (`oliver_2015_romans_10.pdf`):

1. **Rhetorical Speech-in-Character (`Printed p. 1 [PDF p. 1], fn. 1`):** Oliver notes (citing Kruse 2012:407) that Paul employs `speech-in-character` in Romans 1:18–32 and 10:6–8 alongside chiastic structuring.
2. **Temporal-Restoration vs. Ultimate-Salvation Polemic (`Printed p. 3 [PDF p. 3]`; `Printed p. 6 [PDF p. 6]`; `Printed p. 8 [PDF p. 8], fn. 19`):**
   - On `Printed p. 3 [PDF p. 3]`, Oliver rejects Dillow's (`1992:123`) allegation (alongside Palmer `1975:126`) that `σωτηρία` in Rom 10:1 merely denotes Israel's restoration to Palestine rather than ultimate salvation (and cites Palmer `1975:127` again on `Printed p. 6 [PDF p. 6]`, plus Dillow `1992:127, 384`, Moyer `1997:267`, and Hodges `1985:28–33` in `fn. 19` on `Printed p. 8 [PDF p. 8]` regarding future rewards vs. eternal life).
3. **Lexical Field Qualifications (`Printed p. 3 [PDF p. 3], fn. 6`; `Printed p. 5 [PDF p. 5], fn. 11`):**
   - `ζάω` in Rom 1:17 and 10:5 belongs to the same semantic field according to Danker (`[1957] 2000:424–425`; `Printed p. 3 [PDF p. 3], fn. 6`).
   - `δικαιοσύνη` semantics follow Danker (`[1957] 2000:247–249`; `Printed p. 5 [PDF p. 5], fn. 11`).
4. **Structural Figures & Tables Across the Article:**
   - **Figure 1** (Paul's encased Scriptural structure): `Printed p. 4 [PDF p. 4]`.
   - **Table 1** (OT references in Rom 10:5–15) & **Table 2** (Comparison of Deut 30:12–14 and Rom 10:6–8): `Printed p. 5 [PDF p. 5]`.
   - **Figure 2** (Chiasm in Rom 10:9–10): `Printed p. 6 [PDF p. 6]`.
   - **Figure 3** (`2-4-3-1` to `1-2-3-4` / `4-3-2-1` link between Rom 10:8b and vv. 9–10): `Printed p. 9 [PDF p. 9]`.
5. **Confession, Belief, and Reverse Missionary Sequence (`Printed p. 8 [PDF p. 8]`; `Printed p. 10 [PDF p. 10]`):**
   - On `Printed p. 8 [PDF p. 8]`, Oliver engages **Ironside (`1928:131`)** on whether Paul's heart/mouth confession differs from Jesus's open confession sayings (`Mt 10:32; Lk 12:8`), and in **`fn. 20` on `Printed p. 8 [PDF p. 8]`** cites **Moo (`1996:650`)** regarding `Μωϋσῆς γὰρ γράφει` (`praesens`) occurring nowhere else in this form in Paul's letters (alongside `Moo 1996:657` in the body of `p. 8`).
   - On **`Printed p. 10 [PDF p. 10]`**, Oliver traces Paul's reversal of the faith–confession–calling sequence in Rom 10:14–15 (`ἐπικαλέσωνται -> πιστεύσωσιν -> ἀκούσωσιν -> κηρύσσοντος`; anticipated in `Table 1` and its prose comment on `Printed p. 5 [PDF p. 5]`), underscoring that belief/faith remains the prerequisite behind oral calling (`How can they call on the one they have not believed in?`).
