# S09F Synthesis: Oliver (2015) on Romans 10:5–13, B001-4, and B001-5

**Source ID:** S09F  
**Article:** Willem Oliver, "Romans 10:5–13 revisited," *HTS Teologiese Studies / Theological Studies* 71, no. 3 (2015), Art. #2929, 12 pp. (`http://dx.doi.org/10.4102/hts.v71i3.2929`)

---

## 1. B001-4: Romans 10:11 / Isaiah 28:16 (`Substantive`)

Oliver engages **Romans 10:11 / Isaiah 28:16** across **Printed pp. 3, 4, 5, 7, 10, and 11 [PDF pp. 3, 4, 5, 7, 10, 11]** (`S09F-E005`, `S09F-E009`, `S09F-E010`):

1. **Septuagint Labeling and Prophetic Genre (`Printed p. 3, p. 4, p. 5 [PDF p. 3, p. 4, p. 5]`):** Oliver explicitly identifies Paul's citation in v. 11 as **`Is 28:16 LXX`** (`S09F-E009`, `p. 3`; `Table 1, p. 5`) and groups it with Joel 2:32 as prophetic Scriptures invoked to confirm Paul's gospel claim (`p. 4`).
2. **Intertextual Link to Romans 1:16 (`S09F-E009`, `Printed p. 3 [PDF p. 3]`):** Through `καταισχύνω` (Rom 10:11), Paul recalls `ἐπαισχύνομαι` in Romans 1:16, grounding the removal of human shame (citing Jewett 2007:138) in faith-salvation articulated in Rom 10:9–10.
3. **Methodological Caution Against Over-Reading Aho (`S09F-E010`, `Printed p. 3 [PDF p. 3], fn. 5`):** Oliver cautions that Aho's (`1981:308`) claim that Isa 28:16 itself stipulates *"that ‘the way of righteousness by faith is meant for all’"* may be *"a too subjective reading of the verse within this pericope."*
4. **Universality Argument via `γάρ` and `πᾶς` (`S09F-E005`, `Printed p. 7 [PDF p. 7]` & `p. 10`):** Oliver places v. 11 at the head of a four-clause `γάρ` chain (vv. 11–13) asserting universal salvation through `πᾶς` (vv. 11, 13) and `κύριος πάντων` (v. 12; citing Bechtler 1994:306), summarized on p. 10 as *"No shame for a believer / Faith is for everyone."*
5. **Scope Boundary:** Oliver does **not** perform a text-critical collation of MT Isa 28:16 vs. LXX Isa 28:16 and does **not** claim Paul added `πᾶς` to the Septuagint text.

## 2. B001-5: Romans 10:13 / Joel 2:32 [LXX 3:5] (`Substantive`)

Oliver engages **Romans 10:13 / Joel 2:32** across **Printed pp. 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, and 11 [PDF pp. 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11]** (`S09F-E005`, `S09F-E006`, `S09F-E007`):

1. **Prophetic Classification and Pauline Usage (`Printed p. 4 [PDF p. 4], fn. 10`):** Joel 2:32 (from Joel 2:28–32) is treated alongside Isa 28:16 as a prophetic witness, cited directly by Peter in **`Acts 2:17–21`** (fn. 10) and used by Paul elsewhere only in 1 Cor 1:2.
2. **Scriptural Crescendo of Oral Invocation (`S09F-E006`, `Printed p. 10 [PDF p. 10]`):** Completing the fourfold `γάρ` and `πᾶς` universality sequence (`S09F-E005`, `p. 7`), v. 13 introduces *"something ‘new’"* (pp. 7, 10) by tying `ἐπικαλέομαι` (echoing v. 12) directly to `σωθήσεται` (echoing `σωθήσῃ` in v. 9).
3. **Hinge into Apostolic Mission (`S09F-E007`, `Printed p. 5 [PDF p. 5]` & `pp. 10–11`):** Verse 13 transitions from individual confession/belief (vv. 9–10) to Paul's apostolic proclamation in vv. 14–15 (citing Isa 52:7), reversing the verbal sequence (`call -> believe -> hear -> preach`) on p. 10.
4. **Primary-Text Separation:** Project text-critical comparisons (LXX Joel 3:5a vs. MT 2:32a, omission of `καὶ ἔσται`, addition of `γάρ`) come from `P01F`/`P03F`, not from Oliver's secondary exegesis.

## 3. B001-CONTEXT: Romans 10:5–10 & Pericope Encasement (`Contextual Only`)

Oliver's structural claims in Romans 10:5–10 are retained strictly as pericope context (`B001-CONTEXT`):

- **Prophetic Encasement (`S09F-E008`, `Printed p. 3`; `Figure 1, p. 4`; `Table 1, p. 5`):** Apart from v. 12 and vv. 9–10, Paul encases his argument with OT quotations (Lev 18:5, Hab 2:4, Deut 9:4, Deut 30:12–14, Isa 28:16, Joel 2:32).
- **Law vs. Faith Antithesis (`S09F-E001`, `Printed p. 5`, `Table 1`):** Rom 10:5a (Lev 18:5) vs. Rom 10:6a (Deut 30:11–12) forms an `a–b–b–a` chiasm introducing speech-in-character (p. 1, fn. 1).
- **Christological Alteration & Implied Obedience (`S09F-E002`, `S09F-E003`, `Printed p. 4`; `Table 2, p. 5`):** Paul alters *"beyond the sea"* (Deut 30:13) to *"into the deep (abyss)"* in Rom 10:7 (`S09F-E002`), and although Paul passes over *"so we/you may obey it"* (Deut 30:12–14), *"Paul does not cite these words, but implicates them in his own context"* (`S09F-E003`, p. 4).
- **Double Chiasm (`S09F-E004`, `Printed p. 9`; `Figures 2 & 3, pp. 6 & 9`):** Paul takes *"mouth"* and *"heart"* from Deut 30:14 (Rom 10:8b, `2-4-3-1`) and forms the `1-2-3-4` and `4-3-2-1` combinations of vv. 9–10 (`4-3-2-1` wraps across lines as `4-3-2-\n1` on p. 9).
