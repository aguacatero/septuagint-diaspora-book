# S09F — Bibliography Leads

Disaggregated bibliographic entries cited, relied upon, or disputed by Willem Oliver in *Romans 10:5–13 revisited*, *HTS Teologiese Studies / Theological Studies*, Vol. 71, No. 3 (2015), Art. #2929, 12 pages.

---

## 1. Primary Exegetical Leads on Deuteronomy 30, Leviticus 18, and Isaiah 28 (B001-4)

### Akio Ito (2006)
- **Author:** Akio Ito
- **Title:** *"The written Torah and the oral Gospel: Romans 10:5–13 in the dynamic tension between orality and literacy"*
- **Publication Year:** 2006 (*Novum Testamentum*, Vol. 48, No. 3, pp. 234–260)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 4 [PDF p. 4], p. 5 [PDF p. 5], p. 6 [PDF p. 6], p. 8 [PDF p. 8], p. 9 [PDF p. 9]
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon and disputed. Oliver relies on Ito's demonstration that Paul deliberately edits significant words in Deuteronomy 30:11–14 to oppose the Law and favor oral gospel proclamation, but disputes Ito's view that verses 9–10 form synonymous poetic parallelism.

### J. F. Hart (1999)
- **Author:** J. F. Hart
- **Title:** *"Why confess Christ? The use and abuse of Romans 10:9–10"*
- **Publication Year:** 1999 (*Journal of the Grace Evangelical Society*, Vol. 12, No. 23, pp. 3–35)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 1 [PDF p. 1], p. 3 [PDF p. 3], p. 4 [PDF p. 4], p. 5 [PDF p. 5], p. 7 [PDF p. 7], p. 8 [PDF p. 8]
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon and disputed. Cited for the Jewish midrashic technique of partial citation with interpretive glosses in Romans 10:6–8; disputed on whether salvation in Romans 10:10 applies exclusively to Christians already justified.

### James D. G. Dunn (1988)
- **Author:** James D. G. Dunn
- **Title:** *Romans 9–16* (Word Biblical Commentary, Vol. 38B)
- **Publication Year:** 1988 (Dallas: Word Books, pp. 586, 603, 607, 609)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 2 [PDF p. 2], p. 3 [PDF p. 3], p. 4 [PDF p. 4], p. 8 [PDF p. 8]
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon and disputed. Oliver affirms Dunn's recognition of Jewish expositional methods adapting Deuteronomy 30, but rejects Dunn's reading of Romans 10:9–10 as mere synonymous parallelism.

### Colin G. Kruse (2012)
- **Author:** Colin G. Kruse
- **Title:** *Paul’s Letter to the Romans* (Pillar New Testament Commentary)
- **Publication Year:** 2012 (Grand Rapids: Eerdmans, pp. 407–410)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 1 [PDF p. 1], p. 4 [PDF p. 4], p. 7 [PDF p. 7], p. 8 [PDF p. 8]
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon and disputed. Relied upon for Paul's rhetorical personification (prosopopoeia) of righteousness by faith in Romans 10:6; disputed for collapsing faith and confession into identical concepts.

### H.-J. Eckstein (1988)
- **Author:** H.-J. Eckstein
- **Title:** *"Nahe ist dir das Wort: Exegetische Erwägungen zu Röm 10, 8"*
- **Publication Year:** 1988 (*Zeitschrift für die Neutestamentliche Wissenschaft*, Vol. 79, pp. 204–220)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 3 [PDF p. 3]
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Provides key exegetical analysis of Paul's quotation of Deuteronomy 30:14 in Romans 10:8, demonstrating that the "near word" links faith directly to salvation.

### Walter C. Kaiser Jr. (1971)
- **Author:** Walter C. Kaiser Jr.
- **Title:** *"Leviticus 18:5 and Paul: Do this and you shall live (eternally?)"*
- **Publication Year:** 1971 (*Journal of the Evangelical Theological Society*, Vol. 14, No. 1, pp. 20–28)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 3 [PDF p. 3]
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Examines Paul's quotation of Leviticus 18:5 in Romans 10:5 and Galatians 3:12, analyzing Jewish covenantal and legal interpretations.

### Bruce C. Dunson (2011)
- **Author:** Bruce C. Dunson
- **Title:** *"Faith in Romans: The salvation of the individual or life in community?"*
- **Publication Year:** 2011 (*Journal for the Study of the New Testament*, Vol. 34, pp. 19–46)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 2 [PDF p. 2], p. 3 [PDF p. 3], p. 6 [PDF p. 6], p. 7 [PDF p. 7], p. 11 [PDF p. 11]
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Demonstrates that *ek pisteōs* serves as the cohesive theological glue holding Paul's discourse on law, faith, and righteousness together in Romans.

### Adolf Schlatter (1991)
- **Author:** Adolf Schlatter
- **Title:** *Gottes Gerechtigkeit: Ein Kommentar zum Römerbrief*
- **Publication Year:** 1991 (Stuttgart: Calwer Verlag, pp. 312–313)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 9 [PDF p. 9]
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Emphasizes the fundamental contrast in Romans 10:5–6 between Moses writing (*graphei*) regarding the Law and faith speaking (*legei*) regarding the gospel.

### G. Aho (1981)
- **Author:** G. Aho
- **Title:** *"3D Sunday in Lent"*
- **Publication Year:** 1981 (*Concordia Theological Quarterly*, Vol. 45, No. 4, pp. 308–309)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 3 [PDF p. 3], p. 8 [PDF p. 8]
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. Oliver rejects Aho's view that faith and confession in Romans 10:9–10 are merely two sides of the same coin.

### Douglas J. Moo (1996)
- **Author:** Douglas J. Moo
- **Title:** *The Epistle to the Romans* (NICNT)
- **Publication Year:** 1996 (Grand Rapids: Eerdmans, pp. 64–65, 650, 657)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 2 [PDF p. 2], p. 8 [PDF p. 8]
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. Oliver rejects Moo's claim that oral confession is not an independent requirement for salvation alongside heart-faith.

### Herman Ridderbos (1959)
- **Author:** Herman Ridderbos
- **Title:** *Commentaar op het Nieuwe Testament: Aan de Romeinen*
- **Publication Year:** 1959 (Kampen: J. H. Kok, p. 237)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 6 [PDF p. 6], fn. 13
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Explains that the spoken word in Deuteronomy 30:14 was delivered so hearers could repeat it orally and meditate on it in their heart.

## 2. Primary Leads on Joel 2:32, Proclamation, and Eschatological Salvation (B001-5)

### Zane C. Hodges (1989)
- **Author:** Zane C. Hodges
- **Title:** *Absolutely Free: A Biblical Reply to Lordship Salvation*
- **Publication Year:** 1989 (Dallas: Redención Viva, pp. 107, 197–198)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 7 [PDF p. 7], p. 8 [PDF p. 8], p. 9 [PDF p. 9]
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. Oliver rejects Hodges's claim that calling on the Lord's name in Joel 2:32 / Romans 10:13 is only prayer by believers who are already saved.

### J. Ross Wagner (2002)
- **Author:** J. Ross Wagner
- **Title:** *Heralds of the Good News: Isaiah and Paul "in concert" in the Letter to the Romans*
- **Publication Year:** 2002 (Leiden: Brill, pp. 170–180, 356–359)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 5 [PDF p. 5]
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Analyzes Paul's prophetic citation environment in Romans 10, framing the herald's mission around Isaiah and Joel.

### Karl Olav Sandnes (1991)
- **Author:** Karl Olav Sandnes
- **Title:** *Paul – One of the Prophets?: A Contribution to the Apostle’s Self-Understanding*
- **Publication Year:** 1991 (Tübingen: J.C.B. Mohr, pp. 154–171)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 5 [PDF p. 5]
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Examines Paul's apostolic self-understanding as an eschatological prophet heralding the gospel.

### J. D. M. Derrett (1983)
- **Author:** J. D. M. Derrett
- **Title:** *"The reason for the cock-crowings"*
- **Publication Year:** 1983 (*New Testament Studies*, Vol. 29, pp. 142–144)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 4 [PDF p. 4], fn. 9
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Explains that three-fold repetition in Semitic rhetoric (as with Moses's obedience clauses in Deut 30:12–14) confirmed binding legal force.

## 3. Literary Structure, Rhetorical Chiasm, and Hermeneutical Framework

### Nils W. Lund (1942)
- **Author:** Nils W. Lund
- **Title:** *Chiasmus in the New Testament: A Study in Formgeschichte*
- **Publication Year:** 1942 (Chapel Hill: University of North Carolina Press, pp. 31, 35, 139)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 1 [PDF p. 1], p. 2 [PDF p. 2]
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Foundation for identifying chiastic structures in biblical literature, dating chiasm back to ancient Semitic and Greco-Roman rhetoric.

### J. M. Gibbs (2013)
- **Author:** J. M. Gibbs
- **Title:** *Chiastic Structuring: An Introduction*
- **Publication Year:** 2013 (Online Publication / Research Monograph)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 2 [PDF p. 2]
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Establishes the rule that the theological climax of a chiasm lies at its center (*sōthēsē* in Rom 10:9).

### Ulrich Wilckens (1980)
- **Author:** Ulrich Wilckens
- **Title:** *Der Brief an die Römer* (EKKNT 6)
- **Publication Year:** 1980 (Zürich: Benziger Verlag, pp. 11, 227)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 6 [PDF p. 6], fn. 14
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Traces the early baptismal confession formula underlying Romans 10:9.

### Eduard Lohse (2003)
- **Author:** Eduard Lohse
- **Title:** *Der Brief an die Römer* (KEK 4)
- **Publication Year:** 2003 (Göttingen: Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht, pp. 296–297)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 6 [PDF p. 6], fn. 14
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Analyzes the oral public confession of Jesus as Lord in early Christian liturgy.

### William Sanday & Arthur C. Headlam (1902)
- **Author:** William Sanday & Arthur C. Headlam
- **Title:** *A Critical and Exegetical Commentary on the Epistle to the Romans* (ICC)
- **Publication Year:** 1902 (Edinburgh: T & T Clark, p. 290)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 7 [PDF p. 7], p. 8 [PDF p. 8]
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Identifies the public witness character of *homologeō* in Romans 10:9–10.

### Ernst Käsemann (1973)
- **Author:** Ernst Käsemann
- **Title:** *An die Römer* (HNT 8a)
- **Publication Year:** 1973 (Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck, pp. 268–275)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 2 [PDF p. 2]
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon and disputed. Classical discussion of the tension between the righteousness of the Law and the righteousness of faith in Romans 10:5–8.

### Steven R. Bechtler (1994)
- **Author:** Steven R. Bechtler
- **Title:** *"Christ, the telos of the Law: The aim of Romans 10:4–14"*
- **Publication Year:** 1994 (*Catholic Biblical Quarterly*, Vol. 56, No. 2, pp. 288–308)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 1 [PDF p. 1], p. 6 [PDF p. 6], p. 7 [PDF p. 7], p. 11 [PDF p. 11]
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Analyzes the universal scope of salvation (*pas*) and the relationship between Christ as *telos nomou* and the Old Testament citations.

### Thomas R. Schreiner (1998)
- **Author:** Thomas R. Schreiner
- **Title:** *Romans* (Baker Exegetical Commentary on the New Testament)
- **Publication Year:** 1998 (Grand Rapids: Baker Book House, pp. 556–563)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 8 [PDF p. 8]
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Analyzes the synthetic connection between heart-faith and oral confession in Romans 10:9–10.

### N. T. Wright (2002)
- **Author:** N. T. Wright
- **Title:** *"The Letter to the Romans: Introduction, Commentary, and Reflections"*
- **Publication Year:** 2002 (in *The New Interpreter’s Bible*, Vol. X, pp. 393–770, Nashville: Abingdon Press)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 2 [PDF p. 2]
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Interprets Paul's covenantal framework in Romans 9–11 and the fulfillment of Deuteronomy 30 in the renewal of God's people.

### Francis Watson (2004)
- **Author:** Francis Watson
- **Title:** *Paul and the Hermeneutics of Faith*
- **Publication Year:** 2004 (London: T & T Clark, pp. 330–342)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 2 [PDF p. 2], p. 3 [PDF p. 3]
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Detailed textual and hermeneutical study of Paul's intertextual dialogue with Leviticus 18:5 and Deuteronomy 30.

### Mark A. Seifrid (2007)
- **Author:** Mark A. Seifrid
- **Title:** *"Romans"*
- **Publication Year:** 2007 (in G. K. Beale & D. A. Carson, eds., *Commentary on the New Testament Use of the Old Testament*, pp. 607–694, Grand Rapids: Baker Academic)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 2 [PDF p. 2]
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Comprehensive analysis of Paul's explicit citations of the Pentateuch, Isaiah, and Joel in Romans 10.
