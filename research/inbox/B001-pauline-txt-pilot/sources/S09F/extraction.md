## B001-4: Romans 10:6–8 / Deuteronomy 30:12–14 (and Rom 10:11 / Isa 28:16, Rom 10:5 / Lev 18:5)

### S09F-E001
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** S09F-E001
- **B001 Passage ID:** B001-4
- **Author's Claim:** Paul constructs an antithetical a-b-b-a chiasm in Romans 10:5a and 6a to contrast righteousness based on the Law with righteousness based on faith.
- **Short Exact Quotation:** "This contrast is set in the form of a stark a-b-b-a chiasm in verses 5a and 6a"
- **Printed Page Number:** Printed p. 5 [PDF p. 5]
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 5 [PDF p. 5]
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [2]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Greek text of Romans 10:5a (τὴν δικαιοσύνην τὴν ἐκ νόμου) citing Leviticus 18:5 contrasted with Romans 10:6a (ἡ δὲ ἐκ πίστεως δικαιοσύνη) adapting Deuteronomy 30:11–12.
- **Claim Type:** Structural / Chiastic / Exegetical
- **Qualifications Stated by Author:** Paul contrasts the old righteousness attached to Moses with the new righteousness by faith speaking for itself.
- **Extraction Confidence:** HIGH

### S09F-E002

- **Short Exact Quotation:** "The ‘sea’ of Deuteronomy 30:13 is changed to the ‘deep (abyss)’ (Rm 10:7) that would better refer to bringing Christ from the dead."
- **Printed Page Number:** Printed p. 4 [PDF p. 4]
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 4 [PDF p. 4]
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [5]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Romans 10:7 (εἰς τὴν ἄβυσσον) compared with LXX Deuteronomy 30:13 (εἰς τὸ πέραν τῆς θαλάσσης) and MT Deuteronomy 30:13.
- **Claim Type:** Textual-critical / Exegetical
- **Qualifications Stated by Author:** The alteration specifically introduces Paul's explanatory gloss "that is, to bring Christ up from the dead."
- **Extraction Confidence:** HIGH

### S09F-E003
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** S09F-E003
- **B001 Passage ID:** B001-4
- **Author's Claim:** Paul deliberately omits Moses's repeated phrase "so we/you may obey it" from Deuteronomy 30:12, 13, and 14, removing the Mosaic requirement of legal obedience.
- **Short Exact Quotation:** "Three times Moses used the words ‘so we/you may obey it’, thereby making it clear that the Law should be obeyed."
- **Printed Page Number:** Printed p. 4 [PDF p. 4]
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 4 [PDF p. 4]
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [6]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Comparison of Deuteronomy 30:12–14 with Romans 10:6–8, where the obedience clauses are omitted from each clause.
- **Claim Type:** Textual-critical / Exegetical
- **Qualifications Stated by Author:** Partial citation with interpretive glosses was a recognised Jewish hermeneutical method (citing Hart, Dunn, and Kruse).
- **Extraction Confidence:** HIGH

## B001-5: Romans 10:13 / Joel 2:32 (LXX Joel 3:5)

### S09F-E006
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** S09F-E006
- **B001 Passage ID:** B001-5
- **Author's Claim:** Paul cites Joel 2:32 in Romans 10:13 as a scriptural crescendo confirming that oral calling on the Lord's name results in eschatological salvation.
- **Short Exact Quotation:** "It is the ‘something-being-said’ (in this case a repetition of the verb he has just utilised in verse 12) that results in σωτηρίαν."
- **Printed Page Number:** Printed p. 10 [PDF p. 10]
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 10 [PDF p. 10]
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [11]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Verbatim quotation of LXX Joel 3:5 (Πᾶς γὰρ ὃς ἂν ἐπικαλέσηται τὸ ὄνομα κυρίου σωθήσεται) in Romans 10:13.
- **Claim Type:** Exegetical / Structural / Theological
- **Qualifications Stated by Author:** The future passive verb σωθήσεται in verse 13 mirrors σωθήσῃ in verse 9, confirming oral invocation alongside faith as necessary.
- **Extraction Confidence:** HIGH

### S09F-E007
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** S09F-E007
- **B001 Passage ID:** B001-5

- **Short Exact Quotation:** "In Romans 10:14–15 Paul elaborates on verse 13 (citing a part of Is 52:7), emphasising his calling as an apostle, bringing the message orally"
- **Printed Page Number:** Printed p. 5 [PDF p. 5]
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 5 [PDF p. 5]
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [13]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** The verbal chain linking ἐπικαλέσηται (v. 13) to ἐπικαλέσωνται (v. 14a), πιστεύσωσιν (v. 14b), and κηρύσσοντος (v. 14c).
- **Claim Type:** Literary / Structural / Rhetorical

- **Extraction Confidence:** HIGH

## Canonical Scriptural Architecture: Romans 10:5–13

- **B001 Passage ID:** B001-4, B001-5
- **Author's Claim:** Except for verse 12 and the chiastic confession in verses 9–10, Romans 10:5–13 consists of Old Testament citations, creating the impression that Paul encased his words in Scriptures to give them greater prominence.
- **Short Exact Quotation:** "It leaves the impression that, besides the use of the (double) chiasm, Paul has encased these words with Scriptures so as to highlight it more or to give it more prominence"
- **Printed Page Number:** Printed p. 3 [PDF p. 3]
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 3 [PDF p. 3]
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [1]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Encasement structure incorporating Leviticus 18:5 (v. 5), Deuteronomy 30:12–14 (vv. 6–8), Isaiah 28:16 (v. 11), and Joel 2:32 (v. 13) around verses 9–10.
- **Claim Type:** Literary / Structural / Canonical
- **Qualifications Stated by Author:** Paul deliberately selects Old Testament citations from prophetic and covenantal texts to create an oral heraldic framework for his gospel.
- **Extraction Confidence:** HIGH
