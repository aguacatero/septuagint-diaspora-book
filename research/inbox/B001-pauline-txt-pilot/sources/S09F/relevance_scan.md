## 1. Summary of Project Locus Relevance

In *Romans 10:5–13 revisited* (2015), Willem Oliver focuses specifically on the Old Testament citations and rhetorical structure of the sub-pericope Romans 10:5–13:
- **B001-4:** Romans 10:6–8 / Deuteronomy 30:12–14 (alongside Rom 10:11 / Isa 28:16 and Rom 10:5 / Lev 18:5) — Extended Discussion
- **B001-5:** Romans 10:13 / Joel 2:32 (LXX Joel 3:5) — Substantive Discussion

The remaining ten B001 comparisons are **NOT DISCUSSED** anywhere in this source volume.
