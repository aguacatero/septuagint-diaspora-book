## 1. Summary of Project Locus Relevance

In *Romans 10:5–13 revisited* (2015), Willem Oliver analyzes the rhetorical and scriptural structure of Romans 10:5–13. Evaluated strictly against the canonical twelve-locus inventory in `research-queue/B001-pauline-txt-pilot.md`:

- **B001-4 (Romans 10:11 / Isaiah 28:16):** Discussed — **Substantive** exegetical and intertextual discussion (`Printed pp. 3–5, 7, 10–11 [PDF pp. 3–5, 7, 10–11]`, with passing verse citations on pp. 1, 8 and Figure 3 on p. 9). Oliver identifies Romans 10:11 as a reference to `Is 28:16 (LXX)`, links `καταισχύνω` to `ἐπαισχύνομαι` in Romans 1:16 (p. 3), qualifies Aho's (1981:308) reading of Isaiah 28:16 (p. 3, fn. 5), locates the citation in the prophetic frame (p. 4; Table 1, p. 5; p. 11), and links `πᾶς` (vv. 11 & 13) to the universality of salvation (p. 7). He does **not** conduct an extended MT-vs-LXX text-critical collation of Isaiah 28:16.
- **B001-5 (Romans 10:13 / Joel 2:32):** Discussed — **Substantive** discussion (`Printed pp. 4–5, 7–11 [PDF pp. 4–5, 7–11]`). Oliver identifies the Joel 2:32 citation, examines its oral-invocation verb `ἐπικαλέω` and future passive `σωθήσεται` (p. 7, fn. 17; p. 10), and traces its transition into the apostolic preaching chain of Romans 10:14–15 (p. 5; pp. 10–11).
- **Contextual / Ancillary Pericope Material (`B001-CONTEXT`):** Oliver's extended treatment of Romans 10:5–10 (Leviticus 18:5 in v. 5, Deuteronomy 30:11–14 in vv. 6–8, the double chiasm in vv. 9–10, and the scriptural encasement architecture of vv. 5–13) is preserved separately under `B001-CONTEXT` so that it does not inflate direct `B001-4` (`Romans 10:11 / Isaiah 28:16`) coverage.

The remaining ten B001 comparisons (`B001-1`–`B001-3`, `B001-6`–`B001-12`) are **NOT DISCUSSED** anywhere in this source volume.
