## 5. B001-5: Romans 10:13 / Joel 2:32 (Secondary Interpretive Cross-Reference)
- **Page / Locator:** Printed p. 3 (Raw scan p. 79; Derivative p. 22).
- **Theological / Formulaic Adaptation:**
  - On page 3, in the exegetical note on `τοῖς ἐπικαλουμένοις` (1 Cor 1:2), the authors trace the invocation formula to Joel 2:32 and cross-reference Romans 10:12–13:
    > *"This goes back to Joel ii. 32, and involves the thought of faith, the common bond of all. See Rom. x. 12, 13. Here, as there, St Paul significantly brings in the worship of Christ under the O.T. formula for worship addressed to the LORD God of Israel. To be a believer is to worship Christ."*
- **Significance for B001:**
  - Demonstrates that Robertson and Plummer recognize a consistent Pauline christological transfer of the Old Testament Septuagint formula for invoking Yahweh across both 1 Corinthians (1:2) and Romans (10:12–13).

## 6. B001-6: Romans 11:34 / Isaiah 40:13 (Comparative Cross-Epistle Evidence)
- **Page / Locator:** Printed pp. 50–51 (Raw scan pp. 126–127; Derivative pp. 31–32).
- **Textual Divergence and Epistolary Comparison:**
  - On page 50, the authors conduct an explicit textual comparison between 1 Cor 2:16 and Rom 11:34:
    > *"From Isa. xl. 13 (LXX), but shortened by the omission of the middle clause, καὶ τίς αὐτοῦ σύνβουλος ἐγένετο; This clause is retained in Rom. xi. 34, while ὃς συνβιβάσει αὐτόν is omitted."*
  - Authentically printed text preserves Hellenistic unassimilated orthography with nu (`σύνβουλος`, `συνβιβάσει`), adhering to Westcott-Hort typography.
- **Hebrew vs Septuagint Ambiguity:**
  - On page 50, the authors analyze the semantic shift between the Hebrew and Greek versions:
    > *"νοῦν Κυρίου. The νοῦν (LXX) corresponds to the Hebrew for πνεῦμα in the original. In God, νοῦς and πνεῦμα are identical... but not in aspect, νοῦς being suitable to denote the Divine knowledge or counsel, πνεῦμα the Divine action, either in creation or in grace."*
- **Syntactical Irregularity Resulting from Selective Citation:**
  - On page 50, the authors note the grammatical consequence of Paul's omission:
    > *"As St Paul omits the clause containing σύνβουλος, the ὅς is left without any proper construction. But it finds a kind of antecedent in τίς; 'Who hath known... that he should instruct'."*
- **Textual Variant in 1 Cor 2:16b:**
  - On page 51, the authors evaluate the variant reading Χριστοῦ (supported by ℵ A C D3 E L P) versus Κυρίου (supported by B D* F G), noting that scribes would likely alter Χριστοῦ to Κυρίου to harmonize with the preceding quotation.

## 8. B001-8: 1 Corinthians 1:19 / Isaiah 29:14
- **Page / Locator:** Printed p. 19 (also Introduction § VI, p. liii; Derivative pp. 10, 25).
- **Allows Authorial Adaptation & Unknown/Cross-Scriptural Influence:**
  - On page 19, the authors observe that Paul substitutes ἀθετήσω for LXX κρύψω "in accordance with St Paul's usual freedom of citation."
  - They further note an unknown or cross-scriptural verbal influence: *"Possibly ἀθετήσω comes from Ps. xxxiii. 10."*
- **Disagrees with Another Scholar:**
  - On page 19, the authors address Meyer and Ellicott's classification of σοφός, γραμματεύς, and συνζητητής in verse 20: *"This allotment of the terms is adopted by Clement of Alexandria and by Theodoret, and is more probable than that of Meyer and Ellicott, which makes σοφός generic..."*

## 9. B001-9: 1 Corinthians 6:16 / Genesis 2:24
- **Page / Locator:** Printed p. 126 (also Introduction § VI, p. liii; Derivative pp. 10, 36).
- **Discusses a Competing Textual Form:**
  - On page 126, the authors highlight that Paul quotes Genesis 2:24 directly from the LXX containing οἱ δύο, *"although it is not in the original"* (the Hebrew text as represented in standard witnesses).
- **Allows Unknown Source / Textual Form:**
  - On page 126, regarding ὁ κολλώμενος, the authors state: *"The word may come from προσκολλᾶσθαι in Gen. ii. 24, as in Eph. v. 31, or possibly from Ecclus. xix. 2..."*
- **Notes Textual / Formulaic Ambiguity:**
  - On page 126, regarding the subject of φησίν, the authors note: *"The subject to be understood with φησίν must always depend upon the context... Here we may do the same [understand 'God'], or (what amounts to the same) supply ἡ γραφή."*

## 10. B001-10: 1 Corinthians 9:9 / Deuteronomy 25:4
- **Page / Locator:** Printed pp. 183–184 (also Introduction § VII, p. lxii; Derivative pp. 19, 43–44).
- **Acknowledges Uncertainty Between Competing Textual Forms:**
  - On page 183, the authors explicitly open their discussion with measured reserve:
    > *"It is not easy to decide between φιμώσεις and κημώσεις."*
  - They then balance external manuscript testimony (Alexandrian and Byzantine witnesses supporting φιμώσεις against B* D* F G, Chrysostom, and Theodoret supporting κημώσεις) against transcriptional probability:
    > *"How could κημώσεις be so well attested, if it were not original? If it were original it would readily be corrected to the LXX, esp. as κημόω is rare."*
- **Disagrees with Another Scholar:**
  - On page 184, the authors explicitly disagree with Philo (*De Somniis* i. 16; *De Sacrificiis* 1) and the Epistle of Barnabas (x. 1–2), who rejected the literal meaning of Mosaic animal laws:
    > *"St Paul elsewhere allegorizes the O.T. ... but in neither case does he reject the literal meaning. It is not probable that he does so here..."*
- **Expresses Uncertainty & Semantic Ambiguity:**
  - On page 184, the authors express uncertainty over the translation of πάντως ("assuredly" vs. "entirely") and the scope of ἡμᾶς ("Christians", "the Jewish nation", or "mankind generally").

## 11. B001-11: 1 Corinthians 10:7 / Exodus 32:6
- **Page / Locator:** Printed pp. 203–204 (also Introduction § VI, p. liii; Derivative pp. 10, 52–53).
- **Acknowledges Evidence That Weakens an Alternative Interpretation:**
  - On page 203 (fn. *), the authors reject taking τύποι as formal redemptive-historical "types" (where Israelites prefigure Corinthians), noting that this reading fails because *"This would imply that the Corinthians were predestined to fall as the Israelites did."*
- **Discusses Competing Textual Variants:**
  - On page 204, the authors evaluate variants in the introductory formula before γέγραπται: preferring ὥσπερ (ℵ A B D3 L) over ὡς (C D* K P), and πεῖν (B* D* F G) over πιεῖν (A B3 C D3 E K L P).
- **Disagrees with / Cites Another Scholar:**
  - On page 204, the authors discuss Calvin's query regarding why Paul cites the banquet and sport rather than image adoration, noting Calvin's resolution that Corinthian believers thought they could indulge in accessories without engaging in idolatrous worship.
