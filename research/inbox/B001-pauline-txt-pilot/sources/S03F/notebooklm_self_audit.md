Itemized Audit Findings & Corrections
1. Preservation of Possibility Framing for Textual Origin (Check #4 & #6)
Location: Page 19 (Note on 1 Corinthians 1:19 / Isaiah 29:14).
Previous Extraction: Characterized the substitution of ἀθετήσω for κρύψω strictly as Paul's own stylistic adjustment.
Source Observation: Robertson and Plummer explicitly note a potential cross-scriptural origin as a possibility: "Possibly ἀθετήσω comes from Ps. xxxiii. 10" [p. 19].
Correction: Update claim S03F-E001 to explicitly preserve the authors' possibility framing: Robertson and Plummer state that Paul substitutes ἀθετήσω for LXX κρύψω in accordance with his usual freedom of citation, noting that ἀθετήσω possibly originates from Psalm 33:10 LXX.
2. Explicit Qualification on the Semantic Interpretation of πάντως (Check #6)
Location: Page 184 (Note on 1 Corinthians 9:9 / Deuteronomy 25:4).
Previous Extraction: Focused primarily on Paul's rejection of Philo's radical allegory without fully detailing the condition attached to πάντως in verse 10.
Source Observation: On page 184, the authors state: "If πάντως means 'altogether,' it need not be pressed to mean that oxen are not cared for at all by God..." [p. 184].
Correction: Refine claim S03F-E006 to include the explicit lexical qualification: Robertson and Plummer hold that Paul retains the literal principle of Deuteronomy 25:4 while prioritizing its higher spiritual application, noting that even if πάντως is rendered "altogether," it need not be pressed as an absolute denial of God's care for livestock.
