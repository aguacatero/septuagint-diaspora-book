# P02F — B001 Textual Inventory (Primary Relevance Scan)

**Source ID:** P02F  
**Source Title:** *The Old Testament in Greek according to the Septuagint, Vol. I: Genesis–IV Kings*  
**Editor:** Henry Barclay Swete, D.D. (3rd ed., Cambridge University Press, 1901)  
**Source Class:** PRIMARY (Septuagint / Old Greek manual critical edition)  
**Collation Witnesses in Apparatus:** Codex Vaticanus (B, primary base text where extant), Codex Alexandrinus (A), Codex Cottonianus Genesis (D), Codex Bodleianus Genesis (E), Codex Ambrosianus (F)  

---

## Overview

Because P02F is Volume I of Swete's Septuagint edition covering Genesis through IV Kings, only the five Old Testament passages falling within the Pentateuch (Genesis, Exodus, Deuteronomy) are `TEXT_PRESENT`. The seven prophetic quotations (Hosea, Joel, Isaiah) are `NOT_IN_SOURCE` as they belong to Volume III (*The Prophets*).

The printed Greek text and critical apparatus in P02F constitute the primary textual evidence for the Septuagint / Old Greek side of these five B001 comparisons.
