# P02F — Bibliography Leads

**Source ID:** P02F  
**Source Title:** *The Old Testament in Greek according to the Septuagint, Vol. I: Genesis–IV Kings*  
**Editor:** Henry Barclay Swete, D.D. (3rd ed., Cambridge University Press, 1901)  

---

## Direct Manuscript & Edition Leads Cited in Swete Vol. I

The following editions, collations, and scholarly reference works are explicitly cited in Swete's Preface and Table of Manuscripts as foundational for establishing the Septuagint text of Genesis, Exodus, and Deuteronomy:

1. **Codex Vaticanus Graecus 1209 (Codex B)**
   - *Bibliotheca Apostolica Vaticana*, Rome.
   - Photographic facsimile: *Bibliorum Sacrorum Graecorum Codex Vaticanus B*, ed. Cozza-Luzi (Rome, 1889–1890).
   - Core running text for Exodus and Deuteronomy in Swete Vol. I; primary witness for Exodus 32:6 (p. 169), Deuteronomy 21:23 (p. 386), and Deuteronomy 25:4 (p. 392).

2. **Codex Alexandrinus (Codex A, Royal MS 1 D. V–VIII)**
   - *British Museum*, London.
   - Photographic autotype edition: *The Codex Alexandrinus in Reduced Photographic Facsimile* (British Museum, 1879–1883).
   - Core running text for Genesis in Swete Vol. I (supplying Gen 2:24, p. 4, and Gen 15:6, p. 25 during the Vaticanus lacuna); primary collated witness for Exodus and Deuteronomy.

3. **Codex Cottonianus Geneseos (Codex D, Cotton MS Otho B. VI)**
   - *British Library*, London.
   - Collated in Genesis by Tischendorf (*Monumenta Sacra Inedita*, nova coll. vol. ii) and re-collated by Swete.
   - 5th/6th-century illustrated uncial fragments of Genesis; collated at Genesis 2:24 (p. 4) and Genesis 15:6 (p. 25).

4. **Codex Bodleianus Geneseos (Codex E, MS Auct. T. infr. ii. 1)**
   - *Bodleian Library*, Oxford.
   - 8th-century uncial manuscript acquired by Tischendorf in 1853; collated for Genesis 2:24 (p. 4). Defective between Genesis 14:7 and 18:24 (hence absent at Gen 15:6).

5. **Codex Ambrosianus (Codex F, MS A. 147 inf.)**
   - *Biblioteca Ambrosiana*, Milan.
   - 5th-century uncial manuscript of the Octateuch edited by Ceriani (*Monumenta Sacra et Profana*, tom. iii, Milan, 1864).
   - Major collation witness for Exodus 32:6 (mutilated in part), Deuteronomy 21:23 (p. 386), and Deuteronomy 25:4 (p. 392); unique early uncial witness preserving accents and breathings prima manu.

6. **Paul Anton de Lagarde, *Genesis graece* (Leipzig, 1868)**
   - Cited by Swete in the Preface (p. vi) regarding the relationship between the Aldine text and Codex Alexandrinus in Genesis.

7. **Constantin von Tischendorf, *Vetus Testamentum Graece iuxta LXX Interpretes* (6th ed., Leipzig, 1880)**
   - Predecessor manual edition compared and critically evaluated by Swete in establishing the Cambridge manual text.

8. **Brooke, Alan England, and Norman McLean, *The Old Testament in Greek* (Cambridge, 1906–1940)**
   - The larger Cambridge critical edition of the Septuagint, planned alongside Swete's manual edition to incorporate full minuscules, versions, and patristic citations for Genesis through Esther.
