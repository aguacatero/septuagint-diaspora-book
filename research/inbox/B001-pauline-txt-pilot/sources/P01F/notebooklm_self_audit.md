## Itemized Corrections

### 1. Diacritical and Lemma Marker Precision: 1 Corinthians 6:16 (P01F-P009)
- **Previous Transcription:** `16 ἦ WH Treg NIV ] – RP`
- **Correction:** The apparatus on page 344 reads `16 ⸀ἢ WH Treg NIV ] – RP`. The Greek disjunctive particle has a smooth breathing and grave accent (`ἢ`), not a circumflex (`ἦ`). The leading single-word variation marker `⸀` has been restored in all ledger and markdown records.
