# P01F — Variants and Discriminating Features (Primary Counterevidence)

**Source ID:** P01F  
**Source Title:** *The Greek New Testament: SBL Edition*  
**Editor:** Michael W. Holmes (2010)  
**Source Class:** PRIMARY (Greek New Testament critical edition)  

---

## 1. General Textual and Methodological Policies

The front matter of the SBLGNT details several governing editorial policies that shape the constitution of the text and apparatus:

- **Editorial Baseline & Collation:** The baseline text was Westcott and Hort (WH, 1881), collated systematically against Samuel Tregelles (Treg, 1857–1879), Goodrich–Lukaszewski's NIV Greek text (NIV, 2003), and Robinson–Pierpont's Byzantine textform (RP, 2005). In 56 variation units, Holmes departed from all four primary reference editions. The edition departs from the modern standard text (NA/UBS) in more than 540 variation units.
- **Orthography & Lexical Normalization:** Both the text and the apparatus are systematically conformed to the orthography of BDAG (3rd ed.), overriding spelling discrepancies in the collated editions. Orthographical differences (movable consonants, vowel interchanges) are excluded from the apparatus.
- **Breathings on Pronouns:** Treated as interpretive/exegetical decisions. Following a minority view of the UBS Editorial Committee (Philippians 3:21), the SBLGNT occasionally prints rough breathings on forms of αὐτός where reflexive sense is intended.
- **Punctuation & Capitalization:** Punctuation generally adheres to WH, with consecutive marks reduced to a single mark. A high point (`·`) is systematically added before direct speech where no other mark occurs. Direct speech is capitalized.
- **Restraint on Brackets:** Single square brackets (`[ ]`) for doubtful text are strictly limited to six instances across the New Testament (Luke 22:19–20; 24:40; 24:51; 24:52; Eph 1:1; Col 1:20) to prevent evading difficult editorial decisions and biasing text toward inclusion. Double brackets (`〚WH〛`) indicate ancient non-original material preserved by WH.

## 2. Textual Features and Variants for the 12 B001 Passages

### 1. Romans 4:3 (Genesis 15:6)
- **Page / Locator:** Page 317
- **Printed Text:** `τί γὰρ ἡ γραφὴ λέγει; Ἐπίστευσεν δὲ Ἀβραὰμ τῷ θεῷ καὶ ἐλογίσθη αὐτῷ εἰς δικαιοσύνην.`
- **Apparatus Entry:** `NONE IN THIS SOURCE` (no variation unit occurs at verse 3).
- **Discriminating Textual Features:**
  - *Formula & Punctuation:* Opens with rhetorical question closed by Greek question mark (`;`), followed by direct speech capitalized (`Ἐπίστευσεν`).
  - *Syntax:* Integrates postpositive conjunction `δὲ` directly into the citation clause (`Ἐπίστευσεν δὲ Ἀβραὰμ...`).

### 2. Romans 9:25 (Hosea 2:23)
- **Page / Locator:** Page 326
- **Printed Text:** `ὡς καὶ ἐν τῷ Ὡσηὲ λέγει· Καλέσω τὸν οὐ λαόν μου λαόν μου καὶ τὴν οὐκ ἠγαπημένην ἠγαπημένην·`
- **Apparatus Entry:** `NONE IN THIS SOURCE` (no variation unit occurs at verse 25).
- **Discriminating Textual Features:**
  - *Formula & Punctuation:* Prophetic attribution formula `ὡς καὶ ἐν τῷ Ὡσηὲ λέγει·` terminated by high point (`·`); direct speech capitalized (`Καλέσω`).
  - *Orthography:* Prophet's name accented and aspirated as `Ὡσηὲ`.
  - *Syntax & Lexis:* Paired accusative phrases where negative particles `οὐ` and `οὐκ` function as proclitic modifiers modifying nominal and participial designations (`τὸν οὐ λαόν μου`, `τὴν οὐκ ἠγαπημένην`), followed by predicate complements (`λαόν μου`, `ἠγαπημένην`).

### 3. Romans 9:26 (Hosea 1:10)
- **Page / Locator:** Page 326
- **Printed Text:** `καὶ ἔσται ἐν τῷ τόπῳ οὗ ἐρρέθη ⸀αὐτοῖς· Οὐ λαός μου ὑμεῖς, ἐκεῖ κληθήσονται υἱοὶ θεοῦ ζῶντος.`
- **Apparatus Entry:** `26 αὐτοῖς WH NIV RP ] – Treg`
- **Discriminating Textual Features:**
  - *Variant:* Textual variation unit on the indirect object pronoun `⸀αὐτοῖς` in the introductory clause; Holmes prints `αὐτοῖς` with WH, NIV, and RP, whereas Tregelles omits it (`– Treg`).
  - *Formula & Punctuation:* Passive construction `ἐν τῷ τόπῳ οὗ ἐρρέθη ⸀αὐτοῖς·` ending with high point (`·`); direct speech capitalized (`Οὐ`).
  - *Syntax:* Quotation proper is unanimous across all reference editions; relative locative phrase `ἐν τῷ τόπῳ οὗ` is resumed demonstratively by `ἐκεῖ` inside the quotation.

### 4. Romans 10:11 (Isaiah 28:16)
- **Page / Locator:** Page 327
- **Printed Text:** `λέγει γὰρ ἡ γραφή· Πᾶς ὁ πιστεύων ἐπʼ αὐτῷ οὐ καταισχυνθήσεται.`
- **Apparatus Entry:** `NONE IN THIS SOURCE` (no variation unit occurs at verse 11).
- **Discriminating Textual Features:**
  - *Formula & Punctuation:* Scriptural attribution formula `λέγει γὰρ ἡ γραφή·` terminated by high point (`·`); direct speech capitalized (`Πᾶς`).
  - *Orthography:* Elision with apostrophe before rough breathing pronoun (`ἐπʼ αὐτῷ`).
  - *Syntax & Lexis:* Universalizing adjective `Πᾶς` modifying participle `ὁ πιστεύων`.

### 5. Romans 10:13 (Joel 2:32)
- **Page / Locator:** Page 327
- **Printed Text:** `Πᾶς γὰρ ὃς ἂν ἐπικαλέσηται τὸ ὄνομα κυρίου σωθήσεται.`
- **Apparatus Entry:** `NONE IN THIS SOURCE` (no variation unit occurs at verse 13).
- **Discriminating Textual Features:**
  - *Formula & Syntax:* Integrates quotation without introductory verb via postpositive explanatory conjunction `γάρ` (`Πᾶς γὰρ ὃς ἂν...`).
  - *Syntax:* Indefinite relative construction `ὃς ἂν` governing aorist middle subjunctive (`ἐπικαλέσηται`), followed by anarthrous genitive `κυρίου` in `τὸ ὄνομα κυρίου`.

### 6. Romans 11:34 (Isaiah 40:13)
- **Page / Locator:** Page 329
- **Printed Text:** `Τίς γὰρ ἔγνω νοῦν κυρίου; ἢ τίς σύμβουλος αὐτοῦ ἐγένετο;`
- **Apparatus Entry:** `NONE IN THIS SOURCE` (no variation unit occurs at verse 34).
- **Discriminating Textual Features:**
  - *Formula & Syntax:* Seamlessly integrated via postpositive conjunction `γάρ` into doxology without citation formula.
  - *Punctuation:* Formatted as two coordinate rhetorical questions, each closed by Greek question mark (`;`).
  - *Lexis:* Noun phrase `νοῦν κυρίου` (accusative `νοῦν` rather than `πνεῦμα`).

### 7. Romans 15:12 (Isaiah 11:10)
- **Page / Locator:** Page 334
- **Printed Text:** `καὶ πάλιν Ἠσαΐας λέγει· Ἔσται ἡ ῥίζα τοῦ Ἰεσσαί, καὶ ὁ ἀνιστάμενος ἄρχειν ἐθνῶν· ἐπʼ αὐτῷ ἔθνη ἐλπιοῦσιν.`
- **Apparatus Entry:** `NONE IN THIS SOURCE` (no variation unit occurs at verse 12).
- **Discriminating Textual Features:**
  - *Formula & Punctuation:* Iterative attribution formula `καὶ πάλιν Ἠσαΐας λέγει·` closed with high point (`·`); direct speech capitalized (`Ἔσται`).
  - *Orthography:* Elision with apostrophe in `ἐπʼ αὐτῷ`; movable nu printed on future verb `ἐλπιοῦσιν`.
  - *Syntax:* Tripartite quotation: future existential clause (`Ἔσται ἡ ῥίζα τοῦ Ἰεσσαί`), substantival participial phrase with infinitive complement (`καὶ ὁ ἀνιστάμενος ἄρχειν ἐθνῶν·`), and independent future clause (`ἐπʼ αὐτῷ ἔθνη ἐλπιοῦσιν`).

### 8. 1 Corinthians 1:19 (Isaiah 29:14)
- **Page / Locator:** Page 339
- **Printed Text:** `γέγραπται γάρ· Ἀπολῶ τὴν σοφίαν τῶν σοφῶν, καὶ τὴν σύνεσιν τῶν συνετῶν ἀθετήσω.`
- **Apparatus Entry:** `NONE IN THIS SOURCE` (no variation unit occurs at verse 19).
- **Discriminating Textual Features:**
  - *Formula & Punctuation:* Formula `γέγραπται γάρ·` followed by high point (`·`); direct speech capitalized (`Ἀπολῶ`).
  - *Syntax & Lexis:* Coordinate parallel clauses conjoined by `καί`, balanced with first-person singular future active verbs: contract future `Ἀπολῶ` and liquid future `ἀθετήσω`.

### 9. 1 Corinthians 6:16 (Genesis 2:24)
- **Page / Locator:** Page 344
- **Printed Text:** `⸀ἢ οὐκ οἴδατε ὅτι ὁ κολλώμενος τῇ πόρνῃ ἓν σῶμά ἐστιν; Ἔσονται γάρ, φησίν, οἱ δύο εἰς σάρκα μίαν.`
- **Apparatus Entry:** `16 ⸀ἢ WH Treg NIV ] – RP`
- **Discriminating Textual Features:**
  - *Variant:* Textual variation unit on verse-initial disjunctive particle: Holmes prints `ἢ` with WH, Tregelles, and NIV; Robinson–Pierpont (RP Byzantine textform) omits it (`– RP`).
  - *Formula & Punctuation:* Parenthetical attribution verb `φησίν` between coordinating conjunction `γάρ` and subject `οἱ δύο` (`Ἔσονται γάρ, φησίν, οἱ δύο...`). Direct speech capitalized (`Ἔσονται`).
  - *Syntax:* Verbatim consensus on citation text proper (`οἱ δύο εἰς σάρκα μίαν`).

### 10. 1 Corinthians 9:9 (Deuteronomy 25:4)
- **Page / Locator:** Page 349
- **Printed Text:** `ἐν γὰρ τῷ Μωϋσέως νόμῳ γέγραπται· Οὐ ⸀κημώσεις βοῦν ἀλοῶντα. μὴ τῶν βοῶν μέλει τῷ θεῷ,`
- **Apparatus Entry:** `9 κημώσεις Treg NIV ] φιμώσεις WH RP`
- **Discriminating Textual Features:**
  - *Variant & Lexis:* Significant lexical variation unit in verb of prohibition: Holmes selects `κημώσεις` with Tregelles and NIV, rejecting `φιμώσεις` read by Westcott–Hort and Byzantine textform (RP).
  - *Formula & Punctuation:* Legal attribution `ἐν γὰρ τῷ Μωϋσέως νόμῳ γέγραπται·` ending with high point (`·`); direct quotation capitalized (`Οὐ`). Prohibition clause closed with full stop (`.`), separating quotation from ensuing rhetorical question.

### 11. 1 Corinthians 10:7 (Exodus 32:6)
- **Page / Locator:** Page 350
- **Printed Text:** `μηδὲ εἰδωλολάτραι γίνεσθε, καθώς τινες αὐτῶν· ὥσπερ γέγραπται· Ἐκάθισεν ὁ λαὸς φαγεῖν καὶ πεῖν, καὶ ἀνέστησαν παίζειν.`
- **Apparatus Entry:** `NONE IN THIS SOURCE` (no variation unit occurs at verse 7).
- **Discriminating Textual Features:**
  - *Formula & Punctuation:* Comparative introductory formula `ὥσπερ γέγραπται·` marked by high point (`·`); direct speech capitalized (`Ἐκάθισεν`).
  - *Orthography:* Contracted aorist active infinitive form `πεῖν` (rather than `πιεῖν`).
  - *Syntax:* Narrative quotation shifting from singular verb with collective noun (`Ἐκάθισεν ὁ λαός`) governing coordinate infinitives (`φαγεῖν καὶ πεῖν`) to plural verb (`ἀνέστησαν`) governing infinitive (`παίζειν`).

### 12. Galatians 3:13 (Deuteronomy 21:23)
- **Page / Locator:** Page 382
- **Printed Text:** `Χριστὸς ἡμᾶς ἐξηγόρασεν ἐκ τῆς κατάρας τοῦ νόμου γενόμενος ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν κατάρα, ⸂ὅτι γέγραπται⸃· Ἐπικατάρατος πᾶς ὁ κρεμάμενος ἐπὶ ξύλου,`
- **Apparatus Entry:** `13 ὅτι γέγραπται WH Treg NIV ] γέγραπται γάρ RP`
- **Discriminating Textual Features:**
  - *Variant:* Multiword variation unit `⸂ὅτι γέγραπται⸃` in introductory formula: Holmes, WH, Tregelles, and NIV read causal `ὅτι γέγραπται`; Robinson–Pierpont (RP) reads `γέγραπται γάρ`.
  - *Punctuation:* High point (`·`) after formula; direct quotation capitalized (`Ἐπικατάρατος`).
  - *Syntax & Lexis:* Verbatim quotation clause (`Ἐπικατάρατος πᾶς ὁ κρεμάμενος ἐπὶ ξύλου`) is unanimous across all collated editions; features nominal curse construction with `πᾶς` modifying `ὁ κρεμάμενος`, without agency phrase (`ὑπὸ θεοῦ`).
