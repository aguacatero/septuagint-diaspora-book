## Overview

Because P01F is a critical edition of the Greek New Testament text rather than an exegetical commentary, all twelve Pauline passages in the B001 corpus are `TEXT_PRESENT`. The printed Greek text and apparatus entries constitute the primary textual evidence for the New Testament side of the B001 comparisons.
