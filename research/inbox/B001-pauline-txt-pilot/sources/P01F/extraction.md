## Methodological Finding Regarding B001 Corpus

As established in `relevance_scan.md`, P01F is a primary critical edition of the Greek New Testament. It prints the critically established Greek text of the New Testament with a comparative apparatus documenting variant readings among four major editions:
- Westcott–Hort (WH)
- Tregelles (Treg)
- NIV Greek Text (NIV)
- Robinson–Pierpont Byzantine Textform (RP)

P01F does **not** contain an expository commentary or secondary discussion of Old Testament quotations. The 12 assigned B001 Pauline quotation comparisons are printed in the Greek text at the following locators:
- **Romans 4:3** (p. 317)
- **Romans 9:25–26** (p. 326)
- **Romans 10:11, 13** (p. 327)
- **Romans 11:34** (p. 329)
- **Romans 15:12** (p. 334)
- **1 Corinthians 1:19** (p. 339)
- **1 Corinthians 6:16** (p. 344)
- **1 Corinthians 9:9** (p. 349)
- **1 Corinthians 10:7** (p. 350)
- **Galatians 3:13** (p. 382)

In accordance with project guidelines for primary critical editions, the extracted evidence atoms capture the editor's explicit methodological and textual-critical claims governing the establishment, apparatus, and readings of the Pauline text.

## Extracted Evidence Atoms

### P01F-E001: Baseline Text and Collation Method
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `P01F-E001`
- **Passage / Scope:** General Pauline Corpus / NT Text
- **Claim:** The text of the edition was established by using Westcott and Hort as a modified baseline and collating it against three other primary critical editions to identify variant readings.
- **Short Quotation:** *"The starting point for the SBLGNT was the edition of Westcott and Hort."*
- **Printed Page:** Page xi
- **Source Locator:** Introduction, Section 2 ("Methodology")
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [2]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Editions of Westcott–Hort, Tregelles, NIV, and Robinson–Pierpont.
- **Claim Type:** Methodological
- **Qualifications:** Westcott and Hort's text was adjusted to match SBLGNT orthographic standards before systematic collation.
- **Confidence:** HIGH

### P01F-E002: Editorial Target as Earliest Circulating Textform
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `P01F-E002`
- **Passage / Scope:** General Pauline Corpus / NT Text
- **Claim:** The editor defines the target text of the edition as the variant form that most likely reflects the earliest circulating and copied archetype.
- **Short Quotation:** *"...which variant most likely represents the form in which the text first began to be copied and to circulate."*
- **Printed Page:** Page xi (n. 8)
- **Source Locator:** Introduction, p. xi, n. 8
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [8]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Comparative readings across early manuscript witnesses and critical editions.
- **Claim Type:** Methodological / Textual-critical
- **Qualifications:** Explicitly precision of what it means to determine which variant to print.
- **Confidence:** HIGH

### P01F-E003: Restricted Use of Brackets in Pauline Corpus
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `P01F-E003`
- **Passage / Scope:** Ephesians 1:1; Colossians 1:20
- **Claim:** Single square brackets indicating doubtful text are restricted to six instances across the entire New Testament, including two in the Pauline epistles (Ephesians 1:1 and Colossians 1:20), to avoid biasing editorial decisions toward inclusion.
- **Short Quotation:** *"...single brackets appear only six times in the SBLGNT (at Luke 22:19–20; 24:40; 24:51; 24:52; Eph 1:1; Col 1:20)."*
- **Printed Page:** Page xv (n. 16)
- **Source Locator:** Introduction, p. xv, n. 16
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [9]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Disputed wording in Eph 1:1 ([ἐν Ἐφέσῳ]) and Col 1:20 ([διʼ αὐτοῦ]).
- **Claim Type:** Methodological / Textual-critical
- **Qualifications:** Sparingly used to fulfill editorial duty of deciding difficult variants rather than deferring.
- **Confidence:** HIGH

### P01F-E004: Interpretive Breathing Marks on Pronouns in Paul
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `P01F-E004`
- **Passage / Scope:** Philippians 3:21 (Pauline Epistles)
- **Claim:** Breathing marks can represent interpretive decisions, leading the editor to adopt rough breathings on forms of αὐτός in select Pauline contexts following a minority UBS committee view.
- **Short Quotation:** *"...the SBLGNT occasionally prints a rough breathing on forms of αὐτός."*
- **Printed Page:** Page xii (n. 10)
- **Source Locator:** Introduction, p. xii, n. 10
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [12]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Metzger's textual commentary on Philippians 3:21.
- **Claim Type:** Textual-critical / Exegetical
- **Qualifications:** Limited to select instances where breathing conveys a reflexive sense.
- **Confidence:** HIGH

### P01F-E005: Asyndetic Reading in 1 Corinthians 12:10
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `P01F-E005`
- **Passage / Scope:** 1 Corinthians 12:10
- **Claim:** In 1 Corinthians 12:10, the editor rejects the connective conjunction δὲ across clauses, adopting an asyndetic reading not found in any of the four primary reference editions at units 1 and 5.
- **Short Quotation:** *"10 ⸀ἄλλῳ Holmes ] + δὲ WH Treg NIV RP"*
- **Printed Page:** Page xxi (apparatus entry); Page 354
- **Source Locator:** Introduction, p. xxi, Apparatus; text p. 354
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [13, 14]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** 1 Cor 12:10 variation units where Holmes omits δὲ against WH, Treg, NIV, and RP at units 1 and 5 (Treg also omits at units 2 and 3).
- **Claim Type:** Textual-critical
- **Qualifications:** One of 56 variation units where the editor selected a reading outside the four primary editions; uniquely departs from all four at units 1 and 5.
- **Confidence:** HIGH

### P01F-E006: Reduction of Consecutive Punctuation Marks in Pastoral Epistles
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `P01F-E006`
- **Passage / Scope:** 1 Timothy 1:5; 2:7
- **Claim:** Where Westcott and Hort printed consecutive punctuation marks (such as a comma beside a dash in the Pastoral Epistles), the editor systematically reduced them to a single mark.
- **Short Quotation:** *"Where Westcott and Hort employed two consecutive punctuation marks... these have been reduced to a single mark."*
- **Printed Page:** Page xiv
- **Source Locator:** Introduction, p. xiv
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [15]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Punctuation in 1 Tim 1:5 and 1 Tim 2:7.
- **Claim Type:** Methodological
- **Qualifications:** Punctuation otherwise adheres generally to WH.
- **Confidence:** HIGH

### P01F-E007: Case-by-Case Punctuation and Paragraphing in Paul
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `P01F-E007`
- **Passage / Scope:** Philippians 1:18; 2:14
- **Claim:** Where modern paragraph breaks (NRSV) conflict with historical punctuation (WH) in Paul's letters, decisions are made on a contextual basis, preferring WH punctuation at Phil 1:18 but NRSV paragraphing at Phil 2:14.
- **Short Quotation:** *"...at the end of Phil 1:18, WH’s punctuation was given preference over the NRSV paragraph break..."*
- **Printed Page:** Page xiv (n. 15)
- **Source Locator:** Introduction, p. xiv, n. 15
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [16, 17]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Syntax and breaks at Phil 1:18 and Phil 2:14.
- **Claim Type:** Methodological
- **Qualifications:** Resolved contextually rather than by absolute rule.
- **Confidence:** HIGH

### P01F-E008: Orthographic Departure in Colossians 2:16
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `P01F-E008`
- **Passage / Scope:** Colossians 2:16
- **Claim:** While orthography generally conforms to the BDAG lexicon, the editor departs from BDAG in Colossians 2:16 by printing νουμηνίας instead of νεομηνίας.
- **Short Quotation:** *"A rare exception to the guideline is the adoption of νουμηνίας rather than νεομηνίας in Col 2:16."*
- **Printed Page:** Page xiii (n. 11)
- **Source Locator:** Introduction, p. xiii, n. 11
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [18, 19]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Spelling variation unit at Col 2:16.
- **Claim Type:** Methodological / Textual-critical
- **Qualifications:** Explicitly noted as an unusual exception to the general standard.
- **Confidence:** HIGH
