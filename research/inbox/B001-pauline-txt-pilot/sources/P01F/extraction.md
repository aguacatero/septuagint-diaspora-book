### P01F-P001: Romans 4:3 (Genesis 15:6)
- **B001 ID:** `B001-1`
- **Witness Role:** `NT_GREEK`
- **Passage Reference:** Romans 4:3
- **Exact Printed Text:** `τί γὰρ ἡ γραφὴ λέγει; Ἐπίστευσεν δὲ Ἀβραὰμ τῷ θεῷ καὶ ἐλογίσθη αὐτῷ εἰς δικαιοσύνην.`
- **Printed Page / Locator:** Page 317
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [1]

## Part 2: Ancillary Editorial-Method Evidence Atoms (`P01F-E001` to `P01F-E008`)

These items capture Michael W. Holmes's explicit editorial method, collation standards, and textual-critical procedures documented in the front matter and apparatus notes.

### P01F-E001: Westcott and Hort as Initial Collation Baseline
- **Passage / Scope:** Whole edition / NT Text
- **Claim:** The edition established its text using Westcott and Hort as the modified baseline collated against Tregelles, NIV, and Robinson–Pierpont.
- **Printed Page:** Page xi

### P01F-E002: Definition of the Target Text as Earliest Circulating Archetype
- **Passage / Scope:** Whole edition / Methodological Definition
- **Claim:** The editor defines the goal of textual criticism as identifying the form of text that "most likely represents the form in which the text first began to be copied and to circulate."
- **Printed Page:** Page xi (n. 8)

### P01F-E003: Restriction of Single Square Brackets for Doubtful Text
- **Passage / Scope:** Pauline Epistles (Eph 1:1; Col 1:20) and Luke
- **Claim:** Single square brackets are restricted to six instances in the New Testament to avoid evading difficult editorial decisions and biasing text toward inclusion.
- **Printed Page:** Page xv (n. 16)

### P01F-E004: Interpretive Breathing Marks on Pronouns in Paul
- **Passage / Scope:** Philippians 3:21 (Pauline Epistles)
- **Claim:** Breathing marks can represent interpretive decisions, leading the editor to print rough breathings on forms of αὐτός where reflexive meaning is intended, following a minority UBS committee view.
- **Printed Page:** Page xii (n. 10)

### P01F-E005: Asyndetic Reading in 1 Corinthians 12:10
- **Passage / Scope:** 1 Corinthians 12:10
- **Claim:** In 1 Cor 12:10, the editor rejects the connective conjunction δὲ across clauses, adopting an asyndetic reading not found in any of the four primary reference editions at units 1 and 5.
- **Printed Page:** Page xxi (apparatus entry); Page 354

### P01F-E006: Reduction of Consecutive Punctuation Marks in Pastoral Epistles
- **Passage / Scope:** 1 Timothy 1:5; 2:7
- **Claim:** Where Westcott and Hort printed consecutive punctuation marks in the Pastoral Epistles, the editor systematically reduced them to a single mark.
- **Printed Page:** Page xiv

### P01F-E007: Paragraphing vs. Punctuation Contextual Decisions in Philippians
- **Passage / Scope:** Philippians 1:18; 2:14
- **Claim:** Where modern paragraph breaks conflict with WH punctuation, decisions are made on a case-by-case contextual basis (favoring WH punctuation at Phil 1:18, but the modern paragraph break at Phil 2:14).
- **Printed Page:** Page xiv (n. 15)

### P01F-E008: Orthographical Departure from BDAG in Colossians 2:16
- **Passage / Scope:** Colossians 2:16
- **Claim:** The editor departs from the BDAG orthographic guideline in Col 2:16, printing `νουμηνίας` rather than `νεομηνίας`.
- **Printed Page:** Page xiii (n. 11)
