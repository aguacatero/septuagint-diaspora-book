1. B001-12: Galatians 3:13 / Deuteronomy 21:23
Reasoning Map
QUESTION: How does Paul understand and apply the curse formula of Deuteronomy 21:23 (קללת אלהים / κεκατηραμένος ὑπὸ τοῦ Θεοῦ) to Christ's crucifixion, and why does he omit ὑπὸ Θεοῦ in Galatians 3:13

In Deuteronomy 21:23, the Hebrew construct phrase קללת אלהים is grammatically ambiguous: the case attached to קללת can represent either a subjective genitive ("accursed of God") or an objective genitive ("an insult/reproach to God")

Structural Paraphrase: Paul adopts the substance of the Septuagint rendering (that a hanging body is accursed), but instinctively omits ὑπὸ Θεοῦ because Christ was in no literal sense accursed by God, and including those words would have required theological qualification
