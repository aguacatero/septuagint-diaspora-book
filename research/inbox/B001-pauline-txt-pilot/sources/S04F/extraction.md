B001-12: Galatians 3:13 / Deuteronomy 21:23
S04F-E001
Proposed Evidence ID: S04F-E001
B001 Passage ID: B001-12
Author's Claim: The Hebrew construct phrase קללת אלהים in Deuteronomy 21:23 contains a grammatical ambiguity, allowing אלהים to be rendered either as a subjective genitive ("accursed of God") or an objective genitive ("an insult/reproach to God").
Supporting Quotation: "The ambiguity arises out of the construction of אלהים, since the case attached to קללת may denote either the person who pronounces the curse... or the person against whom the curse is pronounced"
