# S07F — Relevance Scan

Based on Pieter K. Baaij's study, *Paulus als Schriftuitlegger: Paulus’ vertolking van Genesis 15:5b–6 in Romeinen 4:3* (2005), the article is an in-depth exegetical investigation devoted exclusively to Paul's quotation and interpretation of Genesis 15:5b–6 in Romans 4:3 (B001-1). The author provides a detailed grammatical, syntactic, and theological analysis comparing Biblical Hebrew (MT), Septuagint Greek (LXX), and Pauline Greek. In passing, the author notes Paul's use of πιστεύω ἐπί with dative in Romans 10:11 / 9:33 (B001-4) in contrast to the simple dative in Romans 4:3. The remaining 10 B001 comparisons are confirmed absent across all 16 pages of the volume.
