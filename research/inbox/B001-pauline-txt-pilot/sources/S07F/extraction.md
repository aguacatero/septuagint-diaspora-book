# S07F — Source-Bounded Scholarly Extraction

Itemized extraction of Pieter K. Baaij's positions and claims across the substantive B001 items (and contextual comparative units) in *Paulus als Schriftuitlegger: Paulus’ vertolking van Genesis 15:5b–6 in Romeinen 4:3*, *In die Skriflig*, Vol. 39, No. 2 (2005), pp. 239–254.

---

### B001-1: Romans 4:3 / Genesis 15:6

#### S07F-E001
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `S07F-E001`
- **B001 Passage ID:** `B001-1`
- **Claim Type:** `HISTORICAL_METHODOLOGICAL`
- **Author's Claim:** Paul constructed his Greek text in Romans 4:3 directly by translating from his own prior consideration of the Hebrew text of Genesis 15:5b–6 rather than passively reproducing the Septuagint.
- **Short Exact Quotation:** "Paulus zijn tekst eerst in het Bijbels-Hebreeuws heeft overdacht"
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 239 [PDF p. 1]; Section 1
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [1, 2]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Grammatical and syntactic comparison between Romans 4:3, Genesis 15:5b–6 MT (*'āman*, *ḥāšaḇ*, *ṣəḏāqāh*), and LXX Genesis 15:6 (*καὶ ἐπίστευσεν Ἀβρὰμ τῷ θεῷ...*).
- **Qualifications / Conditions:** Discovered during the author's dedicated exegesis and translation of Romans conducted continuously since 1986.
- **Extraction Confidence:** `HIGH`
- **Notes:** Baaij 2005:239. Establishes the foundational thesis of Paul as an accurate expositor and retro-translator of Hebrew Scripture.

#### S07F-E002
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `S07F-E002`
- **B001 Passage ID:** `B001-1`
- **Claim Type:** `METHODOLOGICAL_EXEGETICAL`
- **Author's Claim:** Paul functioned as an expositor of Scripture par excellence whose interpretive instruments lead to a clear understanding of both Paul's preaching and the Scriptures themselves.
- **Short Exact Quotation:** "de echte Paulus de schriftuitlegger is bij uitstek"
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 240 [PDF p. 2]; Section 1
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [1, 3]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Hermeneutical framework of Romans demonstrating how Paul's gospel unveils the culmination of the Torah (Rom 10:4) rather than contradicting Hebrew Scripture.
- **Qualifications / Conditions:** Requires modern readers to utilize the linguistic and exegetical instruments Paul himself provides rather than forcing Hellenistic frameworks onto his letters.
- **Extraction Confidence:** `HIGH`
- **Notes:** Baaij 2005:240. Key characterization of Paul as *schriftuitlegger bij uitstek*.

#### S07F-E003
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `S07F-E003`
- **B001 Passage ID:** `B001-1`
- **Claim Type:** `TEXTUAL_CRITICAL_HISTORICAL`
- **Author's Claim:** The grammatical and theological analysis explicitly assumes that the Hebrew text known to Paul did not deviate from the Masoretic Text available today.
- **Short Exact Quotation:** "de Hebreeuwse tekst die Paulus heeft gekend, niet heeft afgeweken van de Hebreeuwse tekst die ons ter beschikking staat, namelijk de Masoretische tekst"
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 243 [PDF p. 5]; Section 1
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [4]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Hebrew consonantal and Masoretic text of Genesis 15:5b–6 (BHS).
- **Qualifications / Conditions:** Explicit working assumption acknowledged by the author; would be falsified if Paul's Hebrew Vorlage diverged significantly from the MT.
- **Extraction Confidence:** `HIGH`
- **Notes:** Baaij 2005:243. Core textual premise of the study.

#### S07F-E004
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `S07F-E004`
- **B001 Passage ID:** `B001-1`
- **Claim Type:** `EXEGETICAL_THEOLOGICAL`
- **Author's Claim:** Paul's translation of *ṣəḏāqāh* by *dikaiosynē* in Romans 4:3 expresses the restoration of the covenant relationship between God and the addressed human being.
- **Short Exact Quotation:** "wil dus zoveel zeggen als \"herstel van de relatie tussen God en de door Hem aangesproken mens\""
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 247 [PDF p. 9]; Section 3
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [9]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Hebrew *ṣəḏāqāh* (צְדָקָה) vs. *ṣædæq*, evaluated through B. Johnson (ThWAT VI, 916) and J. van Dorp (2004).
- **Qualifications / Conditions:** Righteousness is not a static human possession or forensic merit, but God's relational restoration granted in response to human trust.
- **Extraction Confidence:** `HIGH`
- **Notes:** Baaij 2005:247. Defining *ṣəḏāqāh* in Rom 4:3 and Gen 15:6.

#### S07F-E005
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `S07F-E005`
- **B001 Passage ID:** `B001-1`
- **Claim Type:** `EXEGETICAL_GRAMMATICAL`
- **Author's Claim:** Paul uses postpositive δέ in Romans 4:3 to grant extra grammatical emphasis to the preceding verb *episteusen*, highlighting active trust.
- **Short Exact Quotation:** "om het voorafgaande [werk]woord extra nadruk te verlenen"
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 248 [PDF p. 10]; Section 3
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [5]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Greek syntax of Romans 4:3 (*ἐπίστευσεν δὲ Ἀβραὰμ τῷ θεῷ*), where postpositive δέ attaches emphasis to the initial verb rather than placing the subject first.
- **Qualifications / Conditions:** Contrasts with existing Dutch and modern translations (Statenvertaling, NBG 1951, Willibrord 1995, NBV 2004) that put "Abram/Abraham" first.
- **Extraction Confidence:** `HIGH`
- **Notes:** Baaij 2005:248. Function of postpositive δέ in Rom 4:3.

#### S07F-E006
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `S07F-E006`
- **B001 Passage ID:** `B001-1`
- **Claim Type:** `EXEGETICAL_TEXTUAL`
- **Author's Claim:** Paul intentionally replaces the Septuagint's pre-covenant name Abram with Abraham, designating the covenant partner who responds to God.
- **Short Exact Quotation:** "Niet dus \"Abram\" (LXX), maar Abraham, de God antwoordende mens"
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 248 [PDF p. 10]; Section 3
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [7]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Romans 4:3 (*Ἀβραάμ*) vs. LXX Genesis 15:6 (*Ἀβράμ*).
- **Qualifications / Conditions:** Abraham is explicitly viewed as the covenant partner who answers God's promise, anticipating the discussion of Genesis 17:5 in Romans 4:11–18.
- **Extraction Confidence:** `HIGH`
- **Notes:** Baaij 2005:248. Contrast between LXX *Ἀβράμ* and Pauline *Ἀβραάμ*.

#### S07F-E007
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `S07F-E007`
- **B001 Passage ID:** `B001-4`
- **Claim Type:** `GRAMMATICAL_COMPARATIVE`
- **Author's Claim:** In contrast to Romans 9:33 and 10:11 where Paul constructs *pisteuō* with *epi* and dative, in Romans 4:3 he uses a simple dative without preposition.
- **Short Exact Quotation:** "Paulus gebruikt immers niet pi,steuw evpi, cum dativo (zoals in Rom. 9:33; 10:11)"
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 248 [PDF p. 10]; Section 3
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [7]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Grammatical comparison between *πιστεύω τῷ θεῷ* (Rom 4:3) and *πιστεύω ἐπ' αὐτῷ* (Rom 9:33; 10:11 quoting Isa 28:16).
- **Qualifications / Conditions:** Demonstrates deliberate stylistic and prepositional variation across Romans to convey different nuances of faith.
- **Extraction Confidence:** `HIGH`
- **Notes:** Baaij 2005:248. Explicit comparative link to B001-4 (Romans 10:11 / Isaiah 28:16).

#### S07F-E008
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `S07F-E008`
- **B001 Passage ID:** `B001-1`
- **Claim Type:** `EXEGETICAL_GRAMMATICAL`
- **Author's Claim:** By constructing *pisteuō* with a simple dative lacking prepositions *epi* or *eis*, Paul places grammatical emphasis on God as the divine Cause and Initiator.
- **Short Exact Quotation:** "Met pi,steuw cum dativo (zonder prepositie dus) legt Paulus de nadruk op de Veroorzaker"
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 248 [PDF p. 10]; Section 3
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [7, 8]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** *πιστεύω τῷ θεῷ* in Romans 4:3 and Romans 10:16 functioning as a *dativus causae*.
- **Qualifications / Conditions:** Faith is initiated by God's promise and actively accepted by humanity; *pisteuō* renders the inner-transitive hif'il of Hebrew *'āman*.
- **Extraction Confidence:** `HIGH`
- **Notes:** Baaij 2005:248. Dativus causae interpretation of *pisteuō* in Rom 4:3.

#### S07F-E009
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `S07F-E009`
- **B001 Passage ID:** `B001-1`
- **Claim Type:** `METHODOLOGICAL_TEXTUAL`
- **Author's Claim:** Relying uncritically on Septuagint translations obscures Hebrew syntax and leads commentators to teach the opposite of what Hebrew Scripture states.
- **Short Exact Quotation:** "de mens het tegenovergestelde van wat de Schrift zegt"
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 253 [PDF p. 15]; Section 5
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [11, 12]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Comparison of Hebrew syntax in Gen 15:5b–6 and Rom 10:5ff with Septuagint translations; citing F. Breukelman (Bekker 1989:58).
- **Qualifications / Conditions:** Applies specifically when translators and commentators allow existing Greek or vernacular translations to dictate exegesis over Hebrew grammar.
- **Extraction Confidence:** `HIGH`
- **Notes:** Baaij 2005:253. Polemic against uncritical Septuagintal reading of Paul.
