B001-4: Romans 10:11 / Isaiah 28:16 (and Romans 9:33)
S06F-E001
Proposed Evidence ID: S06F-E001
B001 Passage ID: B001-4
Author's Claim: In Romans 10:11, Paul quotes directly from LXX Isaiah 28:16 and adds the pronoun πᾶς ("no one / everyone") to emphasize the universal availability of salvation through faith in Christ.
Short Exact Quotation: "The universal aspect of the statement is emphasised by the pronoun ‘no one’ (πᾶς), which is missing from the quotation in Romans 9:33."
Printed Page Number: Page 104
NotebookLM Citation:
1
Textual Evidence Invoked: LXX rendering of Isaiah 28:16 vs. Hebrew MT; insertion of πᾶς in Rom 10:11 compared to Rom 9:33.
Claim Type: Exegetical / Textual-critical
Qualifications / Conditions: The pronoun πᾶς is absent from LXX Isaiah 28:16 and from Paul's earlier citation in Romans 9:33.
Extraction Confidence: HIGH
S06F-E002
Proposed Evidence ID: S06F-E002
B001 Passage ID: B001-4
Author's Claim: In Romans 10:11, Paul shifts the metaphorical context of the "stone" in Isaiah 28:16 from a judge (as in Rom 9:33) to the resurrection of Jesus Christ and faith in that resurrection.
Short Exact Quotation: "The stone is no longer a judge but the resurrection of Jesus Christ and faith in that resurrection."
Printed Page Number: Page 104
NotebookLM Citation:
1
Textual Evidence Invoked: Re-contextualization of Isaiah 28:16 in Rom 10:11 following its composite use in Rom 9:33.
Claim Type: Exegetical
Qualifications / Conditions: No one who believes in Jesus and professes Him as the resurrected Lord will be put to shame.
Extraction Confidence: HIGH
