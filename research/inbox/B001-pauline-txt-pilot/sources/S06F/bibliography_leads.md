Here is the itemized list of footnoted and bibliographical sources cited, relied upon, or disputed by Mireia Ryšková in "The Reception of the Book of Isaiah in Paul’s Letter to the Romans" (2019) across each substantive B001 discussion and its immediate contextual units.
1. B001-4: Romans 10:11 / Isaiah 28:16 (and Romans 9:33)
Dietrich-Alex Koch
Title: Schrift als Zeuge des Evangeliums: Untersuchungen zur Verwendung und zum Verständnis der Schrift bei Paulus [p. 16]
Year: 1986 [p. 16]
Pages: Cited in Footnotes 14, 35 [p. 16, 34]
Why it matters: Discussed. Cited for the hypothesis that the composite "stone" quotation in Romans 9:33 (Isa 28:16 + 8:14) originated from an oral pre-Pauline tradition [p. 34].
C. H. Dodd
Title: According to the Scriptures. The Substructure of New Testament Theology [p. 97]
Year: 1953 [p. 97]
Pages: Cited in Footnotes 9, 35 [p. 97, 34]
Why it matters: Discussed. Cited for the hypothesis that the composite "stone" text in Romans 9:33 was drawn from a written pre-Pauline testimonia collection [p. 34].
Shiu-Lun Shum
Title: Paul’s Use of Isaiah in Romans: A Comparative Study of Paul’s Letter to the Romans and the Sibylline and Qumran Sectarian Texts [p. 97]
Year: 2002 [p. 97]
Pages: Pages 214, 215–216, 221, 223 (Footnotes 4, 35, 36, 37, 40) [p. 97, 34, 36, 38]
Why it matters: Disputed / Discussed. Shum asserts that Paul quoted directly from an Isaiah scroll in Corinth rather than a testimony collection [p. 34]; he analyzes whether the original stone in Isaiah 28:16 referred to God or the Jerusalem temple [p. 34], and notes Paul's application of Isaiah's monotheistic statements to Christ as co-sovereign [p. 38].
Florian Wilk
Title: Die Bedeutung des Jesajabuches für Paulus [p. 96]
Year: 1998 [p. 96]
Pages: Pages 390, 392 (Footnotes 3, 4, 39, 41) [p. 96, 36, 38]
Why it matters: Relied upon. Cited for describing the "decisive character" (entscheidenden Charakter) of the stone quotation for Israel [p. 36] and Paul's commitment to anchor faith (πίστις) in Scripture for both Jew and Gentile [p. 38].
Frank Schleritt
Title: "Das Gesetz der Gerechtigkeit. Zur Auslegung von Römer 9:30–33", in Between Gospel and Election, ed. Florian Wilk and J. Ross Wagner [p. 36]
Year: 2010 [p. 36]
Pages: Pages 271–297 (summary pp. 296–297; Footnote 38) [p. 36]
Why it matters: Relied upon. Cited regarding the exegesis of Romans 9:30–33 and Christ as the stone of stumbling and firm foundation [p. 36].
J. Ross Wagner
Title: Isaiah and Paul in Concert in the Letter to the Romans [p. 97]
Year: 2003 [p. 97]
Pages: Pages 20–28, 109–117 (Footnotes 6, 34) [p. 97, 35]
Why it matters: Relied upon. Cited regarding scripture memorization/testimonia practices [p. 97] and Paul locating contemporary Israel between desolation and hope [p. 35].
2. B001-6: Romans 11:34 / Isaiah 40:13
Florian Wilk
Title: Die Bedeutung des Jesajabuches für Paulus [p. 96]
Year: 1998 [p. 96]
Pages: Pages 392, 399–401, 404 (Footnotes 57, 64, 65, 66) [p. 52, 63, 64, 65]
Why it matters: Relied upon. Cited for the unsearchable nature of God's sovereignty [p. 52] and the situational, compositional, argumentational, and hermeneutical functions of Isaiah quotations in Romans [p. 63–64].
3. B001-7: Romans 15:12 / Isaiah 11:10
Florian Wilk
Title: Die Bedeutung des Jesajabuches für Paulus [p. 96]
Year: 1998 [p. 96]
Pages: Page 394 (Footnote 61) [p. 59]
Why it matters: Disputed / Contrasted. Ryšková explicitly contrasts her interpretation with Wilk, noting that while Wilk plays down the importance of Romans 15:12 / Isaiah 11:10 [p. 59], Ryšková emphasizes its positive motivational force for Gentile inclusion and community unity [p. 59].
J. Ross Wagner
Title: Isaiah and Paul in Concert in the Letter to the Romans [p. 97]
Year: 2003 [p. 97]
Pages: Page 359 (Footnote 67) [p. 65]
Why it matters: Relied upon. Cited for Paul's self-understanding as a chosen instrument through whom God provokes Israel to jealousy to effect their salvation [p. 65].
4. Surrounding Context: B001-2 & B001-3 (Romans 9:25–26 / Hosea 2:23 & 1:10)
Shiu-Lun Shum
Title: Paul’s Use of Isaiah in Romans [p. 97]
Year: 2002 [p. 97]
Pages: Pages 207, 211 (Footnotes 31, 33) [p. 30, 33]
Why it matters: Discussed. Cited regarding why Paul replaced λαός with number of sons in quoting Isaiah 10:22–23 to avoid lexical confusion with Hosea's use of λαός [p. 33].
Dieter Zeller
Title: Der Brief an die Römer [p. 35]
Year: 1985 [p. 35]
Pages: Pages 181–182 (Footnote 34) [p. 35]
Why it matters: Relied upon. Cited for how Paul's argument opens space for an unexpected work of God among Jew and Gentile [p. 35].
5. Surrounding Context: B001-5 (Romans 10:13 / Joel 2:32 [LXX 3:5])
Shiu-Lun Shum
Title: Paul’s Use of Isaiah in Romans [p. 97]
Year: 2002 [p. 97]
Pages: Page 223 (Footnote 40) [p. 38]
Why it matters: Relied upon. Cited for Paul applying Old Testament monotheistic statements to both God the Father and Christ as Lord [p. 38].
Florian Wilk
Title: Die Bedeutung des Jesajabuches für Paulus [p. 96]
Year: 1998 [p. 96]
Pages: Page 390 (Footnote 41) [p. 38]
Why it matters: Relied upon. Cited for Paul anchoring the universal criterion of faith (πίστις) in Scripture [p. 38].
