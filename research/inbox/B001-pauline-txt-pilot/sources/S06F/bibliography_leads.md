# S06F — Bibliography Leads and Scholarly Cross-References

Itemized list of scholarly works cited, relied upon, or evaluated by Mireia Ryšková in *The Reception of the Book of Isaiah in Paul's Letter to the Romans*, *AUC Theologica*, Vol. 9, No. 2 (2019), pp. 95–116.

> [!NOTE]
> In accordance with project review guidelines, each entry clearly distinguishes the **printed page, PDF page, and footnote where Ryšková cites the work in her article** (within her 22-page publication, pp. 95–116, PDF pp. 1–22) from the **page numbers within the cited scholar's original publication**. Indirect citations (e.g., scholars cited through Jonathan Whitlock or Shiu-Lun Shum) are explicitly identified as such.

---

### 1. B001-4: Romans 10:11 / Isaiah 28:16 (and Romans 9:33)

#### Dietrich-Alex Koch
- **Cited Publication:** *Schrift als Zeuge des Evangeliums: Untersuchungen zur Verwendung und zum Verständnis der Schrift bei Paulus* (Tübingen: J.C.B. Mohr [Paul Siebeck], 1986).
- **Ryšková Citation Locators:**
  - **Printed p. 99 [PDF p. 5], fn. 14:** Cited indirectly through Jonathan Whitlock (2008, p. 20) regarding statistical counts of quotations in Paul's letters (attributing 88/89 quotations to Koch).
  - **Printed p. 105 [PDF p. 11], Section 5 (Rom 9:33), fn. 35:** Discussed in text p. 105; cited indirectly through Shiu-Lun Shum (2002, p. 214) in fn. 35.
- **Scholarly Role & Function:** Discussed. Koch is evaluated for the hypothesis that the composite "stone" quotation in Romans 9:33 (Isa 28:16 + 8:14) originated from an oral pre-Pauline tradition that combined the passages around their shared catchword.

#### C. H. Dodd
- **Cited Publication:** *According to the Scriptures: The Substructure of New Testament Theology* (London: James Nisbet, 1953 / Scribner, 1953).
- **Ryšková Citation Locators:**
  - **Printed p. 97 [PDF p. 3], fn. 9:** Cited indirectly through Jonathan Whitlock (2008, p. 232) regarding primary Old Testament testimonia blocks in early Christianity being understood in context rather than as isolated proof-texts.
  - **Printed p. 105 [PDF p. 11], Section 5 (Rom 9:33), fn. 35:** Discussed in text p. 105; cited indirectly through Shiu-Lun Shum (2002, p. 214) in fn. 35.
- **Scholarly Role & Function:** Discussed. Dodd is evaluated for the hypothesis that the composite "stone" text in Romans 9:33 was drawn from a written pre-Pauline testimonia collection.

#### Shiu-Lun Shum
- **Cited Publication:** *Paul’s Use of Isaiah in Romans: A Comparative Study of Paul’s Letter to the Romans and the Sibylline and Qumran Sectarian Texts*, WUNT 2/156 (Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck, 2002).
- **Ryšková Citation Locators:**
  - **Printed p. 105 [PDF p. 11], Section 5 (Rom 9:33), fn. 35:** Citing Shum p. 214 (reviewing Koch and Dodd).
  - **Printed p. 105 [PDF p. 11], Section 5 (Rom 9:33), fn. 36:** Citing Shum pp. 215–216 (arguing Paul quoted directly from an Isaiah scroll in Corinth).
  - **Printed p. 105 [PDF p. 11], Section 5 (Rom 9:33), fn. 37:** Citing Shum p. 221 (analyzing whether the original stone in Isa 28:16 referred to God or to the temple foundation).
  - **Printed p. 106 [PDF p. 12], Section 6 (Rom 10:11), fn. 40:** Citing Shum p. 223 (providing the basis for the formulation that Paul applies monotheistic statements to Christ as co-sovereign with the Father).
- **Scholarly Role & Function:** Evaluated / Relied upon. Direct scholarly reliance on Shum's analysis of Corinthian scroll access, original stone referent, and high Pauline Christology.

#### Florian Wilk
- **Cited Publication:** *Die Bedeutung des Jesajabuches für Paulus*, FRLANT 179 (Göttingen: Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht, 1998).
- **Ryšková Citation Locators:**
  - **Printed p. 96 [PDF p. 2], fn. 3:** Citing Wilk regarding a "revised" edition of the LXX.
  - **Printed p. 105 [PDF p. 11], Section 5 (Rom 9:33), fn. 34:** Citing Wilk p. 393 on Rom 9:27–29.
  - **Printed p. 106 [PDF p. 12], Section 6 (Rom 10:11), fn. 39:** Citing Wilk p. 392 on the "decisive character" (*entscheidenden Charakter*) of the stone quotation for Israel.
  - **Printed p. 106 [PDF p. 12], Section 6 (Rom 10:11 / 10:13), fn. 41:** Citing Wilk p. 390 on Paul's commitment to anchor faith in Scripture for both Jew and Gentile.
- **Scholarly Role & Function:** Relied upon. Core monograph framing Pauline reception of Isaiah throughout Romans.

#### Frank Schleritt
- **Cited Publication:** "Das Gesetz der Gerechtigkeit. Zur Auslegung von Römer 9:30–33", in *Between Gospel and Election*, ed. F. Wilk and J. R. Wagner (Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck, 2010), pp. 271–297 (summary pp. 296–297).
- **Ryšková Citation Locator:** **Printed p. 106 [PDF p. 12], Section 5, fn. 38.**
- **Scholarly Role & Function:** Relied upon. Cited regarding the exegesis of Romans 9:30–33 and Christ as both stone of stumbling and firm foundation for faith.

#### Dieter Zeller
- **Cited Publication:** *Der Brief an die Römer*, RNT (Regensburg: Pustet, 1985), pp. 181–182.
- **Ryšková Citation Locator:** **Printed p. 105 [PDF p. 11], Section 4 (Rom 9:29), fn. 34.**
- **Scholarly Role & Function:** Relied upon. Cited for Paul's argument opening space for an unexpected work of God transcending limitations and saving both Jew and Gentile.

#### J. Ross Wagner
- **Cited Publication:** *Isaiah and Paul in Concert in the Letter to the Romans*, NovTSup 166 (Leiden: Brill, 2003).
- **Ryšková Citation Locators:**
  - **Printed p. 97 [PDF p. 3], fn. 6:** Citing Wagner pp. 20–28 on memorization practices and access to written Vorlage / testimonia extracts.
  - **Printed p. 105 [PDF p. 11], Section 4 (Rom 9:29), fn. 34:** Citing Wagner pp. 109–117, quoting p. 117 on Paul locating contemporary Israel between desolation and hope.
  - **Printed p. 106 [PDF p. 12], Section 5, fn. 38:** Co-editor of *Between Gospel and Election* with Wilk.
- **Scholarly Role & Function:** Relied upon. Direct reliance on Wagner's analysis of scriptural memory and remnant theology.

### 2. B001-6: Romans 11:34 / Isaiah 40:13

#### Florian Wilk
- **Cited Publication:** *Die Bedeutung des Jesajabuches für Paulus* (Göttingen: Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht, 1998).
- **Ryšková Citation Locators:**
  - **Printed p. 112 [PDF p. 18], Section 11, fn. 57:** Citing Wilk p. 392 on the ungraspable sovereignty of God rounding off the theological argument of Rom 9–11.
  - **Printed p. 116 [PDF p. 22], Summary, fn. 65:** Citing Wilk pp. 399–401 on Wilk's fourfold taxonomy of Isaiah's functions in Paul (situational, compositional, argumentational, hermeneutical).
- **Scholarly Role & Function:** Relied upon. Cited for God's sovereignty rounding off Rom 9–11 and for structural typology of Pauline Isaiah citations.

### 3. B001-7: Romans 15:12 / Isaiah 11:10

#### Florian Wilk
- **Cited Publication:** *Die Bedeutung des Jesajabuches für Paulus* (Göttingen: Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht, 1998).
- **Ryšková Citation Locators:**
  - **Printed p. 114 [PDF p. 20], Section 14 (Rom 15:12), fn. 61:** Citing Wilk p. 394.
  - **Printed p. 115 [PDF p. 21], Section 15, fn. 64:** Citing Wilk p. 392 on missionary legitimization.
  - **Printed p. 116 [PDF p. 22], Summary, fn. 66:** Citing Wilk p. 404 (Romans as culmination and sum of Pauline Isaiah reception).
- **Scholarly Role & Function:** Evaluated / Contrasted.
  - Ryšková explicitly contrasts her interpretation with Wilk: while Wilk plays down the theological weight of Rom 15:12 / Isa 11:10 (Wilk p. 394), Ryšková emphasizes its positive motivational force for Gentile inclusion and community harmony (Ryšková p. 114, fn. 61).
  - Relied upon for Paul's mission legitimization (p. 115) and canonical summary of Isaiah reception (p. 116).

#### Ulrich Wilckens
- **Cited Publication:** *Der Brief an die Römer III*, EKKNT VI/3 (Zürich-Braunschweig, Neukirchen-Vluyn: Benzinger / Neukirchener, 1989), p. 85.
- **Ryšková Citation Locator:** **Printed p. 114 [PDF p. 20], Section 13 (Rom 14:11), fn. 60.**
- **Scholarly Role & Function:** Relied upon. Cited on the intentional intermingling of Christocentrism and theocentrism as typical Pauline theology.

#### J. Ross Wagner
- **Cited Publication:** *Isaiah and Paul in Concert in the Letter to the Romans* (Leiden: Brill, 2003).
- **Ryšková Citation Locator:** **Printed p. 116 [PDF p. 22], Summary, fn. 67:** Citing Wagner p. 359.
- **Scholarly Role & Function:** Relied upon. Cited for Paul's self-understanding as a chosen instrument through whom God provokes Israel to jealousy to effect their salvation.
