# S06F — Bibliography Leads and Scholarly Cross-References

Itemized list of scholarly works cited, relied upon, or evaluated by Mireia Ryšková in *The Reception of the Book of Isaiah in Paul's Letter to the Romans*, *AUC Theologica*, Vol. 9, No. 2 (2019), pp. 95–116.

> [!NOTE]
> In accordance with project review guidelines, each entry clearly distinguishes the **printed page and footnote where Ryšková cites the work in her article** (within her 22-page publication, pp. 95–116) from the **page numbers within the cited scholar's original publication**.

### 1. B001-4: Romans 10:11 / Isaiah 28:16 (and Romans 9:33)

#### Dietrich-Alex Koch
- **Cited Publication:** *Schrift als Zeuge des Evangeliums: Untersuchungen zur Verwendung und zum Verständnis der Schrift bei Paulus* (Tübingen: J.C.B. Mohr [Paul Siebeck], 1986).
- **Ryšková Citation Locators:**
  - Printed p. 97 [PDF p. 3], fn. 14 (on text-plots and contextual quotation).
  - Printed p. 105 [PDF p. 11], Section 5 (Rom 9:33), fn. 35 (citing Shum 2002, p. 214).
- **Scholarly Role & Function:** Discussed. Koch is cited for the hypothesis that the composite "stone" quotation in Romans 9:33 (Isa 28:16 + 8:14) originated from an oral pre-Pauline tradition that combined the passages around their shared catchword.

#### C. H. Dodd
- **Cited Publication:** *According to the Scriptures: The Substructure of New Testament Theology* (London: James Nisbet, 1953).
- **Ryšková Citation Locators:**
  - Printed p. 96 [PDF p. 2], fn. 9 (on primary Old Testament testimonia blocks in early Christianity).
  - Printed p. 105 [PDF p. 11], Section 5 (Rom 9:33), fn. 35 (citing Shum 2002, p. 214).
- **Scholarly Role & Function:** Discussed. Dodd is cited for the hypothesis that the composite "stone" text in Romans 9:33 was drawn from a written pre-Pauline testimonia collection.

#### Shiu-Lun Shum
- **Cited Publication:** *Paul’s Use of Isaiah in Romans: A Comparative Study of Paul’s Letter to the Romans and the Sibylline and Qumran Sectarian Texts*, WUNT 2/156 (Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck, 2002).
- **Ryšková Citation Locators:**
  - Printed p. 105 [PDF p. 11], Section 5 (Rom 9:33), fn. 35 (citing Shum p. 214 on Koch and Dodd).
  - Printed p. 105 [PDF p. 11], Section 5 (Rom 9:33), fn. 36 (citing Shum pp. 215–216).
  - Printed p. 105 [PDF p. 11], Section 5 (Rom 9:33), fn. 37 (citing Shum p. 221).
  - Printed p. 106 [PDF p. 12], Section 6 (Rom 10:11), fn. 40 (citing Shum p. 223).
- **Scholarly Role & Function:** Evaluated / Relied upon.
  - Shum disputes the testimonia hypothesis, arguing Paul quoted directly from an Isaiah scroll in Corinth (cited work pp. 215–216; Ryšková p. 105).
  - Shum examines whether the original stone in Isa 28:16 referred to the temple foundation or God (cited work p. 221; Ryšková p. 105).
  - Shum provides the basis for Ryšková's formulation that Paul applies monotheistic Old Testament statements to Christ as "co-sovereign and co-unique with God the Father" (cited work p. 223; Ryšková p. 106).

#### Florian Wilk
- **Cited Publication:** *Die Bedeutung des Jesajabuches für Paulus*, FRLANT 179 (Göttingen: Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht, 1998).
- **Ryšková Citation Locators:**
  - Printed p. 105 [PDF p. 11], Section 5 (Rom 9:33), fn. 34 (citing Wilk p. 393 on Rom 9:27–29).
  - Printed p. 106 [PDF p. 12], Section 6 (Rom 10:11), fn. 39 (citing Wilk p. 392).
  - Printed p. 106 [PDF p. 12], Section 6 (Rom 10:11 / 10:13), fn. 41 (citing Wilk p. 390).
- **Scholarly Role & Function:** Relied upon. Cited for describing the "decisive character" (*entscheidenden Charakter*) of the stone quotation for Israel (Wilk p. 392; Ryšková p. 106) and Paul's commitment to anchor faith in Scripture for both Jew and Gentile (Wilk p. 390; Ryšková p. 106).

#### Frank Schleritt
- **Cited Publication:** "Das Gesetz der Gerechtigkeit. Zur Auslegung von Römer 9:30–33", in *Between Gospel and Election*, ed. F. Wilk and J. R. Wagner (Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck, 2010), pp. 271–297 (summary pp. 296–297).
- **Ryšková Citation Locator:** Printed p. 106 [PDF p. 12], Section 5, fn. 38.
- **Scholarly Role & Function:** Relied upon. Cited regarding the exegesis of Romans 9:30–33 and Christ as both stone of stumbling and firm foundation for faith.

#### Dieter Zeller
- **Cited Publication:** *Der Brief an die Römer*, RNT (Regensburg: Pustet, 1985), pp. 181–182.
- **Ryšková Citation Locator:** Printed p. 105 [PDF p. 11], Section 4, fn. 34.
- **Scholarly Role & Function:** Relied upon. Cited for Paul's argument opening space for an unexpected work of God transcending limitations and saving both Jew and Gentile.

#### J. Ross Wagner
- **Cited Publication:** *Isaiah and Paul in Concert in the Letter to the Romans*, NovTSup 166 (Leiden: Brill, 2003).
- **Ryšková Citation Locators:**
  - Printed p. 95 [PDF p. 1], fn. 6 (citing Wagner pp. 20–28).
  - Printed p. 105 [PDF p. 11], Section 4, fn. 34 (citing Wagner pp. 109–117, quoted p. 117).
- **Scholarly Role & Function:** Relied upon. Cited on Paul locating contemporary Israel between desolation and hope (Wagner p. 117; Ryšková p. 105).

### 2. B001-6: Romans 11:34 / Isaiah 40:13

#### Florian Wilk
- **Cited Publication:** *Die Bedeutung des Jesajabuches für Paulus* (Göttingen: Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht, 1998).
- **Ryšková Citation Locators:**
  - Printed p. 112 [PDF p. 18], Section 11, fn. 57 (citing Wilk p. 392).
  - Printed p. 116 [PDF p. 22], Summary, fn. 65 (citing Wilk pp. 399–401).

### 3. B001-7: Romans 15:12 / Isaiah 11:10

#### Florian Wilk
- **Cited Publication:** *Die Bedeutung des Jesajabuches für Paulus* (Göttingen: Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht, 1998).
- **Ryšková Citation Locators:**
  - Printed p. 114 [PDF p. 20], Section 14 (Rom 15:12), fn. 61 (citing Wilk p. 394).
  - Printed p. 115 [PDF p. 21], Section 15, fn. 64 (citing Wilk p. 392).
  - Printed p. 116 [PDF p. 22], Summary, fn. 66 (citing Wilk p. 404).
- **Scholarly Role & Function:** Evaluated / Contrasted.
  - Ryšková explicitly contrasts her interpretation with Wilk: while Wilk plays down the theological significance of Rom 15:12 / Isa 11:10 (Wilk p. 394), Ryšková emphasizes its positive motivational force for Gentile inclusion and community harmony (Ryšková p. 114, fn. 61).
  - Cited for Paul's mission legitimization (Wilk p. 392; Ryšková p. 115) and Romans as the culmination and sum of Pauline Isaiah reception (Wilk p. 404; Ryšková p. 116).

#### Ulrich Wilckens
- **Cited Publication:** *Der Brief an die Römer III*, EKKNT VI/3 (Zürich-Braunschweig, Neukirchen-Vluyn: Benzinger / Neukirchener, 1989), p. 85.
- **Ryšková Citation Locator:** Printed p. 114 [PDF p. 20], Section 13, fn. 60.
- **Scholarly Role & Function:** Relied upon. Cited for the intentional intermingling of Christocentrism and theocentrism as typical Pauline theology.

#### J. Ross Wagner
- **Cited Publication:** *Isaiah and Paul in Concert in the Letter to the Romans* (Leiden: Brill, 2003).
- **Ryšková Citation Locator:** Printed p. 116 [PDF p. 22], Summary, fn. 67 (citing Wagner p. 359).
- **Scholarly Role & Function:** Relied upon. Cited for Paul's self-understanding as a chosen instrument through whom God provokes Israel to jealousy to effect their salvation (Wagner p. 359; Ryšková p. 116).

### 4. Immediate Contextual Units: B001-2 & B001-3 (Romans 9:25–26 / Hosea 2:23 & 1:10)

#### Shiu-Lun Shum
- **Cited Publication:** *Paul’s Use of Isaiah in Romans* (Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck, 2002).
- **Ryšková Citation Locators:**
  - Printed p. 104 [PDF p. 10], Section 3, fn. 31 (citing Shum p. 211).
  - Printed p. 104 [PDF p. 10], Section 3, fn. 33 (citing Shum p. 207).
