## 1. Summary of Project Locus Relevance

In *The New Covenant and Paul's Quotations from Hosea in Romans 9:25–26* (2005), J. Paul Tanner focuses specifically and exclusively on the two Hosea citations quoted by Paul in Romans 9:25–26:
- **B001-2:** Romans 9:25 / Hosea 2:23
- **B001-3:** Romans 9:26 / Hosea 1:10

The remaining ten B001 comparisons are **NOT DISCUSSED** anywhere in this source volume.
