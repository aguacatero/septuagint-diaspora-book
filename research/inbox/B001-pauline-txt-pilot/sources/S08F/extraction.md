# S08F — Source-Bounded Scholarly Extraction

Itemized extraction of J. Paul Tanner's explicit positions and claims regarding **B001-2** (Romans 9:25 / Hosea 2:23), **B001-3** (Romans 9:26 / Hosea 1:10), and their surrounding context in Romans 9:24–29, drawn directly from *The New Covenant and Paul’s Quotations from Hosea in Romans 9:25–26*, *Bibliotheca Sacra*, Vol. 162, No. 645 (January–March 2005), pp. 95–110.

---

### B001-2 & B001-3: Itemized Extractions (Romans 9:25–26 / Hosea 2:23 & 1:10)

#### S08F-E001
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `S08F-E001`
- **B001 Passage ID:** `B001-2` (Romans 9:25 / Hosea 2:23)
- **Claim Type:** `Exegetical / Theological`
- **Author's Claim:** Paul cites Hosea 2:23 and 1:10 in Romans 9:25–26 specifically to establish that God's "vessels of mercy" include believing Gentiles alongside a believing Jewish remnant.
- **Short Exact Quotation:** "it seems clear that Paul was citing these verses to establish the point that God's \"vessels of mercy\" include Gentiles."
- **Printed Page Number:** 98
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 98 [PDF p. 4]
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [1, 2]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Greek text of Romans 9:24–26 bracketed by Rom 9:22–24 and Rom 9:30–31 vs. LXX Hosea 2:23 / 1:10b.
- **Qualifications / Conditions:** Paul does not introduce the Hosea quotes with the words "Now concerning the Gentiles," but the structural sandwiching between Rom 9:22–24 and Rom 9:30–31 demands this conclusion.
- **Extraction Confidence:** `HIGH`
- **Notes:** Tanner 2005:98. Supporting Gentile inclusion as vessels of mercy.

#### S08F-E002
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `S08F-E002`
- **B001 Passage ID:** `B001-2` (Romans 9:25 / Hosea 2:23)
- **Claim Type:** `Textual-critical / Exegetical`
- **Author's Claim:** In Romans 9:25, Paul quotes from a Greek rendering of Hosea 2:23 and intentionally reverses clause order while changing the verb to "I will call" (*καλέσω*) to emphasize divine election and match Romans 9:24.
- **Short Exact Quotation:** "Paul quoted from a Greek rendering of Hosea and the fact that he reversed the order of the clauses"
- **Printed Page Number:** 99
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 99 [PDF p. 5, fn. 4]
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [4, 5]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** LXX Hosea 2:23 (*ἐρῶ τῷ οὐ-λαῷ-μου...*) vs. Romans 9:25 (*καλέσω τὸν οὐ-λαόν-μου...*); Hebrew MT Hosea 2:25 (*וְאָמַרְתִּי לְלֹא-עַמִּי עַמִּי-אַתָּה*).
- **Qualifications / Conditions:** Paul switched Hosea's "I will say" to "I will call," probably in view of Romans 9:24 ("even us whom he called"); reversing clause order places this verb at the beginning of the composite quotation.
- **Extraction Confidence:** `HIGH`
- **Notes:** Tanner 2005:99, fn. 4 (citing Douglas J. Moo).

#### S08F-E003
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `S08F-E003`
- **B001 Passage ID:** `B001-3` (Romans 9:26 / Hosea 1:10)
- **Claim Type:** `Historical / Exegetical`
- **Author's Claim:** In Hosea's original eighth-century B.C. historical context, Hosea 1:10b and 2:23 pertained strictly to the northern kingdom of Israel being disowned and later restored, containing no reference to Gentiles.
- **Short Exact Quotation:** "Nothing in the context of these passages makes reference to Gentiles, nor did Hosea imply that the fulfillment of these promises would be with Gentiles."
- **Printed Page Number:** 100
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 100 [PDF p. 6]
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [4, 6]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** MT Hosea 1:6 (*Lo-ruhamah*), 1:9 (*Lo-ammi*), 1:10a–b, 2:23; Gen 22:17 (Abrahamic Covenant allusion in Hos 1:10a).
- **Qualifications / Conditions:** Hosea 1:10a alludes to the sand-of-the-sea promise of the Abrahamic Covenant made specifically to ethnic Israel.
- **Extraction Confidence:** `HIGH`
- **Notes:** Tanner 2005:100. Original Hosea context restricted to northern kingdom of Israel.

#### S08F-E004
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `S08F-E004`
- **B001 Passage ID:** `B001-2, B001-3` (Romans 9:25–26 / Hosea 2:23 & 1:10)
- **Claim Type:** `Hermeneutical / Theological`
- **Author's Claim:** Paul's application of Hosea 1:10b and 2:23 to Gentile believers affirms that the conversion of Gentiles is a direct, partial fulfillment of Hosea's prophecy rather than a mere analogy or general principle.
- **Short Exact Quotation:** "verses 25-26 affirm that the conversion of Gentiles is a direct fulfillment of Hosea's prophecy (though not the complete fulfillment)."
- **Printed Page Number:** 102
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 102 [PDF p. 8]
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [9, 10]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Romans 9:25–26 cited against Hosea 1:10b and 2:23; 1 Peter 2:9–10 parallel.
- **Qualifications / Conditions:** It is a direct fulfillment, but not the complete or exhaustive fulfillment, which still includes the future restoration of ethnic Israel.
- **Extraction Confidence:** `HIGH`
- **Notes:** Tanner 2005:102. Rejection of mere analogy; direct partial fulfillment.

#### S08F-E005
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `S08F-E005`
- **B001 Passage ID:** `B001-2, B001-3` (Romans 9:25–26 / Hosea 2:23 & 1:10)
- **Claim Type:** `Theological / Covenantal`
- **Author's Claim:** The mechanism enabling Gentiles to experience the Hosea status change from "not My people" to "My people" is their full participation in the New Covenant promised in Jeremiah 31:31–34, Ezekiel 36:24–28, and Hosea 2:18.
- **Short Exact Quotation:** "It was the New Covenant that made it possible for the Jewish remnant to become \"the people of God.\" The context of Hosea (particularly the mention of a \"covenant\" in Hosea 2:18) confirms this. Since it was the New Covenant that made it possible for Gentiles to become part of the church, the \"not my people\" in the Hosea passages ultimately had Gentiles in view."
- **Printed Page Number:** 110
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 110 [PDF p. 16]
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [13, 16]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Hosea 2:18 (covenant with beasts/birds), 2:20 (*yd'* / know the LORD), Jer 31:31–34, Ezek 36:24–28, Ezek 34:20–31.
- **Qualifications / Conditions:** Participation in the New Covenant conveys spiritual status as "the people of God," but does not transform Gentile believers into "Israel" or "spiritual Israel."
- **Extraction Confidence:** `HIGH`
- **Notes:** Tanner 2005:110 (and pp. 106–107). New Covenant as the theological bridge for Gentile inclusion.

#### S08F-E006
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `S08F-E006`
- **B001 Passage ID:** `B001-2, B001-3` (Romans 9:25–26 / Hosea 2:23 & 1:10)
- **Claim Type:** `Theological / Hermeneutical`
- **Author's Claim:** Paul does not teach that the church has replaced Israel or that Israel has forfeited her covenant promises; national, land, and kingdom promises remain reserved for ethnic Israel at Christ's return.
- **Short Exact Quotation:** "Paul was not operating by some new theological dictum whereby Israel no longer meant Israel. Instead he had come to understand the Gentile aspect of fulfillment."
- **Printed Page Number:** 103
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 103 [PDF p. 9]
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [18, 22]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Rom 9:6, 9:30–31, 11:1, 11:5, 11:25–27; Jer 30:7; Zech 12:3.
- **Qualifications / Conditions:** Distinguishes shared spiritual blessings enjoyed by New Covenant participants from specific physical and geographic promises reserved for ethnic Israel.
- **Extraction Confidence:** `HIGH`
- **Notes:** Tanner 2005:103 (and pp. 96, 110). Rejection of supersessionism; Israel still means Israel.

#### S08F-E007
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `S08F-E007`
- **B001 Passage ID:** `B001-2, B001-3` (Romans 9:25–26 / Hosea 2:23 & 1:10)
- **Claim Type:** `Exegetical / Theological`
- **Author's Claim:** Paul clarifies in Romans 9–11 the crucial distinction between ethnic Israel in unbelief and the believing Israelite remnant, demonstrating that God's covenant promises to Israel have not failed.
- **Short Exact Quotation:** "In Romans 11 the apostle clarified this distinction between ethnic Israel in unbelief and the believing Israelite \"remnant.\" God's covenant promises to Israel have not failed, because God will fulfill them with the believing remnant."
- **Printed Page Number:** 96
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 96 [PDF p. 2]
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [1, 3]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Romans 9:6 ("not all who are descended from Israel are Israel"), Rom 11:1–5 (remnant chosen by grace), Rom 9:27–29 (Isaiah remnant quotes).
- **Qualifications / Conditions:** True Israel consists of the believing remnant within ethnic Israel; God's word has not failed because the promises were never intended to apply indiscriminately to all physical descendants without faith.
- **Extraction Confidence:** `HIGH`
- **Notes:** Tanner 2005:96. Romans 9:6 true Israel and remnant theology.

#### S08F-E008
- **Proposed Evidence ID:** `S08F-E008`
- **B001 Passage ID:** `B001-2` (Romans 9:25 / Hosea 2:23)
- **Claim Type:** `Textual-critical / Exegetical`
- **Author's Claim:** Paul reversed the order of the clauses in Hosea 2:23 to stress the "My people" aspect of the promise and God's sovereign call to them, aligning the quotation with Romans 9:24.
- **Short Exact Quotation:** "reversed the order of the clauses (perhaps to stress the \"My people\" aspect of the promise and God's \"call\" to them)."
- **Printed Page Number:** 99
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 99 [PDF p. 5]
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [4, 5]
- **Textual Evidence Invoked:** Hosea 2:23 clause structure vs Romans 9:25 clause structure.
- **Qualifications / Conditions:** Reversing the clause order allows the verb *καλέσω* ("I will call") to stand at the head of the composite Hosea quotation.
- **Extraction Confidence:** `HIGH`
- **Notes:** Tanner 2005:99. Clause reversal placing "My people" first to stress divine election call.
