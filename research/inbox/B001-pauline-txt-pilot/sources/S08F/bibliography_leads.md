# S08F — Bibliography Leads

Disaggregated bibliographic entries cited, relied upon, or disputed by J. Paul Tanner in *The New Covenant and Paul’s Quotations from Hosea in Romans 9:25–26*, *Bibliotheca Sacra*, Vol. 162, No. 645 (January–March 2005), pp. 95–110.

---

## 1. Primary Exegetical and Hermeneutical Leads on Romans 9:25–26 / Hosea 2:23 & 1:10 (B001-2 & B001-3)

### Alva J. McClain (1973)
- **Author:** Alva J. McClain
- **Title:** *The Gospel of God’s Grace*
- **Publication Year:** 1973 (Chicago: Moody Press, p. 183)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 98 [PDF p. 4], fn. 1; Section 2
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. McClain argues that Paul's quotations from Hosea in Romans 9:25–26 refer to the inclusion and restoration of national Israel rather than Gentiles. Tanner explicitly rejects this view as inconsistent with the contextual bracket of Romans 9:22–24 and 9:30–31.

### John A. Battle, Jr. (1981)
- **Author:** John A. Battle, Jr.
- **Title:** *"Paul's Use of the Old Testament in Romans 9:25–26"*
- **Publication Year:** 1981 (*Grace Theological Journal*, Vol. 2, No. 1, pp. 115–129)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 98 [PDF p. 4], fn. 1; Section 2
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. Battle contends that Romans 9:25–26 refers strictly to ethnic Israel's restoration. Tanner identifies Battle as a primary modern proponent of the national-Israel-only interpretation and refutes his thesis.

### David L. Turner (1985)
- **Author:** David L. Turner
- **Title:** *"The Continuity of Scripture and Eschatology: Key Hermeneutical Issues"*
- **Publication Year:** 1985 (*Grace Theological Journal*, Vol. 6, No. 2, pp. 275–287, specifically p. 281)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 98 [PDF p. 4], fn. 3; Section 2
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. Following Battle, Turner argues that Paul in Romans 9:25–26 was thinking not of the church or Gentiles, but of the present unbelief and future restoration of national Israel.

### W. Edward Glenny (1992, 1995)
- **Author:** W. Edward Glenny
- **Titles:**
  - *"The 'People of God' in Romans 9:25–26"*, *Bibliotheca Sacra*, Vol. 152 (January–March 1995), pp. 42–59.
  - *"The Israelite Imagery of 1 Peter 2"*, in Craig A. Blaising & Darrell L. Bock (eds.), *Dispensationalism, Israel and the Church* (Grand Rapids: Zondervan, 1992), pp. 156–187.
- **Source Locators:**
  - Printed p. 98 [PDF p. 4], fn. 3; Section 2
  - Printed p. 102 [PDF p. 8], fn. 9; Section 4
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Glenny demonstrates that Paul cited Hosea 2:23 and 1:10 specifically to establish that God's "vessels of mercy" include Gentiles, and that Peter similarly viewed Hosea 2:23 as partially fulfilled with Gentile believers in 1 Peter 2:9–10.

### Douglas J. Moo (1996)
- **Author:** Douglas J. Moo
- **Title:** *The Epistle to the Romans* (NICNT)
- **Publication Year:** 1996 (Grand Rapids: Eerdmans, pp. 611–613)
- **Source Locators:**
  - Printed p. 98 [PDF p. 4], fn. 3; Section 2
  - Printed p. 99 [PDF p. 5], fn. 4; Section 3
  - Printed p. 101 [PDF p. 7], fn. 8; Section 4
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon in part / Disputed in part. Relied upon for textual analysis noting that Paul intentionally substituted *καλέσω* ("I will call") for Hosea's "I will say" to match Romans 9:24 and reversed the clauses of Hosea 2:23. Also relied upon for showing that mere "analogy" is hermeneutically insufficient for Paul. Disputed regarding Moo's replacement/supersessionist leanings that OT predictions of renewed Israel find fulfillment in the church.

### Robert B. Chisholm Jr. (1985, 1990)
- **Author:** Robert B. Chisholm Jr.
- **Titles:**
  - *"Hosea"*, in John F. Walvoord & Roy B. Zuck (eds.), *The Bible Knowledge Commentary, Old Testament* (Wheaton, IL: Victor, 1985), p. 1386.
  - *Interpreting the Minor Prophets* (Grand Rapids: Zondervan, 1990).
- **Source Locators:**
  - Printed p. 101 [PDF p. 7], fn. 8; Section 4
  - Printed p. 107 [PDF p. 13], fn. 18; Section 5
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed in part / Relied upon in part. Disputed on Romans 9:25–26 for proposing that Paul used Hosea merely by "analogy." Relied upon for exegetical analysis of Hosea's covenant theology and literary structure.

### John A. Witmer (1983)
- **Author:** John A. Witmer
- **Title:** *"Romans"*, in John F. Walvoord & Roy B. Zuck (eds.), *The Bible Knowledge Commentary, New Testament* (Wheaton, IL: Victor, 1983), pp. 478–479.
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 101 [PDF p. 7], fn. 8; Section 4
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. Cited alongside Chisholm for treating Paul's citation of Hosea as an analogical application of a general principle rather than direct scriptural fulfillment.

### S. Lewis Johnson (1992)
- **Author:** S. Lewis Johnson
- **Title:** *"Evidence from Romans 9–11"*, in Donald K. Campbell & Jeffrey L. Townsend (eds.), *A Case for Premillennialism: A New Consensus* (Chicago: Moody Press, 1992), pp. 199–223.
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 102 [PDF p. 8], fn. 10; Section 4
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. Proposes that Paul's citation of Hosea relies on an "analogical connection" between Israel and the church. Tanner rejects this analogy framework as inadequate to carry Paul's argumentative burden in Romans 9:24–29.

### George Eldon Ladd (1977)
- **Author:** George Eldon Ladd
- **Title:** *"Historic Premillennialism"*, in Robert G. Clouse (ed.), *The Meaning of the Millennium: Four Views* (Downers Grove, IL: InterVarsity Press, 1977), p. 23.
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 109 [PDF p. 15], fn. 22; Section 6
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. Ladd claims that the New Testament equates the church with "spiritual Israel." Tanner refutes Ladd, arguing that New Covenant participation makes Gentiles "the people of God" without converting them into Israel.

### Walter C. Kaiser Jr. (1989)
- **Author:** Walter C. Kaiser Jr.
- **Title:** *"An Assessment of 'Replacement Theology'"*
- **Publication Year:** 1989 (*Mishkan*, Vol. 10, pp. 9–20)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 95 [PDF p. 1], fn. 1; Section 1
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Provides a critical definition and assessment of supersessionism ("replacement theology"), supporting Tanner's distinction between ethnic Israel and the church.

### Rodney J. Decker (1995)
- **Author:** Rodney J. Decker
- **Title:** *"The Church's Relationship to the New Covenant"*
- **Publication Year:** 1995 (*Bibliotheca Sacra*, Vol. 152, pp. 290–305)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 104 [PDF p. 10], fn. 12; Section 5
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Key monograph analyzing dispensational and covenantal perspectives on how the church relates to the New Covenant promises.

### Richard Daniels (1999)
- **Author:** Richard Daniels
- **Title:** *"How Does the Church Relate to the New Covenant? or, Whose New Covenant Is It, Anyway?"*
- **Publication Year:** 1999 (*Journal of the Evangelical Theological Society*, Vol. 42, No. 1, pp. 64–98, specifically pp. 90, 92)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 104 [PDF p. 10], fn. 13; Section 5
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. Daniels argues that the church is only a "nonparticipating mediator" of the New Covenant. Tanner refutes Daniels, insisting that Gentiles are full, equal participants in New Covenant spiritual blessings.

### Larry D. Pettegrew (1993)
- **Author:** Larry D. Pettegrew
- **Title:** *The New Covenant Ministry of the Holy Spirit*
- **Publication Year:** 1993 (Lanham, MD: University Press of America, pp. 7–14, 27–45, 251–270)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 104 [PDF p. 10], fn. 14; Section 5
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Demonstrates that the indwelling and baptizing ministry of the Holy Spirit is the core sign and blessing of the New Covenant, shared equally by Jewish and Gentile believers in Christ.

### Hans Walter Wolff (1974)
- **Author:** Hans Walter Wolff
- **Title:** *Hosea* (Hermeneia Commentary)
- **Publication Year:** 1974 (Philadelphia: Fortress Press, pp. 51, 55)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 106 [PDF p. 12], fn. 17; Section 5
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Wolff recognizes that Hosea 2:18 represents the first prophetic reference to the end-time "new covenant," validating Tanner's covenantal link between Hosea 2:18 and Jeremiah 31:31.

### Douglas Stuart (1987)
- **Author:** Douglas Stuart
- **Title:** *Hosea–Jonah* (Word Biblical Commentary)
- **Publication Year:** 1987 (Waco, TX: Word Books, pp. 60–61)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 106 [PDF p. 12], fn. 16; Section 5
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Provides grammatical and historical commentary on Hosea 2:18–23, connecting the peace covenant with Yahweh's betrothal and re-establishment of covenant relationship.

### Leon J. Wood (1985)
- **Author:** Leon J. Wood
- **Title:** *"Hosea"*, in Frank E. Gaebelein (ed.), *The Expositor’s Bible Commentary*, Vol. 7 (Grand Rapids: Zondervan, 1985), pp. 178–180.
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 107 [PDF p. 13], fn. 18; Section 5
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. Wood fails to identify the covenant mentioned in Hosea 2:18 as the New Covenant, which Tanner views as an oversight that obscures the theological foundation of Paul's citation.

### Francis I. Andersen & David Noel Freedman (1980)
- **Authors:** Francis I. Andersen & David Noel Freedman
- **Title:** *Hosea* (Anchor Bible, Vol. 24)
- **Publication Year:** 1980 (Garden City, NY: Doubleday, p. 281)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 107 [PDF p. 13], fn. 18; Section 5
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. Offers detailed lexical analysis of Hosea 2:18 but fails to recognize its eschatological New Covenant identity.

### Andrew G. Shead (2000)
- **Author:** Andrew G. Shead
- **Title:** *"The Covenant and Pauline Hermeneutics"*, in Peter Bolt & Mark Thompson (eds.), *The Gospel to the Nations: Perspectives on Paul’s Mission* (Downers Grove, IL: InterVarsity Press, 2000), pp. 33–49.
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 108 [PDF p. 14], fn. 21; Section 5
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Demonstrates that Paul's apostolic self-understanding was grounded in New Covenant ministry (2 Cor 3; Jer 31:31–34), providing the hermeneutical bridge for his reading of Old Testament prophecies.
