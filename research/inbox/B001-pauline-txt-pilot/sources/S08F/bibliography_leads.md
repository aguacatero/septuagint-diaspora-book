# S08F — Bibliography Leads

Disaggregated bibliographic entries cited, relied upon, or disputed by J. Paul Tanner in *The New Covenant and Paul’s Quotations from Hosea in Romans 9:25–26*, *Bibliotheca Sacra*, Vol. 162, No. 645 (January–March 2005), pp. 95–110.

---

## 1. Primary Exegetical and Hermeneutical Leads on Romans 9:25–26 / Hosea 2:23 & 1:10 (B001-2 & B001-3)

### Walter C. Kaiser Jr. (1989)
- **Author:** Walter C. Kaiser Jr.
- **Title:** *"An Assessment of 'Replacement Theology'"*
- **Publication Year:** 1989 (*Mishkan*, Vol. 10, pp. 9–20)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 95 [PDF p. 1], fn. 1; Section 1
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Provides a critical definition and assessment of supersessionism ("replacement theology"), supporting Tanner's distinction between ethnic Israel and the church.

### Alva J. McClain (1973)
- **Author:** Alva J. McClain
- **Title:** *The Gospel of God’s Grace*
- **Publication Year:** 1973 (Chicago: Moody Press, p. 183)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 98 [PDF p. 4], fn. 2; Section 2
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. McClain argues that Paul's quotations from Hosea in Romans 9:25–26 refer to the inclusion and restoration of national Israel rather than Gentiles. Tanner explicitly rejects this view as inconsistent with the contextual bracket of Romans 9:22–24 and 9:30–31.

### John A. Battle, Jr. (1981)
- **Author:** John A. Battle, Jr.
- **Title:** *"Paul's Use of the Old Testament in Romans 9:25–26"*
- **Publication Year:** 1981 (*Grace Theological Journal*, Vol. 2, No. 1, pp. 115–129)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 98 [PDF p. 4], fn. 2; Section 2
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. Battle contends that Romans 9:25–26 refers strictly to ethnic Israel's restoration. Tanner identifies Battle as a primary modern proponent of the national-Israel-only interpretation and refutes his thesis.

### W. Edward Glenny (1995)
- **Author:** W. Edward Glenny
- **Title:** *"The 'People of God' in Romans 9:25–26"*
- **Publication Year:** 1995 (*Bibliotheca Sacra*, Vol. 152, pp. 42–59)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 98 [PDF p. 4], fn. 3; Section 2
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Glenny demonstrates that Paul cited Hosea 2:23 and 1:10 specifically to establish that God's "vessels of mercy" include Gentiles.

### Douglas J. Moo (1996) — Romans 9:24–26 Calling of Gentiles
- **Author:** Douglas J. Moo
- **Title:** *The Epistle to the Romans* (NICNT)
- **Publication Year:** 1996 (Grand Rapids: Eerdmans, p. 611)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 98 [PDF p. 4], fn. 3; Section 2
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Concludes that Paul's Old Testament scriptural support for the calling of Gentiles must be drawn from Hosea.

### David L. Turner (1985)
- **Author:** David L. Turner
- **Title:** *"The Continuity of Scripture and Eschatology: Key Hermeneutical Issues"*
- **Publication Year:** 1985 (*Grace Theological Journal*, Vol. 6, No. 2, pp. 275–287, specifically p. 281)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 98 [PDF p. 4], fn. 3; Section 2
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. Following Battle, Turner argues that Paul in Romans 9:25–26 was thinking not of the church or Gentiles, but of the present unbelief and future restoration of national Israel.

### Douglas J. Moo (1996) — Textual Modification of καλέσω
- **Author:** Douglas J. Moo
- **Title:** *The Epistle to the Romans* (NICNT)
- **Publication Year:** 1996 (Grand Rapids: Eerdmans, p. 612)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 99 [PDF p. 5], fn. 4; Section 3
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Textual analysis noting that Paul intentionally substituted *καλέσω* ("I will call") for Hosea's "I will say" to match Romans 9:24 ("whom He also called") and reversed the clauses of Hosea 2:23.

### Douglas J. Moo (1996) — Hermeneutical Supposition of Replacement
- **Author:** Douglas J. Moo
- **Title:** *The Epistle to the Romans* (NICNT)
- **Publication Year:** 1996 (Grand Rapids: Eerdmans, p. 613)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 101 [PDF p. 7], fn. 6; Section 4
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. Tanner disputes Moo's hermeneutical supposition that Old Testament predictions of renewed Israel find their fulfillment replacement-style in the church.

### Robert B. Chisholm Jr. (1985)
- **Author:** Robert B. Chisholm Jr.
- **Title:** *"Hosea"*, in John F. Walvoord & Roy B. Zuck (eds.), *The Bible Knowledge Commentary, Old Testament*
- **Publication Year:** 1985 (Wheaton, IL: Victor, p. 1386)
- **Source Locator:** Printed pp. 101–102 [PDF pp. 7–8], fn. 8; Section 4
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. Proposes that Paul quoted Hosea merely for an "analogy" or application of a principle to Gentiles rather than direct fulfillment.

### John A. Witmer (1983)
- **Author:** John A. Witmer
- **Title:** *"Romans"*, in John F. Walvoord & Roy B. Zuck (eds.), *The Bible Knowledge Commentary, New Testament*
- **Publication Year:** 1983 (Wheaton, IL: Victor, pp. 478–479)
- **Source Locator:** Printed pp. 101–102 [PDF pp. 7–8], fn. 8; Section 4
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. Cited alongside Chisholm for treating Paul's citation of Hosea as an analogical application of a general principle.

### S. Lewis Johnson (1992)
- **Author:** S. Lewis Johnson
- **Title:** *"Evidence from Romans 9–11"*, in Donald K. Campbell & Jeffrey L. Townsend (eds.), *A Case for Premillennialism: A New Consensus*
- **Publication Year:** 1992 (Chicago: Moody Press, pp. 199–223)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 102 [PDF p. 8], fn. 8; Section 4
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. Proposes an "analogical connection" between Israel and the church in Hosea, which Tanner rejects as inadequate to support Paul's scriptural argument.

### Douglas J. Moo (1996) — Rejection of Mere Analogy
- **Author:** Douglas J. Moo
- **Title:** *The Epistle to the Romans* (NICNT)
- **Publication Year:** 1996 (Grand Rapids: Eerdmans, p. 613)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 102 [PDF p. 8], fn. 8; Section 4
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Agrees with Moo that Paul requires more than an analogy to establish from Scripture justification for God's calling of Gentiles to be His people.

### W. Edward Glenny (1992)
- **Author:** W. Edward Glenny
- **Title:** *"The Israelite Imagery of 1 Peter 2"*, in Craig A. Blaising & Darrell L. Bock (eds.), *Dispensationalism, Israel and the Church*
- **Publication Year:** 1992 (Grand Rapids: Zondervan, pp. 156–187)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 102 [PDF p. 8], fn. 9; Section 4
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Demonstrates that Peter saw his readers as a partial fulfillment of Exodus 19:5–6 and Hosea 2:23.

### Roy B. Zuck (1991)
- **Author:** Roy B. Zuck
- **Title:** *Basic Bible Interpretation*
- **Publication Year:** 1991 (Wheaton, IL: Victor, pp. 267–269, 274–275)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 103 [PDF p. 9], fn. 10; Section 5
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Discusses the hermeneutical concepts of "heightening" and *references plenior* (enlarging OT statements to fill them with related implications).

### David K. Lowery (1983)
- **Author:** David K. Lowery
- **Title:** *"1 Corinthians"*, in John F. Walvoord & Roy B. Zuck (eds.), *The Bible Knowledge Commentary, New Testament*
- **Publication Year:** 1983 (Wheaton, IL: Victor, p. 505)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 103 [PDF p. 9], fn. 11; Section 5
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Cites dating of 1 Corinthians (ca. A.D. 54–55) to argue Paul understood the church as one body by Spirit baptism prior to writing Romans.

### Rodney J. Decker (1995)
- **Author:** Rodney J. Decker
- **Title:** *"The Church's Relationship to the New Covenant"*
- **Publication Year:** 1995 (*Bibliotheca Sacra*, Vol. 152, pp. 290–305)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 104 [PDF p. 10], fn. 12; Section 5
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Key monograph analyzing dispensational and covenantal perspectives on how the church relates to the New Covenant.

### Richard Daniels (1999)
- **Author:** Richard Daniels
- **Title:** *"How Does the Church Relate to the New Covenant? or, Whose New Covenant Is It, Anyway?"*
- **Publication Year:** 1999 (*Faith and Mission*, Vol. 16, spring 1999, pp. 64–98, specifically pp. 90, 92)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 104 [PDF p. 10], fn. 13; Section 5
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. Daniels argues the church is merely a "nonparticipating mediator" between God and Israel. Tanner refutes Daniels, insisting that Gentiles are full, equal participants in New Covenant spiritual blessings.

### Larry D. Pettegrew (1993)
- **Author:** Larry D. Pettegrew
- **Title:** *The New Covenant Ministry of the Holy Spirit*
- **Publication Year:** 1993 (Lanham, MD: University Press of America, pp. 7–14, 27–45, 251–270)
- **Source Locator:** Printed pp. 104–105 [PDF pp. 10–11], fn. 14; Section 5
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Demonstrates that the indwelling of the Holy Spirit is the foundational blessing of the New Covenant enabling Gentile participation in Christ.

### Paul R. Thorsell (1998)
- **Author:** Paul R. Thorsell
- **Title:** *"The Spirit in the Present Age: Preliminary Fulfillment of the Predicted New Covenant according to Paul"*
- **Publication Year:** 1998 (*Journal for the Evangelical Theological Society*, Vol. 41, pp. 397–413)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 105 [PDF p. 11], fn. 14; Section 5
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Cites preliminary fulfillment of the predicted New Covenant Spirit ministry in the present church age according to Paul.

### Douglas Stuart (1987)
- **Author:** Douglas Stuart
- **Title:** *Hosea–Jonah* (Word Biblical Commentary)
- **Publication Year:** 1987 (Waco, TX: Word Books, pp. 60–61)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 106 [PDF p. 12], fn. 16; Section 5
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Grammatical and historical analysis of Hosea 2:18–23, connecting the covenant of peace with divine betrothal.

### Gary W. Light (1993)
- **Author:** Gary W. Light
- **Title:** *"The New Covenant in the Book of Hosea"*
- **Publication Year:** 1993 (*Review and Expositor*, Vol. 90, pp. 219–238, specifically p. 229)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 106 [PDF p. 12], fn. 17; Section 5
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Demonstrates that Hosea 2:18 refers to the New Covenant through the intimate marriage metaphor ("Anyone who knows YHVH must exclaim 'iSi... It is a new covenant in kind much more than in time").

### Leon J. Wood (1985)
- **Author:** Leon J. Wood
- **Title:** *"Hosea"*, in Frank E. Gaebelein (ed.), *The Expositor’s Bible Commentary*, Vol. 7
- **Publication Year:** 1985 (Grand Rapids: Zondervan, pp. 178–180)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 107 [PDF p. 13], fn. 18; Section 5
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. Criticized for ignoring the mention of "covenant" in Hosea 2:18 and making no attempt to connect it with the New Covenant.

### Francis I. Andersen & David Noel Freedman (1980)
- **Authors:** Francis I. Andersen & David Noel Freedman
- **Title:** *Hosea* (Anchor Bible, Vol. 24)
- **Publication Year:** 1980 (Garden City, NY: Doubleday, p. 281)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 107 [PDF p. 13], fn. 18; Section 5
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. Offers detailed lexical analysis of Hosea 2:18 but offers no help identifying the covenant.

### Hans Walter Wolff (1974)
- **Author:** Hans Walter Wolff
- **Title:** *Hosea* (Hermeneia Commentary, trans. Gary Stensell)
- **Publication Year:** 1974 (Philadelphia: Fortress Press, pp. 51, 55)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 107 [PDF p. 13], fn. 18; Section 5
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Explicitly affirms that Hosea 2:18 contains "the first reference to a 'new covenant' of the endtime (cf. Jer. 31:31)."

### Andrew G. Shead (2000)
- **Author:** Andrew G. Shead
- **Title:** *"The Covenant and Pauline Hermeneutics"*, in Peter Bolt & Mark Thompson (eds.), *The Gospel to the Nations: Perspectives on Paul’s Mission*
- **Publication Year:** 2000 (Downers Grove, IL: InterVarsity Press, pp. 33–49)
- **Source Locator:** Printed pp. 108–109 [PDF pp. 14–15], fn. 21; Section 5
- **Why It Matters:** Relied upon. Insight into Paul's understanding of New Covenant ministry in light of Jeremiah 31:31–34 and 2 Corinthians 3.

### George Eldon Ladd (1977)
- **Author:** George Eldon Ladd
- **Title:** *"Historic Premillennialism"*, in Robert G. Clouse (ed.), *The Meaning of the Millennium: Four Views*
- **Publication Year:** 1977 (Downers Grove, IL: InterVarsity Press, p. 23)
- **Source Locator:** Printed p. 109 [PDF p. 15], fn. 22; Section 6
- **Why It Matters:** Disputed. Claims the New Testament applies Old Testament prophecies to the church so as to identify it as "spiritual Israel"—a position Tanner explicitly refutes.
