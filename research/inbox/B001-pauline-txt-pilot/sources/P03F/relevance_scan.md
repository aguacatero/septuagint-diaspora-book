# P03F — B001 Textual Inventory (Primary Relevance Scan)

**Source ID:** P03F  
**Source Title:** *The Old Testament in Greek according to the Septuagint, Vol. III: Hosea–4 Maccabees*  
**Editor:** Henry Barclay Swete, D.D. (Cambridge University Press, 1894)  
**Source Class:** PRIMARY (Septuagint / Old Greek manual critical edition)  
**Collation Witnesses in Apparatus:** Codex Vaticanus (B, primary base text where extant), Codex Sinaiticus (א / S), Codex Alexandrinus (A), Codex Marchalianus (Q), Codex rescriptus Cryptoferratensis (Γ), and Dublin fragments (Z / O)  

---

## Overview

Because P03F is Volume III of Swete's Septuagint edition covering Hosea through 4 Maccabees (The Prophets and remaining books), only the seven Old Testament passages falling within the Prophetic corpus (Hosea, Joel, Isaiah) are `TEXT_PRESENT`. The five Pentateuchal quotations (Genesis, Exodus, Deuteronomy) are `NOT_IN_SOURCE` as they belong to Volume I (*Genesis–IV Kings*, evaluated under accepted source P02F).

The printed Greek text and critical apparatus in P03F constitute the primary textual evidence for the Septuagint / Old Greek side of these seven B001 comparisons.
