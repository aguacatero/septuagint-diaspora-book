# P03F — Bibliography Leads

**Source ID:** P03F  
**Source Title:** *The Old Testament in Greek according to the Septuagint, Vol. III: Hosea–4 Maccabees*  
**Editor:** Henry Barclay Swete, D.D. (Cambridge University Press, 1894)  

---

## Direct Manuscript & Edition Leads Cited in Swete Vol. III

The following editions, collations, and scholarly reference works are explicitly cited in Swete's Preface and Table of Manuscripts as foundational for establishing the Septuagint text of Hosea, Joel, and Isaiah:

1. **Codex Vaticanus Graecus 1209 (Codex B)**
   - *Bibliotheca Apostolica Vaticana*, Rome.
   - Photographic facsimile: *Bibliorum Sacrorum Graecorum Codex Vaticanus B*, ed. Cozza-Luzi (Rome, 1889–1890).
   - Core running text for all Prophetic books in Swete Vol. III; primary base text for Hosea 1:10 (p. 2), Hosea 2:23 (p. 3), Joel 2:32 (p. 42), Isaiah 11:10 (p. 122), Isaiah 28:16 (p. 149), Isaiah 29:14 (pp. 151–152), and Isaiah 40:13 (p. 174).

2. **Codex Sinaiticus (Codex א / S, Petropolitanus)**
   - *Imperial Library of St. Petersburg* (now British Library / National Library of Russia).
   - Facsimile edition: *Bibliorum Codex Sinaiticus Petropolitanus*, ed. Constantin von Tischendorf (St. Petersburg, 1862).
   - Extant for Joel and Isaiah; collated throughout Joel 2:32, Isaiah 11:10, Isaiah 28:16, Isaiah 29:14, and Isaiah 40:13.

3. **Codex Alexandrinus (Codex A, Royal MS 1 D. V–VIII)**
   - *British Museum*, London.
   - Photographic autotype edition: *The Codex Alexandrinus in Reduced Photographic Facsimile* (British Museum, 1879–1883).
   - Fully extant across all seven Prophetic targets; major collation witness in Hosea, Joel, and Isaiah.

4. **Codex Marchalianus (Codex Q, Vaticanus Graecus 2125)**
   - *Bibliotheca Apostolica Vaticana*, Rome.
   - Photographic heliotype edition and monograph: Antonio Ceriani, *De codice Marchaliano vaticano graeco 2125* (Rome: Vatican Press, 1890).
   - 6th-century Egyptian uncial of the Prophets preserving extensive Hexaplaric annotations; critical for the marginal readings and variant attestations at Isaiah 28:16 (Hexaplaric Three `ου σπευσει`), Isaiah 40:13, Hosea 1:10, Hosea 2:23, and Joel 2:32.

5. **Codex rescriptus Cryptoferratensis (Codex Γ)**
   - *Basilian Monastery of Grottaferrata* (Cod. Crypt. A. $\delta$. 1).
   - Edited by Giuseppe Cozza-Luzi (*Sacrorum Bibliorum vetustissima fragmenta graeca et latina*, pars iii, Rome, 1877).
   - 8th/9th-century palimpsest uncial fragments containing substantial sections of the Prophets; collated at Isaiah 11:10, Isaiah 28:16, Isaiah 29:14, and Isaiah 40:13.

6. **Fragmenta Tischendorfiana / Dublinensia (Codex Z / O)**
   - Fragmentary palimpsests of Isaiah edited by Constantin von Tischendorf (*Monumenta Sacra Inedita*, nova coll. vol. i) and T. K. Abbott (Dublin, 1880).
   - Collated at Isaiah 29:14 (p. 152).

7. **Eberhard Nestle, *Septuagintastudien***
   - Cited by Swete in the Preface (p. xix) regarding corrections and collations for Codex Alexandrinus and the Prophets.
