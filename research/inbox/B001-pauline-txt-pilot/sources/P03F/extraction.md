## Part 1: Primary B001 Textual Evidence Atoms (`P03F-P001` to `P03F-P007`)

These items document the printed Greek text and textual apparatus for the seven Prophetic passages in the B001 corpus. Rows are normalized in `primary_text_ledger.csv`.

### P03F-P001: Hosea 2:23 [LXX 2:25] (Romans 9:25)
- **B001 ID:** `B001-2`
- **Witness Role:** `OLD_GREEK`
- **Passage Reference:** Ὡσηὲ (Hosea) 2:23 [LXX 2:25]
- **Exact Printed Text:** `καὶ σπερῶ αὐτὴν ἐμαυτῷ ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς, καὶ ἀγαπήσω τὴν Οὐκ ἠγαπημένην, καὶ ἐρῶ τῷ Οὐ λαῷ μου Λαός μου εἶ σύ, καὶ αὐτὸς ἐρεῖ Κύριος ὁ θεός μου εἶ σύ.`
- **Printed Page / Locator:** Page 3
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [1]
- **Apparatus Present:** YES
- **Apparatus Readings & Witnesses:** `23 αγαπησω] ελεησω AQ | ηγαπημενην] ηλεημενην AQ`
- **Textual Feature:** The base text of Codex Vaticanus (B) exhibits love-terminology (`ἀγαπήσω` and participle `ἠγαπημένην`), directly matching the verb/participle stem in Paul's citation in Rom 9:25 (cf. accepted P01F-P002 `καὶ τὴν οὐκ ἠγαπημένην ἠγαπημένην`), whereas Codices Alexandrinus (A) and Marchalianus (Q) substitute mercy-terminology (`ἐλεήσω / ἠλεημένην`, cf. 1 Pet 2:10). The noun phrase designation `τῷ Οὐ λαῷ μου` is unanimously attested across B, A, and Q without apparatus variation.
- **Limitation:** Supplies only the Greek uncial witnesses collated by Swete at this locus (Codex Vaticanus B base text, Codex Alexandrinus A, Codex Marchalianus Q); lacks Hebrew MT text of Hos 2:23 and New Testament citation context (evaluated under accepted P01F-P002).

### P03F-P002: Hosea 1:10 [LXX 2:1] (Romans 9:26)
- **B001 ID:** `B001-3`
- **Witness Role:** `OLD_GREEK`
- **Passage Reference:** Ὡσηὲ (Hosea) 1:10 [LXX 2:1]
- **Exact Printed Text:** `καὶ ἔσται ἐν τῷ τόπῳ οὗ ἐρρήθη αὐτοῖς Οὐ λαός μου ὑμεῖς, κληθήσονται καὶ αὐτοὶ υἱοὶ θεοῦ ζῶντος.`
- **Printed Page / Locator:** Page 2
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [2]
- **Apparatus Present:** YES
- **Apparatus Readings & Witnesses:** `10 εσται] + μετα ταυτα AQ | ου 1°] ουχ A | κληθησονται] + εκει AQ | om και αυτοι A (s' θ' το και om Qmg)`
- **Textual Feature:** Codex Vaticanus (B) base text reads `κληθήσονται καὶ αὐτοί`, while Codices Alexandrinus (A) and Marchalianus (Q) add the local adverb `ἐκεῖ` (`+ εκει AQ`), exactly matching the Pauline citation form in Rom 9:26 (cf. accepted P01F-P003 `ἐκεῖ κληθήσονται υἱοὶ θεοῦ ζῶντος`). In addition, Codex A omits `καὶ αὐτοί`, aligning closely with Paul's omission of those pronouns.
- **Limitation:** Supplies only the Greek uncial witnesses collated by Swete at this locus (B, A, Q); lacks Hebrew MT text of Hos 1:10 and New Testament citation context (evaluated under accepted P01F-P003).

### P03F-P003: Isaiah 28:16 (Romans 10:11)
- **B001 ID:** `B001-4`
- **Witness Role:** `OLD_GREEK`
- **Passage Reference:** Ἠσαίας (Isaiah) 28:16
- **Exact Printed Text:** `διὰ τοῦτο οὕτως λέγει κύριος Κύριος Ἰδοὺ ἐγὼ ἐμβάλλω εἰς τὰ θεμέλια Σειὼν λίθον πολυτελῆ ἐκλεκτὸν ἀκρογωνιαῖον ἔντιμον, εἰς τὰ θεμέλια αὐτῆς, καὶ ὁ πιστεύων οὐ μὴ καταισχυνθῇ.`
- **Printed Page / Locator:** Page 149
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [3]
- **Apparatus Present:** YES
- **Apparatus Readings & Witnesses:** `16 om κς 2° א A Q* (hab Qmg) Γ | εμβαλω א A Q | Σιων BabאAQΓ | αυτης] + ※ τεθεμελιωμενον Qmg | ο πιστευων] + επ αυτω א A Q | ου μη καταισχυνθη] οι γ' (θη sup ras A?vid) ου σπευσει Qmg`
- **Textual Feature:** Codex Vaticanus (B) base text reads `ὁ πιστεύων οὐ μὴ καταισχυνθῇ` without a prepositional complement, but Codices Sinaiticus (א), Alexandrinus (A), and Marchalianus (Q) all explicitly add `ἐπ᾽ αὐτῷ` (`+ επ αυτω א A Q`), matching the wording found in Paul's quotation in Rom 10:11 (cf. accepted P01F-P004 `Πᾶς ὁ πιστεύων ἐπʼ αὐτῷ οὐ καταισχυνθήσεται`) and Rom 9:33; whether this attestation represents a direct textual antecedent is deferred to later cross-source synthesis. The apparatus also records the Hexaplaric reading of the Three (Aquila, Symmachus, Theodotion) `οὐ σπεύσει` in Qmg, reflecting the Hebrew יָחִישׁ.
- **Limitation:** Supplies only the Greek uncial witnesses collated by Swete at this locus (B, א, A, Q, Γ); lacks Hebrew MT text of Isa 28:16 and New Testament citation context (evaluated under accepted P01F-P004).

- **B001 ID:** `B001-6`

- **Passage Reference:** Ἠσαίας (Isaiah) 40:13
- **Exact Printed Text:** `τίς ἔγνω νοῦν Κυρίου, καὶ τίς αὐτοῦ σύμβουλος ἐγένετο, ὃς συμβιβᾷ αὐτόν;`
- **Printed Page / Locator:** Page 174
- **NotebookLM Citation:** [5]
- **Apparatus Present:** YES
- **Apparatus Readings & Witnesses:** `13 συμβουλος αυτου AQΓ | συμβιβα] συμβιβασει אc.a (-βασι sed rurs ras σι) AQmgΓ | 14 ... αυτω 2°] + η τις προεδωκεν αυτω και ανταποδοθησεται (-δωθ. A) αυτω א* (improb אc.a et postea) A`

Furthermore, the apparatus documents that Swete records an addition in א* and A at Isaiah 40:14 (`+ η τις προεδωκεν αυτω και ανταποδοθησεται [-δωθ. A] αυτω`) corresponding closely to the wording of Romans 11:35; this edition alone documents witness attestation and does not determine the origin or direction of dependence (deferred to cross-source synthesis).
- **Limitation:** Supplies only the Greek uncial witnesses collated by Swete at this locus (B, א, A, Q, Γ); lacks Hebrew MT text of Isa 40:13 and New Testament citation context (evaluated under accepted P01F-P006).

## Part 2: Editorial Method & Manuscript Collation in the Prophets (`P03F-E001` to `P03F-E005`)

### P03F-E001: Primacy of Codex Vaticanus (B) in the Prophetic Books
- **Claim:** Swete adheres strictly to the rule of printing the text of Codex Vaticanus (B, 4th cent.) as the base text wherever extant throughout the Prophets, reproducing its orthography, itacisms, and line division, and relegating variants of all other witnesses to the apparatus.
- **Support:** Preface, p. v: "The great Vatican MS., whose text and order we have generally followed, ends with the Prophets."

### P03F-E002: Critical Apparatus Witnesses for the Prophetic Corpus
- **Claim:** Unlike Volume I (where the apparatus was largely restricted to B, A, D, E, F), the prophetic apparatus in Volume III introduces key uncial witnesses including Codex Sinaiticus (א), Codex Marchalianus (Q, 6th cent.), Codex rescriptus Cryptoferratensis (Γ, 8th/9th cent.), and Dublin fragments (Z/O).
- **Support:** Preface, p. v: "In the Prophets it has been possible to employ, in addition to the great codices B א A, the Codex Marchalianus (Q), the Codex rescriptus Cryptoferratensis (Γ), and the Dublin fragments of Isaiah (O), as well as those edited by Tischendorf (Z)."

### P03F-E003: Manual Edition Distinct from Later Comprehensive Collations
- **Claim:** Swete’s manual edition provides a diplomatic uncial collation rather than an eclectic reconstruction of the archetype; it does not cite minuscule manuscripts, daughter versions (except in specialized notes), or later patristic citations, and is distinct from the Göttingen Septuaginta editions (Ziegler).
- **Support:** Preface, pp. v–vi; prolegomena to manual series.

### P03F-E004: Adherence to Manuscript Orthography and Incorporation of Volume I Principles
- **Claim:** Swete explicitly incorporates by reference the general editorial principles stated in the preface to Volume I, while the printed prophetic text directly demonstrates adherence to Codex Vaticanus orthography and itacisms (e.g. `Σειών` for `Σιών` at Joel 2:32, p. 42; Isa 28:16, p. 149).
- **Support:** Preface, p. v: "The general principles upon which the edition is based were stated in the preface to the first volume..."; confirmed by direct edition-level observation of printed pages (pp. 42, 149).

### P03F-E005: Apparatus Typography Convention (Unaccented Minuscules)
- **Claim:** The critical apparatus consistently prints variant readings in Greek minuscules without breathings or accents (e.g. `23 αγαπησω] ελεησω AQ` on p. 3; `16 ο πιστευων] + επ αυτω א A Q` on p. 149; `13 συμβουλος αυτου AQΓ` on p. 174).
- **Support:** Directly observed edition typography across printed pages (pp. 3, 42, 122, 149, 174); distinguished as an edition-level typographic convention rather than an explicit preface claim (though Swete notes that ancient manuscripts lack breathings/accents prima manu, Preface pp. vii, x).

## Part 3: Textual-Critical Evaluation of Apparatus Limits

1. **Uncial Scope:** The evidence in P03F is strictly limited to the major uncial manuscripts collated by Swete for the prophetic books (B, א, A, Q, Γ, Z). Minuscule families, catena traditions, and papyri (such as 967) are not cited.
2. **Apparatus Reporting Conventions:** Swete reports variants from the base text (B); silence in the apparatus denotes agreement with B among the witnesses cited in the margin as extant for that page.
3. **Primary Route Boundary:** No Hebrew MT readings or NT textual variants are manufactured from P03F; cross-source NT comparisons reference accepted P01F canonical evidence rows (`P01F-P002` through `P01F-P008`).
