Itemized Audit Findings & Corrections
1. Preservation of Authorial Hedging in Textual Vorlage (Check #4 & #6)
Location: Page 264 (Note on Romans 9:25 / Hosea 2:23).
Previous Extraction: Stated definitively that "Paul utilized an Old Greek manuscript tradition of Hosea 2:23 reflecting the reading of Codex Vaticanus."
Source Observation: Sanday and Headlam write with explicit probability framing: "In the second clause St. Paul seems to have used a text containing the reading of the Vatican MS., for the latter can hardly have been altered to harmonize with him" [p. 264].
Correction: Re-frame claim S02F-E003 to preserve the authors' hedged phrasing: Sanday and Headlam state that Paul seems to have used an Old Greek text containing the reading of Codex Vaticanus (ἀγαπήσω), noting that Codex Vaticanus is unlikely to have been altered retrospectively to harmonize with Paul.
2. Clarification of Scribal/Psychological Mechanism for Lexical Variant (Check #1 & #5)
Location: Page 264 (Note on Romans 9:25 / Hosea 2:23).
Previous Extraction: Characterized the substitution of καλέσω for ἐρῶ purely as an intentional literary alteration to emphasize divine calling.
Source Observation: On page 264, the authors explain the specific textual/psychological mechanism: "and for ἐρῶ substitutes καλέσω which naturally crept in from the ἐκάλεσεν of the previous verse, and changes the construction of the clause to suit the new word" [p. 264].
Correction: Update claim S02F-E004 to specify that Sanday and Headlam attribute the substitution of καλέσω for ἐρῶ to contextual assimilation (creeping in from ἐκάλεσεν in v. 24) which then prompted a syntactical adjustment to a double accusative construction.
