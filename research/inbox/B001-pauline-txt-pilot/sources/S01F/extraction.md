- **B001 Passage ID:** `B001-1`
- **Author's Claim:** Paul transposes the word order of ἐπίστευσεν and Ἀβραάμ between Romans 4:3 and Galatians 3:6, exhibiting minor inconsistency in quotation form without altering the fundamental textual meaning.
- **Short Quotation:** "the words ἐπίστευσεν and Ἀβραάμ have been transposed. The variations in the last two examples do not affect the sense of the texts"
- **Printed Page Number:** p. 83
- **Source Locator:** Chapter III (Double Citations), p. 83 (PDF p. 93)
- **NotebookLM Citation:** Prompt 2 / Citation Marker 1

- **Claim Type:** Textual-critical
- **Qualifications / Conditions:** The author explicitly notes that this word-order transposition does not alter the underlying semantic sense.
- **Verification Status:** `VERIFIED` (Visibly verified against thesis p. 83 / PDF p. 93).
