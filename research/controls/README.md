# Control Corpora

Controls help determine whether features in the primary corpus are actually distinctive.

They are not silently counted as primary evidence.

## Controls

- `philo.md` — Hellenistic Jewish Greek-scriptural practice.
- `hebrews.md` — early Christian Greek-scriptural practice outside the primary corpus.
- `textual_pluriformity.md` — Old Greek, Jewish Greek revisions, DSS Hebrew witnesses, and related textual plurality.

The purpose of the controls is comparative calibration: to test whether a feature attributed to Gospel authorship is unusual, typical of Greek-scriptural Jewish composition, typical of early Christian composition, or explainable by broader textual plurality.
