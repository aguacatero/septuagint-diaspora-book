# Textual Pluriformity Control

This control prevents the project from reducing first-century scriptural comparison to a simple Greek-versus-Masoretic binary.

Relevant comparison classes include Old Greek, other Jewish Greek translations or revisions, Dead Sea Scroll Hebrew witnesses, MT-type Hebrew traditions, and evidence for differing Hebrew Vorlagen.

A New Testament reading that differs from reconstructed Old Greek must not automatically be classified as direct Hebrew consultation. Another Greek textual form, a Hebraizing revision, authorial adaptation, oral/liturgical transmission, or an unknown intermediary may provide a better explanation.

The purpose of this control is to identify which textual explanation is best supported, not to force every reading into one predetermined tradition.
