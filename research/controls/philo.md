# Philo Control

Philo is used as a **Hellenistic Jewish Greek-scriptural control**, not as evidence that any Gospel author copied Philo or shared his precise linguistic competence.

Questions for comparison include:

- What does scriptural reasoning look like in a clearly Greek-writing Jewish author?
- How are Greek scriptural word choices exploited exegetically?
- Which features attributed to Gospel authors may instead be normal features of Hellenistic Jewish Greek-scriptural composition?

Claims about Philo's source text or language competence require their own primary-source and scholarly support before use.
