# Research Backlog

This backlog is ordered by dependency. Do not skip ahead merely because later topics are more interesting.

## Phase 0 — Workflow validation

### B001 — Pauline TXT pilot

Status: **CURRENT**

Twelve Pauline quotations used to validate source acquisition, passage reports, candidate registry entries, adversarial review, and reviewer promotion.

## Phase 1 — Complete TXT corpus

### B002 — Undisputed Paul complete quotation census

Expand from the B001 pilot to every explicit or strongly identifiable scriptural quotation in the seven undisputed Pauline letters.

Deliverable: complete Pauline TXT candidate census, with allusions kept separate.

### B003 — Mark complete quotation census

Complete Mark TXT census under the same rules used for Paul.

Include known counterexamples and mixed/indeterminate cases without filtering them out.

### B004 — Matthew complete quotation census

Complete Matthew TXT census.

Treat material inherited from Mark separately from Matthew's redactional/scriptural choices where the evidence permits.

### B005 — Luke–Acts complete quotation census

Complete Luke and Acts TXT census while preserving the distinction between authorial practice and independent historical attestation.

### B006 — John complete quotation census

Complete John TXT census with special attention to passages whose wording may favor Hebrew-like or revised Greek forms.

### B007 — TXT synthesis and author-by-author comparison

Only after B002–B006 are reviewed:

- compare classifications by author;
- identify Greek-scriptural dominance, mixed practice, Hebrew-direct candidates, authorial adaptation, and indeterminate cases;
- quantify only categories justified by the registry;
- do not convert confidence labels into pseudo-probabilities.

## Phase 2 — Narrative generation (NAR)

### B101 — Select strongest NAR candidates

Use reviewed TXT findings to nominate passages/stories for causal analysis.

### B102+ — Gospel narrative-generation batches

Apply the causal ladder:

- scriptural coloring;
- scriptural shaping;
- co-construction;
- probable narrative generation.

Always compare competing causal models.

## Phase 3 — Hellenistic / Greco-Roman comparison (CUL)

### B201 — Comparative-method source pack

Acquire specialist methodology and define comparanda before testing individual Gospel stories.

### B202+ — Controlled cultural comparisons

Apply chronology, accessibility, specificity, density, structure, Jewish-scriptural alternatives, genre controls, differences, and transmission tests.

Weak parallels are allowed to fail.

## Phase 4 — Historical residue (HIS)

### B301 — Historical source inventory

Build the evidence inventory for Paul, Gospel traditions after dependence correction, Josephus, Tacitus, and other relevant ancient witnesses.

### B302+ — Claim-by-claim historical audit

Begin from zero assumed biography and audit propositions independently.

Possible conclusions remain open under the Project Constitution.

## Book-writing gate

Do not begin final argumentative chapter drafting until the relevant research phase is reviewed and stable enough to support prose.

Drafting may occur for methodological sections earlier, but evidentiary conclusions must remain downstream of the registry.
